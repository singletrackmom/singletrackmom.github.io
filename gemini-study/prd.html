Gemini for Students Under 18

Usability study · early stage, in development

Overview PRD Assignments Results References

Product requirements for the study: what it measures, how a result is decided, and what the council receives.

Early stage, in development. Testing began on 3 October 2026. The under-18 account has been run on the eleven high-risk content courses. The 18 and older runs are in progress, and the fourteen AI course assignments have not been run. The results shown are drafts from one run per step and still need a faculty member in each discipline to confirm them.

1. Summary

The Faculty Executive Council has paused the student rollout of Gemini because a meaningful share of Maricopa students are under 18, and Google gives those students a restricted version. The district is working to get students access, and the council’s sign-off is one of the steps. The council asked for this evidence at its last meeting.

2. Goal

The study measures one thing: can a student using the under-18 account meet the competency of an assignment that uses Gemini? Where the answer is no, the study says where the student is stopped and what the instructor would change so the student can still meet it.

What the study will report
OutcomeWhat the council will see
Competency met, yes or noFor every assignment, whether the course competency can be met in each account, shown side by side.
Where a student is blockedThe exact step where the under-18 account is stopped, and the message Gemini gives.
Under 18: do this insteadA short clause the instructor can add to the assignment so a student under 18 still meets the competency.
What the instructor adjustsWhat changes in the assignment, or whether an alternate assignment is needed.
Final tallyA count of any competencies that could not be met, and which ones.

3. What is measured, and what is not

What the study measures and what it leaves out
MeasuredNot measured
Whether the competency of each assignment can be met in the under-18 account.How many tools are restricted. Tools are noted only when a missing tool keeps a student from meeting a competency.
The step where a student is stopped, and Gemini’s exact message.Which Gemini model writes better. Answer quality is noted only when it changes whether the competency is met.
What the instructor changes so the student can still meet the competency.Student learning. No students take part.

4. How a result is decided

Definitions used to decide each result
TermDefinition
YesEvery step that produces evidence for the competency completed in that account.
Yes, with an adjustmentOne or more steps were blocked, and the competency can still be met if the instructor adds the “Under 18: do this instead” clause.
Not with Gemini as writtenThe steps that carry the competency were blocked. The student needs a route that does not depend on Gemini, or an alternate assignment.
BlockedGemini refuses in words, or the step fails on every attempt, up to five.
Not a blockA general error that clears when the prompt is sent again. These happened in both accounts.
Missing toolA tool that is not in the account’s menu. The same prompt is then sent in a standard chat. If that works, the competency is not affected.
Under-18 restrictionA block that happens in the under-18 account and does not happen at the same step in the 18 and older account.

5. Scope

Courses and accounts in scope
AI-specific coursesAll 14 courses the district lists as AI courses. These require students to use AI tools.
High-risk content courses11 courses whose ordinary content may meet a youth content filter: nursing, emergency medicine, addiction pharmacology, psychological disorders, human sexuality, criminal investigation, victimology, life drawing, art history, Holocaust studies, and ethical hacking.
ControlCollege Algebra, where no content block is expected.
Under-18 accountA test account set up by Google and designated under 18. It sits in Google’s demonstration domain.
18 and older accountA Maricopa staff account, used with the permission of the Office of Information Technology. The study assumes a staff account and an 18 and older student account receive the same Gemini.

7. Controls and limits

Controls and known limits
Same conditionsBoth accounts are run in the same browser, on the same day, with the same prompts and the same retry rule.
Two different domainsThe under-18 account is in Google’s demonstration domain and the 18 and older account is in Maricopa’s. A difference could come from the age setting or from how each domain is set up.
One run per stepGemini’s answers vary. One run is a snapshot. Blocks were inconsistent even within one account.
Role-playsStakeholder role-plays were run in a standard chat with the role instructions pasted in. Gems themselves have not been tested.
Draft judgmentsThe assignments, the results and the fixes were drafted with AI assistance and are not final until faculty confirm them.

8. Ethics and governance

Safeguards
No minors take partAdults use the under-18 account. The under-18 condition comes from the account designation.
Fictional adults onlyEvery case, patient, client and victim in the assignments is a fictional adult, and the content stays at textbook level.
Google set up the test accountThe report says so plainly, so the findings are not read as Google’s own evaluation.
Not publicThese pages are not indexed and are not linked from the site.

9. Open questions

  • Whether Google can supply an 18 and older test account in the same demonstration domain, for a like-for-like comparison.
  • The age label and admin settings Google applied to the under-18 test account.
  • Whether Maricopa’s own setup for students under 18 will match the test account.
  • The date the council needs the results.
