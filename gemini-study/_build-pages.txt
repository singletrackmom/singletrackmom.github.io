def parse(fn,batch):
    t=open(SRC+fn).read(); out=[]
    for p in re.split(r'\n(?=### )',t)[1:]:
        m=re.match(r'### (\S+) (.*)',p)
        out.append(dict(batch=batch,code=m.group(1),name=m.group(2).replace(' (control)','').strip(),
            comps=re.findall(r'\*\*Competency (\d+):\*\* “(.*?)”',p),
            title=re.search(r'\*\*The assignment:\*\* (.*?)\. ',p).group(1),

    return out

SOLUTION='''  <p>The blocks found so far follow patterns, and each one has a workable alternate. So the fix does not have to be found one assignment at a time. The proposal is an under-18 check that an instructor runs on any assignment that uses Gemini. It is proposed, not built, and not yet tested.</p>
  <div class="demog">
   <p class="cap">Proposed under-18 check for an assignment</p>
   <ol class="flowsteps">
    <li><strong>Compare the assignment to the patterns this study found.</strong> For example: a role-play that involves violence or thoughts of death, analysis of one person’s drug use, evaluating a security scan, a clinical guidelines report on sexual health, or a step that edits an image or generates music or video.</li>
    <li><strong>Draft the alternate.</strong> A prompt, or a check inside the Dial Your Course tool, writes an “Under 18: do this instead” clause for each step that matches a pattern, keeping the same competency.</li>
    <li><strong>Verify in an under-18 test account.</strong> Run the flagged steps once. This is the only reliable test.</li>
    <li><strong>Add the clause to the assignment.</strong> Every student gets the same assignment, and the clause tells a student under 18 what to do at that step.</li>
   </ol>
   <p class="foot">Why the third step is needed: Gemini cannot reliably predict its own under-18 filter. In this study the same account answered one prompt and refused the next on the same subject. A prompt can draft a good alternate. It cannot promise the original will be blocked or that the alternate will pass.</p>
