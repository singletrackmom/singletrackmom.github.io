Gemini for Students Under 18

Usability study · early stage, in development

Overview Assignments Results PRD References

Can a student under 18 meet the competencies of a course assignment that uses Google Gemini? A study for the Maricopa Faculty Executive Council, by the Student Support and Success domain of the AI Resource Center.

Early stage, in development. Testing began on 3 October 2026. The under-18 account has been run on the eleven high-risk content courses. The 18 and older runs are in progress, and the fourteen AI course assignments have not been run. The results shown are drafts from one run per step and still need a faculty member in each discipline to confirm them.

Goal

The study measures one thing: can a student using the under-18 account meet the competency of an assignment that uses Gemini? Where the answer is no, the study says where the student is stopped and what the instructor would change so the student can still meet it.

If students under 18 can meet the competencies even with Gemini’s restrictions, the council has what it needs to sign off. The district is working to get students access to Gemini, the council’s sign-off is one of the steps, and the council asked for this evidence at its last meeting.

What the study will report
OutcomeWhat the council will see
Competency met, yes or noFor every assignment, whether the course competency can be met in each account, shown side by side.
Where a student is blockedThe exact step where the under-18 account is stopped, and the message Gemini gives.
Under 18: do this insteadA short clause the instructor can add to the assignment so a student under 18 still meets the competency.
What the instructor adjustsWhat changes in the assignment, or whether an alternate assignment is needed.
Final tallyA count of any competencies that could not be met, and which ones.

Audience

The Faculty Executive Council first, then district administration and the AI Resource Center steering committee. Faculty use the results to see whether their own assignments need an “Under 18: do this instead” clause.

Process

How the study runs

  1. Start from the competency. Each assignment is written from one official Maricopa course competency, as a simulation, case, portfolio or project.
  2. Run it in both accounts. The same prompts, typed the same way, in the under-18 account and the 18 and older account.
  3. Decide: was the competency met? Yes as written, yes with an adjustment, or not with Gemini as written.
  4. Where it was not met, write the fix. The step that stopped the student, and an “Under 18: do this instead” clause for the instructor.
  5. Faculty confirm. A faculty member in the discipline checks the result and the fix before anything is used with students.
