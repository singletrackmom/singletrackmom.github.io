Examples of What Gemini Blocks for Students Under 18

Usability Studies · Case study

Overview Assignments Results PRD References

Can a student under 18 complete a course assignment that uses Google Gemini? A study by the Student Support and Success domain of the Maricopa AI Resource Center.

In committee review. The district approved Gemini in other areas. Access for students under 18 was the open question, and this study was commissioned to answer it. The report is with the Student Support and Success domain for feedback before it goes to the Faculty Executive Council. Testing ran from 3 to 4 October 2026. Both accounts were run on all 27 example assignments: 14 AI courses, 11 high-risk content courses, and 2 courses added for Deep Research and music generation. The assignments are examples built for demonstration. The results come from one run per step.

Goal

The study asks one question: can a student under 18 complete an assignment that uses Google Gemini, as written? Where the student is stopped, the study records what was blocked and what the student does instead. The instructor teaches to the course competency either way, so each alternate step is written to keep it.

A meaningful share of Maricopa students are under 18, and Google gives those students a restricted version of Gemini. The study shows what that restriction changes in real coursework, so instructors know where to add an alternate step.

Audience

Maricopa faculty and faculty leadership, district administration, the AI Resource Center, and Google. Faculty use the results to see whether their own assignments need an “Under 18: do this instead” clause.

Process

How the study runs

  1. Start from the competency. Claude Opus 5.5 agents drafted each example assignment from one official Maricopa course competency, as a simulation, case, portfolio or project. The Assignments tab has every one.
  2. Run it in both accounts. Identical prompts, typed the same way, in an under-18 test account and an 18 and older account.
  3. Decide the outcome. Completed as written, completed with an alternate step, or not completable in Gemini. A block counts only when the 18 and older account completes the same step.
  4. Write the alternate step. Where the student is stopped, and an “Under 18: do this instead” clause for the instructor. The Results tab has the detail.

Technology

  • Google Gemini, in two test accounts: one under 18, one 18 and older.
  • Claude Opus 5.5 agents, to draft the example assignments from official course competencies.
  • One browser, the same prompts and the same retry rule for both accounts.
  • Results logged step by step and published as static pages. No student took part and no student data was used.

Outcomes

A student under 18 can complete 26 of 27 assignments that use Gemini: 18 as written and 8 with an alternate step added by the instructor. One needs another route.

A block counts as an under-18 restriction only if the 18 and older account completes the same step. Every block listed on the Results page was confirmed that way.

Two kinds of blocks turned up: tools the under-18 account is not offered, and content it refuses. Every gap has a tested or proposed “Under 18: do this instead” step. The tools, the blocks and every assignment are on the Results tab, and the proposed fix is in the PRD.
