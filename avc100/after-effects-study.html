After Effects study guide

Learning design

← Intro to Digital Arts redesign case study

A focused, scoped learning path for the exact After Effects skills I need, nothing more. A few hours a week, applied to real work.

This is a working study aid, not a full course. I am a designer who knows Illustrator and Photoshop cold, but After Effects is new ground. I do not need to master everything. I need two things: enough to teach the AVC 100 project where students animate a branded social-media piece from a template, and enough to build a sixty to ninety second motion-graphics hero animation for my portfolio and Mines demo (draw-on line art, a 2.5D parallax feel).

Everything below is scoped to those two goals. The plan is applied, so I learn each skill by building the AVC 100 project alongside my students. If something is not on this page, I can skip it for now.

What I actually need

Nine skills cover both goals. That is the whole list.

  1. The workspace. Panels, timeline, and the terminology so nothing feels foreign.
  2. Compositions and layers. Set up a comp at the right size and frame rate, stack layers.
  3. Importing PSD and AI files. Bring in layered Photoshop and Illustrator art as a comp I can animate.
  4. Keyframes and interpolation. The core of animation: set keyframes, then use Easy Ease and the Graph Editor so motion is not robotic.
  5. Shape layers and Trim Paths. The draw-on line-art effect for the hero animation.
  6. Text animation. Animate type on and off, which the branded piece needs.
  7. 3D layers, cameras, and parallax. Enough 3D to fake depth and get the 2.5D parallax look.
  8. Motion graphics templates (.mogrt). How a template works and how to replace media inside it, since AVC 100 students work from one.
  9. Rendering and export. Get a clean MP4 out of the Render Queue.

The learning path

Built to run a few hours a week, in order, each step tied to the AVC 100 project so the learning is applied.

Week 1, get oriented

Watch one full overview so the interface stops being scary, then read the official workflow and terminology page. Skills 1 and 2.

  • Watch: Ben Marriott, I’ll Teach You After Effects in 60 Minutes. Best single overview, timestamped chapters, free project file.
  • Read: Get to know After Effects for workflow and terminology, and the Get started hub.

Week 2, build the comp and bring in art

Start the AVC 100 branded piece. Make a comp, then import layered Illustrator and Photoshop art. Skills 2 and 3.

  • Read: Composition basics and Importing still images as a layered comp.
  • Watch: Convert Illustrator layers to shape layers and Prepare vector art for animation.

Week 3, make it move

The heart of it. Learn keyframes, then fix the timing with Easy Ease and the Graph Editor. Skill 4.

  • Read: Animation basics Setting keyframes Keyframe interpolation (Easy Ease and the Graph Editor), and Speed between keyframes.

Week 4, text and templates

Animate the branded text, then learn how the AVC 100 template works so I can teach students to drop their content into it. Skills 6 and 8.

  • Read: Animating text.
  • Read: Motion graphics templates (.mogrt) and Replace media in a .mogrt.

Week 5, export and finish the AVC 100 piece

Get a clean file out. Skill 9. At this point the branded social-media piece is done and I have taught the workflow I need for students.

  • Read: Basics of rendering and exporting and Export H.264 / MP4 from the Render Queue.

Weeks 6 to 8, the portfolio hero animation

Now the harder, more rewarding piece: the sixty to ninety second hero animation. Learn the draw-on line art, then fake depth with 3D layers and a camera. Skills 5 and 7.

  • Draw-on line art: Shape layers overview Shape attributes and path operations (Trim Paths), plus Brooker Films, Animate Lines with Trim Paths.
  • 2.5D parallax: 3D layers and Cameras, lights, and points of interest.
