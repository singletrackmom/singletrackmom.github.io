# AVC 100 · Grade Structure, Fall

**Goal:** Weight the course so the work that takes the longest is worth the most, and make the gradebook enforce what the syllabus promises.

**Audience:** Me, and the faculty who copy this course.

**Process:** Two assignment groups, weighted. Projects 55%, Exercises and Reflection 45%. Points inside each group reflect effort, not arithmetic.

---

## The weighting

| Assignment group | Weight |
|---|---|
| **Projects** | **55%** |
| **Exercises and Reflection** | **45%** |

> ### ⚠️ Turn assignment-group weighting ON
>
> **The current course has weighted groups (50/50) and `apply_assignment_group_weights` is switched OFF.** Canvas is ignoring the weights entirely and grading on raw points. The syllabus says one thing; the gradebook does another; nothing warns you, and a student finds the gap while calculating what they need on a final.
>
> **Canvas: Assignments → three-dot menu, top right → Assignment Group Weight → check "Weight final grade based on assignment groups."**
>
> **This is the single most consequential fix in the course**, and it takes ten seconds.

**Once weighting is on, the raw point totals do not have to add up to anything.** Canvas normalizes within each group. A project step is 15 because it takes longer than an exercise, not because 15 makes a total come out right.

## Student Success · now including Digital Media Programs

**Digital Media Programs at GCC moves into the Student Success module**, and it belongs there. It is not program recruitment sitting awkwardly beside the coursework — **it is the answer to "why am I in this course, and what can I do after it."** That is the same persistence question the rest of the module asks.

The six items:

1. Online Learning Tips
2. Where to get help *(absorbing the orphaned "Getting Help with Coursework or Technology")*
3. GCC Cares
4. Advising
5. Reputable Tutorial / Learning Sources
6. **Digital Media Programs at GCC**

**All six in the module. All 3 points. All checkmark-graded.**

**Fix the orphan:** "Getting Help with Coursework or Technology" is in **no module**, is worth **5** while its siblings are worth 3, and covers the same ground as "Where to get help." Merge its content in. Nothing is lost.

### Declare this group

These serve an **institutional** outcome — persistence — not a course competency. **That is legitimate, and it should be stated rather than inferred.** The persistence literature (Tinto's model of academic and social integration; Astin's involvement theory) is consistent that optional support resources go unused by the students who most need them. **The credit is the mechanism that makes the intervention reach the student.**

Group them, name the group **Student Success**, and put one sentence in the module overview saying what it is for. **A declared exception reads as a design decision. An undeclared one reads as an accident, and a reviewer cannot tell the difference from the outside.**

## The reflections · what they actually are

**I misread these initially and want the record straight.** They are not scrapbooks and they are not career filler.

### Industry Inspiration × 3 — reflective practice

Every one asks the student to compare a professional's creative process to **their own**:

| | Prompt |
|---|---|
| **Graphic Design** (Paula Scher) | *"Can you identify with her total creative immersion experience?"* |
| **Photography** (Jason Lee) | *"Do you have any other interests in your life that cross over to your creative side, like Jason Lee describes his life as a skateboarder crossing over into photography?"* |
| **Animation** (Rebecca Sugar) | *"Is your process for digital arts similar to this animator, or do you use a different process?"* |

**That is metacognition — reflective practice in Schön's sense.** A beginner watching an expert's process and holding it against their own is how they start to believe they *have* a process.

**Do not turn these into formal analysis of the artifact.** They are not about the poster. They are about the person making it.

### The one fix they need: an anchor

*"Write 100 words about something that inspired you"* is completable by a model in ten seconds. **But a model does not know the student's process.**

**Add one clause to each:**

> *"...**Point to a specific moment in the project you just finished** — a thumbnail you abandoned, a revision you made after critique, a decision you reversed — and say whether your process looked like theirs or didn't."*

Same reflection. Now anchored to an artifact **only they have**. A model cannot cite the twentieth thumbnail they drew.

### Course Reflection — persistence and advising

It asks which tool they enjoyed, which was hardest, whether their understanding of Digital Media changed, **which program they are pursuing**, and it walks them to an advisor with the Field of Interest named.

**That is persistence work, not a summative essay.** It belongs with Student Success in intent, even if it lives in the Reflection line.

**Same anchor fix, plus the program question you already asked for:**

> *"Which of the Digital Media programs discussed this term are you considering, and what in your own work this semester pointed you there?"*

## The nine project rubrics, all at 15

**Bold criteria are new.**

### Step 1 · Sketch — 15

| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Research: 10+ reference images | 2 |
| Thumbnail sketches — 20 poster / 10 postcard / 4–6 storyboard frames | 3 |
| **Sketches captured for critique** — photographed or scanned square to the page, evenly lit, in focus, linework readable at full size in Miro. *If a classmate cannot see it, they cannot critique it.* | **2** |
| Typeface selected and justified *(poster)* / carried forward *(postcard, animation)* | 1 |
| Color palette selected and justified *(poster)* / carried forward | 2 |
| Posted to Discord before the deadline | 1 |
| Critique given to at least 4 classmates, added to the Miro board | 2 |
| Final Miro board submitted with all elements including feedback | 2 |

### Step 2 · Build — 15

| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Core production — vector art *(poster)* / compositing *(postcard)* / keyframing *(animation)* | 4 |
| Palette and typography applied, carried from the prior project | 2 |
| Correct document size and setup | 2 |
| **Focal hierarchy clear** — one element leads the eye | **2** |
| Posted to Discord before the deadline | 1 |
| Critique given to at least 4 classmates, added to the Miro board | 2 |
| Final Miro board submitted with the deliverable and feedback | 2 |

**Postcard Step 2 keeps its load-bearing criterion:** each element on its own **editable layer**. The After Effects project imports that PSD as a Composition. **A flattened file breaks the next project.**

### Step 3 · Refine — 15

| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Revised in response to at least two specific peer critiques | 4 |
| **Articulates what changed and why, naming the design principle involved** | **2** |
| Work finalized and polished — composition, readability | 3 |
| **File prepared for output** — correct resolution, color mode, and size. *The PDF would print. The MP4 would play. The JPG is at delivery size.* | **2** |
| Posted to Discord before the deadline | 1 |
| Critique given to at least 4 classmates, added to the Miro board | 2 |
| Final Miro board submitted with the final deliverable and feedback | 1 |

## The vocabulary self-checks · restored and rebuilt

**5 points each, unlimited retakes, formative.** A study aid a student can fail, retake, and learn from.

**Both are corrupted and cannot be copied forward.**

**The bug:** every answer choice in the matching question shares the same `ident` — all 16 response labels are `ident="1"` in one quiz and `ident="2"` in the other. Canvas cannot tell the four answers apart; all four correct-answer mappings point at the same choice. **The question is unanswerable.** It is a QTI *export* defect, so **it recurs on every course copy** — including the copies going to other faculty.

**Second bug:** "Imaging and Animation Vocabulary Check" contains the *design* terms. Its matching question is a duplicate of the other quiz's.

**The fix, smaller than it looks:** the multiple-choice questions exported **cleanly**. Only the matching questions are broken.

> **Rebuild both as multiple choice only. Delete the matching questions.**

No matching question, no corruption. The six MC questions in the imaging quiz are already correct (resolution, PSD, keyframe, selection, compositing, scale).

**The matching content moves into the critique, where it does real work:**

> *"In your classmate's poster, find contrast, hierarchy, and alignment. Name the element carrying each one, and describe the effect on your eye."*

Same vocabulary. Ungoogleable. And it feeds the critique instead of sitting beside it.
