# AVC 100, all video scripts (Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects)

Read-along scripts for your screen-recordings. **Say** lines are narration (paraphrase freely), **Do** lines are what you click. Exercises and Plan steps are short (about **3 to 5 min**); Build steps are longer (about **8 to 10 min**).

**Recording tips:** open your files before you record, mistakes you fix on camera are good teaching, and zoom the interface up so tools read on a phone.

> Note: the Photoshop and After Effects scripts match the lessons we built. The **Illustrator** scripts are my best reconstruction from your poster assignment (18 x 24 travel poster, vector, Adobe Color palette, limited type, traced reference images). Tweak any tool I guessed wrong.

# ILLUSTRATOR, Graphic Design Poster

## Color Palette Exercise (short, ~3 min)

- **Intro (15s) Say:** "Quick one. Before we design, every project needs a color palette. We will build one in Adobe Color."
- **Build it (1.5 min) Say:** "I go to color dot adobe dot com. I pick a color harmony on the left, like Analogous or Complementary, then drag the dots on the color wheel until I have five colors I like for my topic. A desert poster might be warm oranges and tans." **Do:** Open Adobe Color, choose a harmony, drag the wheel to set 5 colors.
- **Save it (45s) Say:** "I save it to my library, or I just write down the five hex codes. In Illustrator I can add these as swatches so they are one click away." **Do:** Save the theme / copy the hex values.
- **Wrap (15s) Say:** "Five colors that fit your topic. Put your palette on your Miro board. That is it."

## Lesson and Exercise: Illustrator, Pen and Shape practice (short, ~4 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "The main Illustrator skill is making clean vector shapes. We will practice with the Pen tool and the shape tools, no design pressure."
- **Shapes (1 min) Say:** "First the easy way. The Rectangle, Ellipse, and Polygon tools make instant shapes. Click and drag, hold Shift for a perfect circle or square." **Do:** Draw a few shapes with the shape tools.
- **Pen tool (1.5 min) Say:** "Now the Pen tool, the big one. Each click drops an anchor point and straight lines connect them, so I can trace any straight-edged shape. To curve a line, I click and drag, and handles appear that bend the path. Trace the practice shapes provided, straight ones first, then the curvy ones." **Do:** Click anchor points to trace a straight shape, then click-drag to make curves.
- **Wrap (30s) Say:** "Trace the practice shapes, fill them with color, and turn them in. Once the Pen tool feels okay, you are ready to build your poster."

## Poster, Step 1: Plan on Miro (short, ~4 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "Before Illustrator, we plan the poster on a Miro board."
- **Research (1 min) Say:** "I search my location and gather at least 10 reference images onto my board, the iconic stuff: for Zion, the cliffs, bighorn sheep, Angel's Landing. These are for inspiration and to trace later." **Do:** Drag reference images onto a Miro board.
- **Thumbnails (1 min) Say:** "On paper I sketch 20 quick thumbnails of poster layouts, photo them onto the board." **Do:** Show thumbnail sketches, add to board.
- **Type and color (45s) Say:** "I add the one typeface I will use for my title and the color palette I built." **Do:** Add typeface and palette.
- **Submit (1 min) Say:** "Post your board to Discord at least two days before the deadline, comment on classmates' boards, screenshot the comments you get, paste them onto your board, and submit the board to Canvas. Same workflow we use for everything."

## Poster, Step 2: Develop the poster (long, ~9 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "Using the feedback from Step 1, we start building the 18 by 24 travel poster. Keep it simple, just a few elements you can draw as vector art."
- **Artboard (40s) Do:** File, New, 18 x 24 inches, Create.
- **Place reference (1 min) Say:** "File, Place, one of my research images. I lower its opacity so I can see through it, and I lock that layer so it does not move while I trace." **Do:** Place a reference, lower opacity, lock the layer.
- **Trace or draw (3 min) Say:** "On a new layer I trace or draw my iconic elements as simplified vector shapes with the Pen tool and the shape tools. A few bold shapes, like a mountain ridge or a cactus, not every detail. Travel posters are simplified." **Do:** New layer, trace or draw simplified shapes.
- **Apply the palette (2 min) Do:** Fill shapes with flat palette colors, background first, then shapes on top.
- **Headline (1 min) Say:** "I add a simple headline with the Type tool, my location name in my chosen typeface and a palette color." **Do:** Type tool, set the headline.
- **Save and submit (1 min) Say:** "File, Save As, PDF. Then put it on a Miro board, post to Discord a few days early, give feedback to at least four classmates, screenshot the feedback you gave along with your post, and submit the board with your PDF." **Do:** Save PDF.

## Poster, Step 3: Finalize and export (medium, ~5 min)

- **Intro (15s) Say:** "Last step. We use the Step 2 feedback to finalize and polish."
- **Refine (2 min) Say:** "I apply the feedback: clean up the shapes, fix spacing and color, adjust the type. Then I align everything and make sure it reads from across the room. Less is more." **Do:** Refine shapes, spacing, color, and type; align.
- **Export (1 min) Say:** "File, Save As, PDF. That is my final." **Do:** Save the final PDF.
- **Submit (1 min) Say:** "Put the final poster on a Miro board, post to Discord a few days early, give feedback to at least four classmates, screenshot the feedback you gave along with your post, and submit the board with your final PDF."

# PHOTOSHOP, Photographic Postcard

## Cut It Out exercise (short, ~4 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "Quick video. I will show the selection tools by making a sticker sheet. The skill is cutting a subject out of its background."
- **Page (30s) Do:** File, New, 1500 x 1500, 300 ppi. Paint Bucket, fill with a color.
- **Easy cut-out (1 min) Say:** "Open the ice cream cone. Click Select Subject up top, those marching ants are my selection. Edit, Copy. Back to my sticker page, Edit, Paste. Command or Control T, hold Shift, scale, then move it." **Do:** Select Subject, copy, paste, scale, place.
- **Fuzzy cut-out (1.5 min) Say:** "Now the dog. Select Subject, but the fur is rough, so I click Select and Mask, use the Refine Edge brush along the fur, OK. Copy and paste it on." **Do:** Select Subject, Select and Mask, Refine Edge brush, copy, paste.
- **Wrap (30s) Say:** "Cut out three things, arrange like stickers, export a JPG, put it on Miro, post to Discord. Done."

## Postcard, Step 1: Plan on Miro (short, ~4 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "Before Photoshop, we plan the postcard on Miro. Same destination, typeface, and palette as your poster."
- **Research (1 min) Do:** Drag a few destination photos and a few animal photos (real backgrounds) onto a Miro board.
- **Thumbnails (1 min) Say:** "Ten quick thumbnails, where the animal goes and where the type goes, leaving open space for type. Photo them onto the board." **Do:** Show thumbnails, add to board.
- **Type and color (30s) Do:** Add the poster's typeface and palette.
- **Submit (1 min) Say:** "Post to Discord two days early, critique classmates, paste the comments you get onto your board, submit the board."

## Postcard, Step 2: Build (long, ~8 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "We build the postcard you planned, using the selection skills from the exercise."
- **Page (40s) Do:** File, New, 1200 x 1800 (or 1800 x 1200), 300 ppi.
- **Background (1 min) Say:** "File, Place Embedded, my destination photo, then Shift-drag a corner to fill the whole page, Enter." **Do:** Place Embedded, resize to fill, Enter.
- **Select the animal (2 min) Do:** Open the animal photo, Select Subject, refine fuzzy edges in Select and Mask.
- **Copy and paste (30s) Do:** Copy, switch to postcard, Paste. Note the background did not change.
- **Place (1 min) Do:** Ctrl/Cmd + T, Shift to scale, Move tool to position per the thumbnail.
- **Type (1 min) Do:** Type tool, set the destination name in the poster's typeface and a palette color.
- **Save and export (45s) Do:** Save As PSD (keep layers), then Export As JPG.
- **Submit (40s) Say:** "Postcard on a Miro board, post to Discord two days early for critique, paste the comments back, submit the board. Keep your PSD, the animal and type come back in After Effects."

# AFTER EFFECTS, Social Media Animation

## Make It Move exercise (short, ~4 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "Quick one. The skill is making a layer move with keyframes. We will make an image fly into the frame."
- **Comp (30s) Do:** Composition, New Composition, vertical 1080 x 1920, a few seconds.
- **Add image (30s) Do:** Drag in an image (even one of your sticker cut-outs) onto the timeline.
- **Keyframes (1.5 min) Say:** "I select the layer and press P for Position. With the playhead at the start I click the stopwatch, then drag the image off the edge of the frame, that is keyframe one. I move the playhead forward about a second and drag the image to where it should land, that makes keyframe two. Spacebar to watch it fly in." **Do:** P, stopwatch, drag off-screen, move playhead, drag on-screen.
- **Export and wrap (1 min) Say:** "Press N to trim the end of the work area, then Composition, Add to Media Encoder, H.264, export an MP4. Put it on Miro, post to Discord. If it flies in, you have the skill."

## Animation, Step 1: Storyboard on Miro (short, ~4 min)

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "Before After Effects, we storyboard the motion. A storyboard shows what happens over time, not just where things sit."
- **Frames (1.5 min) Say:** "On paper I sketch about four to six frames: frame one, my destination background alone; frame two, the animal entering, with an arrow showing which side it flies in from; frame three, the type entering from another side; frame four, everything in place, matching my postcard." **Do:** Show storyboard frames with direction arrows.
- **Assets and timing (45s) Say:** "I add my destination, animal, and type from the postcard, and a short note on the order and timing of the moves." **Do:** Add assets and a timing note to the board.
- **Submit (1 min) Say:** "Post to Discord two days early, critique classmates, paste the comments onto your board, submit the board. You are deciding the motion now so the build goes fast."

## Animation, Step 2: Build from the template (long, ~8 min)

> This script uses the **template** you provide. Send me your .aep and I will lock the exact comp names and steps. Below is the plug-and-play flow.

- **Intro (20s) Say:** "Now we build the animation. You are not animating from scratch, you are dropping your own assets into a template, so this goes quickly."
- **Open the template (45s) Say:** "I open the template project and find the main composition in the Project panel. Everything is already animated, my job is to swap in my stuff." **Do:** Open the template, open the main comp.
- **Bring in your assets (2 min) Say:** "I import my postcard pieces, File, Import. Because I saved my postcard as a layered Photoshop file, I import it as a Composition so my background, animal, and type each come in on their own layer, ready to animate." **Do:** File, Import, choose the PSD, import as Composition with layers.
- **Replace and scale (2 min) Say:** "I drop my layers into the template's placeholders, and I scale each to fit by selecting the layer and pressing S, then adjusting." **Do:** Swap assets into the template, press S to scale each.
- **Update the text (1 min) Say:** "I update the template's text layer to my destination name, in my typeface and palette color, so it matches the rest." **Do:** Edit the text layer.
- **Time it (1 min) Say:** "I check the timing so the moves fit my footage, and I press N to set the end of my work area." **Do:** Adjust timing, press N.
- **Export (1 min) Say:** "Composition, Add to Media Encoder, H.264, Match High Bitrate, and export the MP4 to my project folder." **Do:** Add to Media Encoder, H.264, export MP4.
- **Submit and wrap (40s) Say:** "Put your MP4 on a Miro board, post to Discord two days early for critique, paste the comments back, submit the board. That is your finished destination project across all three programs. Nice work."
