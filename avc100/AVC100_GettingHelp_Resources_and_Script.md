# Getting Help with Coursework or Technology

Where to go at GCC when you need help with the tech side of your classes: getting online, getting a computer or laptop, getting tutoring, and getting accommodations. (For GCC Cares, advising, and tips for learning online, see the other videos.)

> Resource details pulled from the CopaMigo student-services tool. Hours and numbers change, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked GCC page before relying on it.

---

## Quick links: where to get help

### IT Service Desk (Helpdesk), for logins and tech

Your first stop for MEID, passwords, Canvas, student email, My.Maricopa, Duo two-factor, campus Wi-Fi, using a computer lab, and borrowing a laptop.

- **Main campus:** High Tech Center 1 (HT1) · **623-845-3555**
- **North campus:** Beshbito Building · **623-888-7124** · hours (Fall/Spring) Mon–Thu 7:30am–8pm, Fri 8am–3pm
- **Email:** ServiceDesk@gccaz.edu
- **Open a ticket online:** [gccaz.edu/technology-help/service-desk](https://www.gccaz.edu/technology-help/service-desk)
- **HT1 building and map:** [High Tech Center 1](https://www.gccaz.edu/locations/main-campus/high-tech-center-1)
- **After hours:** the Maricopa District Student Help Desk is 24/7 for Canvas, MEID, and student email when the GCC desk is closed.

### Borrow a laptop for the semester (take home)

- **IT Student Device Checkout:** [gccaz.edu/StudentDeviceRequest](https://www.gccaz.edu/StudentDeviceRequest)
- Request it ASAP, supply is very limited. If you are in a media program (like Digital Arts or Animation), ask the Service Desk about higher-spec devices.

### Use a computer on campus right now (computer labs)

No checkout needed, just walk in:

- **Computer Commons, High Tech Center 1 (HT1)**, Main
- **Library Media Center**, Main · [building and map](https://www.gccaz.edu/locations/main-campus/library-media-center)
- **Student Union**, Main · [building and map](https://www.gccaz.edu/locations/main-campus/student-union)
- **Beshbito Building**, North
- **Save your work:** campus computers erase files every night. Save to a USB drive, the cloud (OneDrive or Google Drive), or your H: drive.

### Campus Wi-Fi

- Connect to the **Eduroam** network with your MEID and password.
- Trouble connecting? The **IT Service Desk** (above) handles Wi-Fi.

### Wi-Fi hotspot to take home (through the Library, not IT)

- **West Circulation desk, Main campus** · **623-845-3119** · email DL-GC-LMC-HOTSPOTS@gccaz.edu
- Not available at North campus.

### Library Media Center, research help and devices

- **Website:** [lib.gccaz.edu](https://lib.gccaz.edu) · **Hours:** [lib.gccaz.edu/about/hours](https://lib.gccaz.edu/about/hours/)
- **Ask a Librarian (24/7 chat):** [lib.gccaz.edu/students/ask](https://lib.gccaz.edu/students/ask/)
- **Book a research consultation:** [lib.gccaz.edu/students/ask/consultation](https://lib.gccaz.edu/students/ask/consultation/)
- The library also lends **calculators and short-term laptops** for in-library use (4-hour limit, stays in the library) at the circulation desk, Main and North.

### Free tutoring

- **All tutoring:** [gccaz.edu/academics/tutoring](https://www.gccaz.edu/academics/tutoring) · drop-in 30-minute sessions
- **Math:** [gccaz.edu/academics/tutoring/math](https://www.gccaz.edu/academics/tutoring/math) · mathsolution@gccaz.edu
- **North campus:** [gccaz.edu/academics/tutoring/north](https://www.gccaz.edu/academics/tutoring/north)
- **Online 24/7:** [Brainfuse](https://webapp.gccaz.edu/apps/brainfuse), plus tutoring on GCC's Discord
- A **Writing Center** is available for feedback on your writing.

### Disability Resource Services (DRS)

- **Website:** [gccaz.edu/student-life/disability-resource-services](https://www.gccaz.edu/student-life/disability-resource-services)
- **Administration Building, Main campus** · **623-845-3080** · email drsfrontdesk@gccaz.edu · fax 623-845-3273 · [building and map](https://www.gccaz.edu/locations/main-campus/administration-building)
- Apply in DRS Connect (sign in with your MEID), submit your documentation, then meet a DRS specialist to set up accommodations.

## Video script (about 4 minutes)

**Say** lines are your narration; **Do** lines are what to show on screen (a slide or the GCC page).

**Intro (about 20 sec)**
- **Say:** "Hi everyone. This video is your map for getting help with the tech side of this class: getting online, getting on a computer, getting tutoring, and getting accommodations. Bookmark this page, because everything I mention is linked right below the video."

**The Helpdesk (about 1 min)**
- **Say:** "Start here for anything login or tech related. If you cannot get into Canvas, your student email, My.Maricopa, or your Duo two-factor, or your password is acting up, that is the IT Service Desk. On Main campus they are in High Tech Center 1, and the number is 623-845-3555. You can also open a ticket online any time, and if it is the middle of the night, the Maricopa District Help Desk is open 24/7 for Canvas and login problems."
- **Do:** Show the Service Desk page and the HT1 location.

**Computers and laptops (about 1 min)**
- **Say:** "Need a computer? You have two options. To use one right now, walk into any campus lab, the Computer Commons in HT1, the Library, or the Student Union, no checkout needed. Just remember, those computers wipe your files every night, so always save to a USB drive or the cloud. To take a laptop home for the whole semester, request one through IT Student Device Checkout, and do it early because they go fast. If you are in a media program, ask them about the higher-spec machines."
- **Do:** Show the Student Device Checkout page and the lab list.

**Wi-Fi (about 30 sec)**
- **Say:** "For Wi-Fi on campus, connect to the network called Eduroam with your MEID. If you need internet at home, the Library lends Wi-Fi hotspots you can take with you, just stop by the West Circulation desk on Main campus."
- **Do:** Show the hotspot info.

**Tutoring and the Library (about 45 sec)**
- **Say:** "Stuck on the actual coursework? Free tutoring is one of the best deals on campus. There are drop-in 30-minute sessions, math tutoring, a Writing Center, and Brainfuse, which is online and open 24/7. And the Library is more than computers, you can chat with a librarian any time or book a one-on-one research consultation."
- **Do:** Show the tutoring page and the Library Ask-a-Librarian page.

**Disability Resource Services (about 30 sec)**
- **Say:** "Last one, and an important one. If you have a disability, whether visible or not, Disability Resource Services sets up accommodations for your classes. It is private, it is your right, and it helps. Apply through DRS Connect with your MEID, send in your documentation, and meet with a specialist. They are in the Administration Building on Main campus."
- **Do:** Show the DRS page.

**Wrap (about 15 sec)**
- **Say:** "That is your help map. You do not have to figure this out alone, all of these are here for you, and every link is right below. See you in class."
