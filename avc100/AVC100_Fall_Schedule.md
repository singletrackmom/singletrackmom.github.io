# AVC 100 · Fall 2026 Schedule

**Goal:** Fit the 3 × 3 project pattern into the existing eight-week window without moving a single date outside it.

**Audience:** Me, and the faculty who copy this course.

**Process:** Keep the sandbox's Wednesday/Saturday rhythm. Cap every day at two due dates. Give every Sketch step a day of its own. Land the tool lesson the Saturday before the project that needs it.

## Why it is laid out this way

### The tool lesson always lands the Saturday *before* the project opens

| Lesson due | Project opens |
|---|---|
| **Illustrator** · Sat Sep 5 | **Poster** · Wed Sep 9 |
| **Photoshop** · Sat Sep 19 | **Postcard** · Wed Sep 23 |
| **After Effects** · Sat Oct 3 | **Animation** · Wed Oct 7 |

**You learn the tool, then you use it.** Four days between them, every time. This is the same relationship in all three modules, so a student learns the rhythm once.

### Every Sketch day is alone

**Sep 9. Sep 23. Oct 7.** Nothing else is due on those days.

Sketch is the step that requires real time away from the screen: twenty thumbnails on paper, photographed, assembled in Miro. **Pairing it with an exercise would guarantee the exercise gets done and the sketches get rushed** — and the sketches are where the design thinking happens.

### Each project runs Wed → Sat → Wed

| | Sketch | Build | Refine |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Poster** | Wed Sep 9 | Sat Sep 12 | Wed Sep 16 |
| **Postcard** | Wed Sep 23 | Sat Sep 26 | Wed Sep 30 |
| **Animation** | Wed Oct 7 | Sat Oct 10 | Wed Oct 14 |

**Identical shape, three times.** Three days from Sketch to Build, four from Build to Refine — the longer gap sits where the critique has to land and be responded to.

### The light items ride along

Student Success items, Industry Inspiration reflections, and the vocabulary self-checks are all short. **They pair with a heavier item rather than consuming a slot**, and they never pair with a Sketch.

## What is not in the schedule any more

**Nothing was dropped.** Two things merged:

- **"Getting Help with Coursework or Technology"** — merged into *Student Success: Where to get help*. It was in no module and carried a stale due date of **July 13, 2026**, which is a leftover that would have shipped to every faculty member who copied this course.
- **"Digital Media Programs at GCC"** — moved *into* the Student Success module, where it belongs. It answers *"why am I in this course and what can I do after it,"* which is the same persistence question the rest of the module asks, and it sets up the Course Reflection's *"which program are you pursuing?"*

## The workload caveat, stated plainly

**AVC100 is one credit.** The seat-hour budget is **40 total student hours** — 13.5 direct instruction, 26.5 outside work.

The course as scheduled requires roughly **31 hours** of outside work. **That is about 118% of budget, and adding the two Refine steps does not improve it.**

**This is not an argument against the restructure.** The Refine step is the most AI-resistant work in the course and running it in one project out of three was leaving evidence on the table.

**It is an argument that the course is mis-credited.** Three Adobe applications, three critique cycles, revision, and a published portfolio is not a one-credit course, and it never was. **That is a curriculum conversation, and it should be had with this schedule in hand.**
