# Social Media Animation

An animated version of your postcard: a short, vertical animated video, the kind you see as an Instagram or TikTok story or reel. The pieces you already made will move. For example, your animal flies in from one side and your type slides in from the other. You built the art in your postcard, so this project is about giving those pieces motion.

---

## Step 1: Storyboard

### Objective

Plan the motion of your animation before you build it, reusing the same destination, animal, and type from your postcard.

### Assignment

A **storyboard** is a few frames that show what happens over time, start to finish. Thumbnails show where things sit; a storyboard shows how they move. Sketch about **4 to 6 frames:**

1. **The opening:** your destination background alone.
2. **The animal enters:** draw an arrow showing which direction it flies in from.
3. **The type enters:** draw an arrow showing which direction it comes in from.
4. **The final hold:** everything in place, matching your finished postcard.

Then add a short note on the order and timing of the moves, and gather it all on one Miro board (listed in Submission below).

### Submission

Deliverables for Step 1 gathered on one Miro board:

1. Your **storyboard frames** (4 to 6), sketched on paper with direction arrows (take a photo of this to add to your Miro board).
2. Your **destination, animal, and type** from the postcard.
3. A short **note on the order and timing** of the moves.
4. Take a screenshot of your Miro board and submit to Discord 2 days before the deadline to receive feedback from your classmates.
5. Give feedback to at least 4 classmates.
6. Add the feedback you gave to your classmates to your Miro board.
7. Submit the screenshot of your final Miro board with feedback attached here for your grade.

## Step 2: Build

### Objective

Build your animation by giving the pieces of your own postcard some motion. You already made the art, so now you bring your postcard into After Effects and make each piece move with a couple of keyframes.

### Assignment

After Effects has a **Project** panel at the top left, a **Composition** preview in the middle, a **Timeline** along the bottom, and the **menus** across the top. Two keys you will use: **P** for Position (to make things move) and **S** for Scale (to resize).

1. **Import your postcard.** Click the **File** menu, then **Import**, then **File**. Choose your postcard PSD file, set **Import As** to **Composition**, then click **Import**. Your background, animal, and type each come in on their own layer.
2. **Open it.** In the **Project** panel at the top left, double-click your postcard composition to open it in the Timeline. It opens already vertical at your postcard size, which is perfect for a story.
3. **Slide in the animal.** Click the animal layer, then press the **P** key so its Position shows. Move the playhead to the very start, click the **stopwatch** next to Position, and drag the animal just off the edge it flies in from. Move the playhead ahead about 1 second, then drag the animal to its resting spot. Those two markers (keyframes) make it slide in.
4. **Slide in the type.** Do the exact same thing on the type layer, coming in from the other side, matching your storyboard.
5. **Preview and trim.** Press the **spacebar** to watch it play. When it looks right, move the playhead to the moment your animation ends, then press the **N** key to set the end of your work area there so you do not export dead time.
6. **Export your MP4.** Click the **Composition** menu, then **Add to Adobe Media Encoder Queue**. Media Encoder opens with your animation in a list. Choose **H.264** from the Format menu, and set Match High Bitrate. Now look at the **Output File** name (in yellow): click it, choose a folder you will remember (your **Desktop** is easiest), and name the file with your own name. Then click the green **play** button at the top right. When it finishes, your **MP4** is sitting in that folder, ready to submit.

### Submission

1. Your finished animation, exported as an **MP4**.
2. **Post your MP4 to Discord** 2 days before the deadline so classmates can watch it and give feedback. Drag the MP4 file straight into the channel. If Discord says the file is too large, put it on your Miro board or Google Drive and paste the link instead.
3. Give feedback to at least 4 classmates by replying to their posts.
4. Add your MP4 (or its link) and the feedback you received to your Miro board.
5. Submit your **MP4** and a screenshot of your final Miro board (with feedback) here in Canvas for your grade.
