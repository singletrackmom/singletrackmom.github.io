Make the content

The fun part: shoot and edit short videos, take photos, design simple graphics, and plan a content calendar. It is hands-on creative work with your phone and a camera, riding whatever is trending that week. If you like making things and being a little bit on camera, this is your lane.

Grow an account

Tracking what works and making the numbers climb is the whole portfolio, and it is the thing employers actually want to see.

How to get qualified (the real path)

No degree. This job is hired on a portfolio and results, so the whole move is building one real example you can point to.

The on-ramp, right at home: intern on the family photography business’s social. Run the Instagram and Facebook, build a content calendar, make reels, and grow the following, then write it up as a before-and-after case study: “grew the account from X to Y, here is the content I made.” One real example beats a class, and you can start this week.

Stack a free credential on top: HubSpot’s Social Media certification and Google’s Digital Marketing fundamentals are both genuinely free, and Meta has a social media course on Coursera.
