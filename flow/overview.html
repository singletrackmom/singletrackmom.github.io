Find Your Flow

Agentic & personal build

A gentle career-exploration agent that serves one realistic, low-barrier career path a day, each with a real open job showing the true pay and day-to-day.

Open Find Your Flow → PRD →

Goal

Take the overwhelm out of choosing a career by offering one honest option at a time, in small doses. The aim is to move from “I can’t think about this” to one small real step: touring a school, stacking a free certificate, or applying to a single job.

Find Your Flow is a daily career-exploration newsletter for a young person figuring out what to do next. A scheduled agent publishes one short, designed letter a day, each built around a single realistic, low-barrier career. Every edition shows the real job outlook and pay, a concrete way to get qualified, and one real, currently-open job posting so the field is always paired with proof it is hiring.

Audience

A young person exploring careers, someone who freezes at “what do you want to do with your life?” when it arrives as one giant list. The tone stays gentle and encouraging, presenting each path as an option to consider rather than an instruction to follow.

Process

It is a personal build, a daily agent that runs on a schedule. Each morning it picks a fresh career, verifies the growth rate and median pay against the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, finds a real local training path, and confirms one live job on an employer’s own careers page. It then composes the letter into a locked, phone-first template and keeps a running board of jobs to apply to today. The site is static, with no forms, logins, or tracking, and nothing personal is collected.
