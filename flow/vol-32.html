Clinic and practice management · the person who keeps the whole clinic running

What it is

You run the clinic, not the exam room

A practice manager keeps a doctor's office or clinic running smoothly. Most days you are making the schedule work, hiring and training the front desk and medical assistants, sorting out a patient's problem at the counter, keeping supplies stocked, and making sure the clinic follows privacy and safety rules. Most people start one step smaller, leading the front desk, scheduling or patient check-in, and grow into running a whole clinic. Job titles to search: practice manager, clinic operations supervisor, patient access supervisor, front office lead, clinic manager.

Why it might fit you

Your medical assistant year is the first rung

The government says managers usually need experience in an administrative or clinical job in a healthcare setting first. Your medical assistant certificate and job are exactly that, and you would know firsthand what the MAs and the front desk need to have a good day. It is a people job: patients, staff, and doctors all day, and you are up and moving around the clinic. The honest part: there is more computer time than in an MA job, and you are the person people bring problems to. The math is mostly schedules and a budget in a spreadsheet, and AI tools are a real help with reports and staffing plans. You also get to pick the clinic, like adult primary care, orthopedics, rehab, or dermatology.

The money and the outlook

About $124,000, with a bachelor's

The government says medical and health services managers earn a median of $123,860 a year (May 2025). In doctors' offices the median is $105,770. Jobs are projected to grow 24 percent from 2025 to 2035, much faster than average, with about 62,300 openings a year. A bachelor's degree is the typical ask, though some employers hire with an associate's, and experience can sometimes stand in for school. Those medians are for managers; first supervisor jobs pay less, like the one below.

A way in before the degree

Grow from the front desk while you study

The government says many managers begin as medical records specialists, administrative assistants, or other healthcare office staff. That means you can keep working in a clinic, take on more of the front office and scheduling, and study online at the same time. A question worth asking any clinic you work for: do they help pay for school?

One real job, live right now

Rehab Operations Supervisor, Intermountain Health

Wheat Ridge Clinic · $26.74 to $41.28 an hour (posted range) · full time

Intermountain Health runs Lutheran Hospital in Wheat Ridge, a short drive from Golden, plus clinics nearby. This supervisor leads the team that handles registration, scheduling, and referrals at a rehab clinic: hiring, training, coaching, solving problems, and keeping the clinic on its goals. They prefer a bachelor's in a business or health field, three years of leadership experience, and experience with Epic, the scheduling and records system many clinics use. This is a job for a few years down the road, a picture of where the degree plus clinic experience leads. On site, Monday to Friday. Posted in early November, checked live today.

Where to study, close to home

A small certificate, then a bachelor's online

  • Red Rocks · Medical Office Administration Certificate, 9 credits over two semesters, online: Medical Terminology, Medical Office Administration, and a 90-hour office internship. The Red Rocks medical assisting certificate already includes the first two classes, so if that is your certificate, ask an advisor whether you are one internship away.   • Red Rocks · Business associate (AA), about 2 years, in Lakewood, Arvada, or online. Built to transfer into a bachelor's.Start
  • MSU Denver · Health Care Management B.S., on campus or fully online, $326.25 a credit for Colorado residents (2026-27 rate), or $4,987.22 for a full-time 12-credit term with fees. Online majors can get up to $1,000 a semester in scholarships. The page does not list total credits; most bachelor's are about 120.Finish

You do not need a bachelor's to lead a front desk or earn the office certificate. You will want one for most practice manager jobs. Everything here stops at a bachelor's.
