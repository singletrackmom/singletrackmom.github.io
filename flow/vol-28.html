Forensics with a bachelor’s · catching what others miss

What forensic means

Finding the evidence

Forensic just means work that finds evidence someone can use in a case. It is not only the psychologist on TV. It is the crime scene tech, the casino team that spots cheaters, and the person who pulls deleted texts off a phone. Plenty of it starts with a bachelor’s, and one of them you can start this month.

Crime scene and lab

Forensic science technician

You collect evidence at the scene, then test it in the lab: fingerprints, fibers, DNA, drugs. It is one of the fastest-growing jobs in the country, and a bachelor’s is the way in. The honest part: it is a science degree, so expect chemistry and biology.

The one you can start now

Casino surveillance

Every casino in Black Hawk has a surveillance room watching the tables and the money, looking for cheating, theft, and anything off. You can start at 18 with a high school diploma, paid about $22 to $24 an hour. Pair that experience with a criminal justice degree and you are a strong pick for investigator roles later.

Digital forensics

The evidence on the phone

Most cases now have a phone or laptop in them. Digital forensics people recover what was deleted and show who did what, when. Job titles to search: digital forensics technician, cybercrime investigator, eDiscovery specialist. A cybersecurity bachelor’s gets you there.

One real job, live right now

Surveillance Agent, Ameristar Casino Black Hawk

Black Hawk · $22 to $24 an hour · 18+ with a high school diploma · nights and weekends

You watch the gaming floor and money-handling areas on camera, log anything unusual, and report problems. There is a short math test to get in, and you need a Colorado support gaming license, which they pay back after 90 days. Open until filled.
