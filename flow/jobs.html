Optional, no rush

How to get qualified, if you ever want to

None of the jobs above require any of this. It is here so the options sit in one place and you can see what they actually cost in time and money. Nothing on this list has a deadline today.

The smallest step

Red Rocks in Arvada teaches the office side of a clinic: medical terminology, scheduling, patient records, insurance. You can take one course and stop there, or stack it into their Medical Office Administration certificate. Closest campus to the house, and it makes a front desk application stronger right away.

Red Rocks, Medical Office Technology ›

The PTA route you already looked at

Arapahoe Community College runs the accredited PTA associate degree. It is selective admission with one application window a year, so there is nothing to submit right now. Prerequisites can be taken at Red Rocks, which is closer.

Arapahoe CC, PTA associate degree ›

The faster, pricier PTA route

Pima Medical Institute in Denver runs the same kind of PTA associate degree on a shorter schedule with day and evening classes. It costs more. Worth putting side by side with Arapahoe before deciding anything.

Pima Medical Institute, PTA program ›

Later, only if you stay in it

CMAA is the national certification for medical office and front desk work. It needs about a year of medical office experience first, so it is something a front desk job unlocks later, not something to chase now.

NHA, CMAA certification ›
