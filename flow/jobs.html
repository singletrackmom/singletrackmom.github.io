Jobs you can get now

Updated 22 November 2026

After your certificate

Jobs in the Denver metro and California that build on your medical assistant certificate without a doctor's office. Every one was open on the employer's own careers page on 16 November 2026.

You could apply with your MA certificate
New

AdaptHealth, Medical Equipment Technician, Lafayette

Visit patients at home to set up oxygen and other home medical equipment, and teach them and their families how to use it.

$21 to $23/hr · high school diploma · driver's license

About 35 minutes from home. You would be in patients' homes every day, setting up equipment, checking on how they are doing with it, and answering questions. A diploma is the requirement and healthcare experience is preferred, so your certificate and clinic year help. There is driving, lifting, and an occasional on-call shift. The posting does not say whether it is full or part time, so ask.

AdaptHealth, Medical Equipment Technician, Mountain View, California

The same home-visit job in the Bay Area: set up equipment in patients' homes and teach them to use it safely.

$22 to $27/hr · high school diploma · driver's license

A California version of the card above, if a move happens. Same diploma requirement, with healthcare experience preferred. The posting notes that California requires a state license for staff who work on site at a home medical equipment facility, which takes a short state-approved course. The posting does not say whether it is full or part time.

AdaptHealth, Patient Liaison, Culver City, California

Work inside a Los Angeles area hospital, meeting patients before they go home, delivering their equipment, and teaching them how to use it.

$21.63 to $26.92/hr · high school diploma · 1 year of related work

A people job on your feet: patients, nurses, and hospital staff all day, plus going over insurance and payments with patients. It asks for a diploma and one year of healthcare, insurance, or customer service work. One line in the posting says the experience they count must be in medical equipment, diabetes, or incontinence sales, so ask about that before applying. The posting does not say whether it is full or part time.

New

Stryker, OnSite Specialist, Parker

Be a medical device company's equipment person inside a hospital: set up, troubleshoot, and keep devices ready during surgeries.

$23.74 to $32.12/hr posted range, varies by location · high school diploma

About 45 minutes from home. A diploma is required, and an associate's, bachelor's, or certification is preferred, along with operating room experience and anatomy knowledge, which your MA training gives you a start on. Shifts can rotate and include nights, weekends, and on call. A foot in the door at one of the biggest device companies, where sales and clinical jobs come open.

Worth aiming for with a bachelor's
New

Stryker, Associate Sales Representative, Acute Care, Denver

The entry rung of device sales: show hospital equipment to nurses and doctors, train staff, and solve problems.

$65,000 base plus bonus · bachelor's required · about 50% travel

A bachelor's degree is required and no professional experience is needed; a sales or marketing internship is preferred. Field based, about half your time on the road, with a driver's license and lifting up to 50 pounds when you demo equipment. A picture of where a marketing or business degree can lead.

New

Medtronic, Associate Clinical Specialist, Cardiac Rhythm, Mountain View, California

The cardiac device specialist job: support pacemaker and defibrillator checks and procedures in clinics and hospitals.

$70,000 to $79,000 base (U.S. range, some California areas differ) · bachelor's, or diploma plus 4 years of healthcare work

A bachelor's is the simplest way in, but the posting also accepts a high school diploma plus four years of healthcare work, so years as an MA could count. It does not ask for IBHRE certification up front. Expect about 80 percent of your time driving around the territory, weekend and holiday coverage, and wearing heavy lead aprons during procedures. Medtronic expects applications to close around November 23.
