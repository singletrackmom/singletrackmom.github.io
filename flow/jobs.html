MSU Denver, Simulated Participant, standardized patient, Denver

This one is different and kind of perfect for you.

$21/hr · temporary, on-call, variable schedule · no degree, no needles

Metropolitan State University trains people to play a “patient” (or a patient’s loved one, or a member of the care team) so nursing students can practice on someone realistic and safe. It is basically paid acting: you learn a role, stay in character, and give honest feedback. Outgoing, people-first, no medical background and no needles on you, just a high-school diploma or GED, good memory, and good listening. It is on-call with a variable schedule, so you pick up sessions when they fit. It is on the Auraria campus downtown, a little farther than your usual zone, but the gig is occasional and easy, so the drive is worth it for a few hours of fun, well-paid work. Apply on MSU Denver’s own Workday page with a resume.
