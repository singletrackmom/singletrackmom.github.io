Medical Assistant for a med spa · Red Rocks, a closer look

What it takes

Really just six classes

Eight items on paper, but honestly it is six real classes: anatomy and physiology, medical terminology, medical office administration, a hands-on lab, clinical skills, and pharmacology. The other two are a one-credit exam prep and the internship, which is you out working, not sitting in a lecture. Six classes, then you are in the field.

6 real classes18 credits
+ exam prep1 credit
+ internshiphands-on hours

The part that might sell you

You can travel with this

Here is the fun part. Once you have the certificate and about a year of experience, travel medical-assistant jobs open up, the same way travel nurses work. Picture a few months in Hawaii or California, housing help included. California travel MAs average around $2,200 a week, Hawaii around $1,800, some assignments higher. Get certified, work a year, then get paid to live somewhere beautiful.

Hawaii + CAreal assignments
~$1,800-2,200a week

The plan

Certificate first, work sooner

The fastest way in is the Medical Assisting certificate at Red Rocks: those six classes plus the internship, about 28 credits and roughly a year, and you finish ready to sit for the national CMA exam and get hired. It stacks into the degree later if you ever want it.

≈28 creditsabout a year

The honest part

No degree to get hired

For medical-assistant jobs in Colorado, and for the travel agencies too, employers are not asking for a four-year degree. What they want is the national certification, the CMA. The certificate gets you there. The degree is a nice-to-have for later, not a gate to the first job.

Good to know

No biology prerequisite to start the anatomy class is built in and only asks that you are college-ready in English and math. The classes run as one cohort, in order.

Bigger plans, later: the full associate degree or the esthetician certificate are both open too, this one is just the fastest start.

It opens doors too: the associate is the popular springboard into Radiologic Technology (X-ray) or Sonography at Red Rocks, and the anatomy and science classes also carry toward a Physical Therapy Assistant program. So if any of those ever call to you, a lot of the work is already done.
