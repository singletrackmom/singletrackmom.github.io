Medical assistant, med spa

Same wellness-and-beauty world you already gravitate to, but as a real career with room to grow. A med spa medical assistant keeps the treatment side running for the providers: the plastic surgeons, the nurse injectors, the skincare pros. Calm, pretty rooms. No ER chaos. And the school path is short and close to home.

What you actually do

You keep the glow running

You greet and room clients, prep the treatment rooms, set up for Botox, filler, and laser, take the before-and-after photos, hand things to the provider, and walk clients through aftercare. It is people-facing and hands-on, and it rewards being warm, organized, and good with people, which is already you.

Who hires them

There is one two minutes away

Med spas and plastic surgery offices are all over Denver, but here is the fun part: Rejuvenate MedSpa sits right on Heritage Road in Golden, run by board-certified plastic surgeons with nurse injectors and skincare pros on staff. It is exactly this world, walking distance. A place you could literally ask to shadow.

The life · the perk

The wellness world, as a career

You already light up around wellness and beauty. This is that, but a career that pays and grows. In Denver these roles run about $22 to $30 an hour, so full-time is roughly $46K to $62K, and often more once a med spa adds bonuses on treatments and products. And if you ever want to be the one doing the injections, that is a nurse path you can grow into from right here.

How to get in

Red Rocks does both

You were right: Red Rocks Community College, the closest campus to home, runs a Medical Assisting program and an Esthetician skincare program. So you could start with medical assisting and stack skincare later, or lean whichever way excites you. Short programs, not a four-year anything. Pima Medical Institute is the faster-track backup if you want it.

How to get qualified (just this one job)

Good news: no long degree. Red Rocks Community College (closest to home) runs both a Medical Assisting certificate and an Esthetician skincare program, so you can lean whichever way lights you up, and even stack them. Pima Medical Institute is the faster associate route you already toured.

The smallest first step: pop into Rejuvenate MedSpa on Heritage Road and ask if you could shadow for an afternoon, or just open a Red Rocks program page and read for five minutes. No commitment. Only a look.
