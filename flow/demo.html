Today’s idea to explore

Airline Customer Service

Travel perks without the cabin · phone or airport · Spanish pays

A travel job that is not flight attendant: airlines hire phone and reservations agents (many fully remote) and airport customer service / gate agents at the terminal. The best part is the flight perks: you fly free on standby, your family usually flies free too, and you get deep discounts on tickets anywhere the airline goes. Pay starts around $19 to $24 an hour (with benefits and paid time off) and climbs, and your Spanish gets you hired faster and often earns a little extra.

Denver (DEN) is one of the busiest airports in the country, about 25 minutes away, and United, Southwest, and Frontier all hire customer service here. Peek at the openings and watch a quick “day in the life” · a lot of the phone roles are work-from-home.
