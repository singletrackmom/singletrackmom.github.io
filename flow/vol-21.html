How to get qualified (the real path)

The credential employers ask for is a health coach certificate, no degree, no four years. The least-daunting first step is the ACE Health Coach Certification, a self-paced, NCCA-accredited program you can finish in about three to six months from home. There are no school prerequisites, you just need to be 18, have a high school diploma, and hold a CPR/AED card to sit the exam. That one certificate is enough to start getting hired as a coach.

The long game, only if you want it: the top-paying roles ask for the National Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach (NBC-HWC) credential, which needs an associate degree or 4,000 coaching hours first. So do the ACE cert now, start working, and if you love it, pick up a two-year associate later (Red Rocks or Front Range transfer to save money) to unlock the board exam and the higher pay. No rush, and it is yours to choose.
