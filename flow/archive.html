Medical Assisting, the Plan
A closer look at the medical-assistant path she asked about: the fast way in is the Red Rocks Medical Assisting certificate, about 28 credits and roughly a year, ending ready for the national CMA exam, since Colorado employers hire on the certification, not a four-year degree. It stacks into the 61-credit associate later, and her Spanish, English, and history already count toward that degree, so nothing is wasted. The esthetician certificate (about 20 credits) stays on the table and can stack on later, but the medical-assistant certificate is the stronger first move: it pays more, it is a national credential, and it opens far more doors, clinics, dermatology, hospitals, and even travel assistant assignments in places like Hawaii and California.
