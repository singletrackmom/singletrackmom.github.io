Radiography and imaging programs

Where your medical assistant work gets you into imaging

Colorado programs, checked on each school’s own site.

Goal

Show you which imaging programs are within reach after the Red Rocks certificate, and exactly where your medical assistant work earns points on the application.

Audience

You, finishing the MA certificate and watching classmates line up for radiography and other imaging programs next.

Process

Every fact here was read on the school’s own program page or admissions handbook on November 22, 2026. Where a school’s own pages disagree with each other, this page says so. Programs near Golden are at the top. Programs that mean moving are below them. Sonography, the other imaging path, is last.

What your MA work counts for

No school on this page gives points for the CMA certificate by itself. What scores is paid, face-to-face patient care, and letters from the people who supervised it. So the certificate opens the door, and the MA job you take after it is what earns the points.

Medical terminology

Every radiography program here lists medical terminology as a prerequisite or a recommended course. If your MA coursework included one, check the course number on your transcript against what each school asks for. Red Rocks accepts HPR 1038, HPR 1039 or HPR 1040.

Radiography near Golden

All three end in an associate degree and the ARRT radiography exam. Clinicals are unpaid at every one.

Your school

Red Rocks Community College, Radiography AAS, Arvada

Five semesters, 79 credits, starting each fall. The hard one to get into.

About $15,600 in state, not counting prerequisites

Clinical internships are placed around the Denver metro, up to 100 miles away. They are unpaid, shifts run 8 to 10 hours, and the program suggests limiting outside work while you are in it.

How you are scored: 100 points. Prerequisite GPA 50, two recommendation letters 20, personal statement 10, paid healthcare experience 10, job shadowing 10. Top scorers interview, and the interview alone decides who moves on.

Where your MA helps: the 10 healthcare experience points are for six or more months of paid, direct patient care, which is what an MA job is. Letters from healthcare supervisors or co-workers score higher, so your clinic supervisor is the letter to ask for. Job shadowing is 1 point per hour with a radiologic technologist, up to 10, and you set it up yourself.

Prerequisites: RTE 1001 Intro to Radiography, medical terminology, ENG 1021 or 1031, MAT 1340 or higher, BIO 2101 and 2102, and one PSY or SOC course, all with a C or better. They can be in progress when you apply but must be finished by May.

Honest flag: Red Rocks lists the window as December 1 to January 31 on one page and December 15 to January 31 on another. Apply in mid-December and you are inside both.

Biggest class

Community College of Denver, Radiologic Technology AAS

22 months full time, or a new 27-month part-time option with evening classes. Lowry campus, moving to Auraria in January 2027.

About $18,000 in state, prerequisites included

More than 200 people apply each year and about 35 to 38 get in. Attending an information session is required, and the application asks for the date you went.

How you are scored: up to 65 application points, then up to 35 for the interview. GPA 20 (they want at least 3.0 in science courses), patient care or customer service experience 15, two professional references 10, essay 10, job shadowing 5 (six documented hours minimum), and 5 more for taking RTE 1001 at CCD with a B or better.

Where your MA helps: patient care experience is the second-biggest category on the application. The essay asks you to reflect on your job shadowing, so shadow first and write second.

Prerequisites: ENG 1021 or 1031, MAT 1240 or 1340, one of PSY 1001, PSY 2440, SOC 1001 or COM 1250, BIO 2101 and 2102 (with BIO 1010 or 1111 first), and RTE 1001. Medical terminology (HPR 1039) is strongly recommended now and will be required later.

Hospital-based

UCHealth School of Radiologic Technology, Aurora

Run by the hospital at the Anschutz campus. 23 months, six semesters including summers, ending in an AAS.

No federal student loans · clinicals unpaid

The Aurora window is August 1 to October 1 each year, so this year’s has passed and the next one opens August 2027. The same program runs in Colorado Springs at UCHealth Memorial on a January 1 to March 1 window.

How you are picked: the top 50 applicants by points interview. Points come from GPA, coursework, employer references, work experience and previous health care experience.

Where your MA helps: patient care experience is a named factor, and they want three employment references plus an employer reference form, so your MA job and supervisor count twice.

Prerequisites: career math or college algebra, English comp, A&P I and II with lab, general psychology, intro to radiology or health professions, and medical terminology, each with a C or better.

Honest flag: the school does not take federal student loans, so the money side needs a plan before you apply.

Radiography if you move

Real options, but each one means living near campus for about two years.

Morgan Community College, Radiologic Technology AAS, Fort Morgan

22 months full time, 77 credits. Out on the plains northeast of Denver.

Applications accepted in late spring

Where your MA helps: prior health care work experience is one of the named scoring areas, along with prerequisite GPA, your grades in A&P and Intro to Radiography, writing skills, and living in their service area. Medical terminology is recommended there, not required, so yours is a plus.

Prerequisites: PSY 2440, ENG 1021, BIO 2101 and 2102, and RTE 1001 (taken within a year of acceptance, 80 percent or better).

Pueblo Community College, Radiologic Technology AAS, Pueblo

Five semesters, 77 credits, starting each fall. The program calls it like having a full-time job.

Applications due April 1 · 30 to 40 accepted

Everyone takes a test on the prerequisite material, is scored in five areas, and the ones who qualify interview with a panel. The top 30 to 40 combined scores get in.

Where your MA helps: medical terminology (HPR 1038) is a prerequisite you may already have. The program pages do not name the five scoring areas, so ask the program whether patient care counts.

Prerequisites: ENG 1021, MAT 1140, BIO 1006, HPR 1038, and RTE 1001, which is offered in spring only.

Sonography

Ultrasound, the no-radiation side of imaging, from letter 9. Both options want an associate degree or about two years of college first, so this is the longer road.

Red Rocks Community College, Diagnostic Medical Sonography BAS, Arvada

A bachelor’s degree in 21 months, once you have an associate degree or about two years of college. General or echocardiography (heart).

About $23,500 · 9 to 10 general seats, 6 echo

The window is January 5 to February 28. Starting with spring 2027 applicants, every prerequisite has to be finished at the time you apply, and physics and a communications course join the list.

Where your MA helps: paid patient care is scored, and medical assistant is on the program’s preferred allied health list, which earns higher points. Job shadowing with a sonographer earns 1 point for every 4 hours, up to 40 hours. Medical terminology (HPR 1040) is a prerequisite.

UCHealth Diagnostic Medical Sonography, Aurora

A 13-month certificate program at University of Colorado Hospital, Monday to Friday.

$20,000 tuition · 12 seats for 2027

You need an associate or bachelor’s degree to apply, and an MA certificate alone does not qualify. They get 40 to 60 applications a year. The current window runs September 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027.

Where your MA helps: the program says MA job titles count as direct patient care experience, one of the things it looks for in competitive applicants.

What to do now

  • Take the prerequisites at Red Rocks with strong grades. A&P I and II (BIO 2101 and 2102), English comp, MAT 1340, PSY 1001 and RTE 1001 cover Red Rocks, CCD and UCHealth. Prerequisite GPA is half of the Red Rocks score.
  • Plan around A&P. Part II comes after Part I, and CCD now wants BIO 1010 or 1111 before Part I, so map the semesters before you pick a cycle.
  • Work as an MA for at least six months. Paid, face-to-face patient care is the category every program scores.
  • Shadow a radiologic technologist. Ten hours covers the Red Rocks maximum and the CCD minimum of six.
  • Line up two healthcare supervisors for letters. Ask three or four people in case one does not follow through.
  • Go to an information session. Required at CCD, and Red Rocks runs one too.
