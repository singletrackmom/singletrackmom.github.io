How this works

Find Your Flow

a tiny career letter, made just for you

This is a little daily letter built for you by an AI helper your mom set up. Here is the whole idea, plainly.

Goal

Help you find a career that actually fits, one small step at a time, with zero pressure. Every letter surfaces a real option with a good outlook and good pay, shows the real path to qualify, and a real job so you can see it is not made up.

Audience

You, Jillian. Every pick is weighed against what you actually like (people, travel, fitness, helping, no kids) and what you want (not a lot of school, not math-heavy, near Colorado or a short move). If it does not fit you, it does not go in.

Process · how a letter gets made

  1. Pick a fresh career (never a repeat), often straight off the Bureau of Labor Statistics fast-growing list.
  2. Pull the real numbers: growth, pay, and the accredited Colorado training, plus live job listings so you see the pay yourself.
  3. Write it up as a swipeable letter with a clear “how to get qualified” box and one real job.
  4. Keep a running list of what has been covered, so it stays fresh and honest.

Nothing here is decided for you. These are options to explore, and the choice is always yours. No personal information about you is collected or stored.
