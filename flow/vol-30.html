Community health worker · the person who gets people through

What it is

You walk people through the system

A doctor says “see a specialist, get these labs, come back in a month.” A lot of people never make it, because of a ride, a bill, a form, or just not knowing who to call. A community health worker or patient navigator is the one who sits down with them, makes the calls, books the appointment, finds the food or housing help, and checks back until it is done. The job titles to search: community health worker, patient navigator, care navigator, care coordinator.

Why it might fit you

You already speak clinic

Your medical assistant certificate is not wasted here, it is a head start. You know how a clinic day runs, how scheduling and charts work, and how to talk to a nervous patient. This job keeps the people part and drops the needles: it is talking, problem solving, and moving between the front desk, the exam room, and the community. Postings often ask for a year of patient-care experience, which is exactly what MA work gives you.

The money and the outlook

About $52,000, and growing fast

The government says half of community health workers earn more than $51,850 a year and half earn less (May 2025). Jobs are projected to grow 13% over the next ten years, much faster than average, with about 7,000 openings a year. The usual way in is a high school diploma plus on-the-job training, and a short certificate makes you stand out.

Where a bachelor’s takes it

Health education specialist

With a public health bachelor’s, the next rung is health education: running the programs, classes, and outreach that community health workers carry out. The median is $64,070 a year (May 2025), growing 6%, faster than average. A bachelor’s is the ask, plus an optional exam called the CHES. No master’s needed.

One real job, live right now

Care Coordinator, STRIDE Community Health Center

Wheat Ridge Clinic · $20.67 to $24.03 an hour · Monday to Friday, days · hybrid after training

About ten minutes from Golden. You check in with patients about things like food and housing, connect them to help in the community, follow each referral until it lands, and call people who need a follow-up visit. They ask for a high school diploma, a BLS card, and a year of supporting patient care. A second language is a big plus. No nights or weekends. Open until filled, checked live today.

Where to study, close to home

A short certificate now, a bachelor’s if you want more

  • Red Rocks · Patient Navigator certificate, 12 credits, in Lakewood. The page does not say how many semesters, so that is a question to ask.Start
  • MSU Denver · Community Health Worker Training Program: five online, self-paced courses, $450 each with the fifth free (about $1,800 plus fees), 2 to 3 semesters. Built for Colorado’s CHW certification.Start
  • Community College of Denver · Public Health transfer associate, about 2 years, designed to move into the MSU Denver public health degree.Next
  • MSU Denver · Public Health bachelor’s, offered fully online, about $326 a credit for Colorado residents in 2026–27. Its Community Health Educator certificate preps you for the CHES exam.Finish

You do not need a bachelor’s to start as a community health worker or navigator. You need one for the health educator rung. Everything here stops at a bachelor’s.
