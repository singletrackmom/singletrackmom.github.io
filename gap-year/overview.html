Gap Year Planner

Personal Agents · Case study

OverviewLive toolPRD

A scheduled agent that keeps one university student’s plan for living, working and studying abroad current: country guides, seasonal jobs, visas and the savings it takes.

Goal

Turn a vague wish to spend a year abroad into a plan that holds up: where it is legal to work, what each place costs, which jobs are hiring for the coming season and what has to be saved before leaving, without losing ground on a graduate health-professions path.

Audience

One university student weighing a gap year, a season of work abroad or study abroad, and needing each option laid out side by side before committing.

Process

SectionWhat it holds
Country guidesVisa rules with the official link, work options, cost of living, study abroad, transport and language for each home base.
Seasonal workWorking holiday visas, work exchange and resort jobs, with the season each one hires for.
MoneyWhat to have saved before leaving and the scams to watch for.
Staying on trackHow time abroad fits around the hands-on hours graduate programs require.
MapEvery home base pinned, each linked to its guide.

A scheduled agent re-checks the planner, opens every link, updates visa rules and adds newly posted seasonal and resort jobs. Its companion, Pre-Health Pathway, covers the pre-health path at home.

Technology

A scheduled AI agent working from written rules, publishing a static, noindexed page with an interactive map built on Leaflet and OpenStreetMap, and no logins, forms or tracking.

Outcomes

In use. It began as the travel half of a study abroad board and became its own planner once a gap year was on the table.
