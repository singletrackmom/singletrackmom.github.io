Online course design and learning experiences, AI tools, and agentic builds for how people learn next.

AI tools & strategy

RenderAn AI career-launch environment, blank page to working pilot. Students build it across my capstone and graduate owning a portable career agent. District barriers studyA 10-college UX study of the student journey across Maricopa, mapping barriers and turning them into prioritized, human-in-the-loop AI pilots. CopaMigoA bilingual AI triage tool that routes a student’s plain-language problem to the right human service, with a warm handoff card at the end. Dial Your CourseA tool that reviews a whole Canvas course for alignment, accessibility, and seat-hour compliance, then fixes what it finds. Nothing leaves your machine. Syllabus CheckerA submit-and-check tool that verifies syllabi against MCCCD and program requirements, replies to faculty instantly, and logs every submission in one place. CultivateMy own connectivist learning environment: a hub for professional development and a curated news feed. My first AI build, the experiment that started all of this.

Current research

Agentic & personal builds

WayfinderA living road-trip planner with live weather, fuel strategy, and mapped stops, plus a generator that builds one for anyone from their own interests. SoarA daily agent that rebuilds an aerospace dashboard for a college-bound engineer: space news, launches, clubs, and a live internship watch. FocusA daily agent that surfaces real, verified job leads for a working photographer, checked live on employers’ sites and published to a private board. Devan Does Travel StuffA gap-year travel planner with an interactive map, a savings plan, and a work-exchange model that turns a year abroad into something fundable. Summer WorkA rolling agent that finds high-paying, flexible summer gig work, verified live on employers’ own sites and refreshed every single morning. Find Your FlowA gentle career-exploration agent that serves one realistic, low-school career path a day, with a real open job showing the true pay and daily reality.
