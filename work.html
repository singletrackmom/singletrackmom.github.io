Work

AI tools, learning experiences, and agentic builds for how people learn next. Screenshots below; short walkthroughs on the way.

AI tools & strategy

RenderAn AI career-launch environment taken from a blank page to a working pilot. Students build it across my capstone and graduate owning a portable career agent and learning plan. District barriers studyA 10-college UX study of the student journey, mapping barriers and turning them into prioritized, human-in-the-loop AI pilots. My League for Innovation Fellows project. CopaMigoA bilingual AI triage tool that routes a student’s plain-language problem to the right human service with a warm handoff card. CultivateMy own connectivist learning environment: a hub for professional development and a curated news feed. My first AI build, the experiment that started all of this.

In the classroom

Intro to Digital Arts redesignLed the redesign of my program’s highest-enrollment, lowest-success course around student support and connection, grounded in retention data and measured by change in success rate. Design history, taught by studentsStudents learn design history by teaching it, each pair building an interactive tool for their era. The extra-credit version is an AI vibe-coding challenge, no coding required. Rough CutA feeder-school outreach newsletter built on a research spreadsheet covering 36+ regional high schools, reaching future students before for-profit colleges do.

Agentic & personal builds

WayfinderA living road-trip planner with live weather, a fuel strategy, van-clearance awareness, and mapped stops, plus a generator that builds one for anyone from their interests. SoarA daily agent that rebuilds an aerospace dashboard for a college-bound engineer: news, launches, clubs, and a live internship watch. FocusA daily agent that finds fresh photo-gig leads and a marketing idea for a working photographer, published to a private board. Devan Does Travel StuffA gap-year travel planner with an interactive map, a savings plan, and a work-exchange model for a fundable year abroad. Summer WorkA rolling agent that finds high-paying, flexible summer gig work, verified live on employers’ own sites, refreshed each morning. Find Your FlowA gentle career-exploration agent that serves one realistic, low-school career path a day, with a real open job showing the true pay and day-to-day.
