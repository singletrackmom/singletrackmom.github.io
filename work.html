Work

A selection of work I’m proudest of. The AI tools come first because that is where my attention is now, not because the rest is finished: the Traveler, the Design Studio, and the internship program still run every year. All of it comes out of the same practice, decades of online course design, instructional design, and experiential learning.

AI tools & strategy

Synthetic SMEs & OER Course BuildingA rigorous graduate STEM course built from open educational resources by a panel of AI agents cast as a subject-matter expert, an instructional designer, and a synthetic student, with a human in the loop. Includes the full course, a styled Canvas view, eight module decks, an instructor prep kit, and the reusable build skill. Dial Your CourseDrop a Canvas course in. Nineteen checks run against it: seat hours, outcome alignment, accessibility, AI resistance. Approve the fixes, get the course back. Student Journey Gap AnalysisA 10-college UX study across Maricopa that sends synthetic-student agents down the real student journey to find where students hit barriers, ranked and turned into prioritized, human-in-the-loop AI pilots. CopaMigoA bilingual AI triage tool that routes a student’s plain-language problem to the right human service with a warm handoff card. RenderAn AI career-launch environment, blank page to working pilot. Students build it across my capstone and graduate owning a portable career agent. Syllabus CheckerA submit-and-check tool that verifies syllabi against MCCCD and program requirements, replies to faculty instantly, and logs every submission in one place. CultivateMy own personal learning environment: a hub for professional development and a curated news feed. My first AI build, the experiment that started all of this.
