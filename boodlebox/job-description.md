# BoodleBox: Onboarding & Digital Success Lead
Captured 10 Oct 2026 from https://boodlebox.ai/careers/onboarding-digital-success-lead (detailed capture, not verbatim). Full-time. Remote, United States; company hub in Colorado Springs, CO. About 20 people. VC-backed GenAI platform for higher education: 100,000+ users across 1,200+ colleges and universities. Values: service, education, collaboration.

## The role
First working contact for a new partner college after the contract is signed. Technical setup (SSO, roster sync, training schedules, semester-start timelines). Checklist-driven project management plus relationship work. A book of smaller partner accounts and a scalable "digital motion" to keep them engaged. Daily personal use of AI, interest in building bots, workflows or sites. "Build the function, not inherit it."

## What you'll own
1. Every onboarding: handoff from sales through kickoff, implementation plan, timelines, milestones, exit criteria, handoff to customer success. No school should have to start twice.
2. Technical setup: SSO, LMS integrations, rostering, provisioning, workspace configuration; partners live on time.
3. The implementation plan: tailored to the institution's goals, owners, and what must be true by week two, week six and end of term. Not a template.
4. A book of business: partners under 200 seats without an implementation package or expansion plan. Owns engagement, health, renewal and expansion from kickoff.
5. The digital motion: onboarding sequences, updates and touchpoints that keep many accounts informed and growing without a call for each.
6. The process itself: continuously improve playbooks, checklists, exit criteria, templates.
7. The details: recap emails, follow-ups, a calendar that holds up in the heaviest onboarding season.

## Who you are
1. Project manager who loves people: holds timelines without nagging; moves dates deliberately.
2. Technically fearless: comfortable with SSO, LTI, rostering, technical docs, admin consoles; solves rather than escalates.
3. Highly organized: twelve onboardings at once without anything slipping.
4. Systems builder: workflows, templates, sequences and docs so repeated questions answer themselves.
5. Warm at scale: small partners feel known; low-touch does not mean low-care.
6. AI proficient: has built workflows; beyond casual use.
7. Calm under pressure: precise when a semester is at stake.
8. Mission-driven: no legacy title required.

## Hiring approach
No implementation title required. Backgrounds named as welcome: IT, student services, the registrar's office, help desks, teaching, event production. No enterprise SaaS experience required. Wants evidence of making a complicated thing succeed on a real deadline. Adoption is usually decided in the first thirty days.

## Offer
Competitive base plus bonus (no number posted). Unlimited PTO. Medical, dental, vision with 75% of premiums paid. 401(k). Fully remote.

## How to apply
Email careers@boodle.ai, not just a resume. Subject line that tells them something about you. Include: LinkedIn profile; a time you were handed something complicated with a hard deadline and made it land (what almost went wrong, what you did); a link, screenshot or description of something you built to solve a real need (bonus if built with AI).
