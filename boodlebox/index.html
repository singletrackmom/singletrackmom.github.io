Gemini under-18 study (Examples of What Gemini Blocks for Students Under 18)

Hard deadline · what almost went wrong · many stakeholders · built with AI

  • Google Gemini student accounts for Maricopa held up by the faculty council; presented several times, new list of questions each time
  • Final ask: what outcomes are different for students under 18
  • EDUCAUSE: district IT director asked how the committee could show it; Google booth had no data and wants the results
  • Weekend, due Monday. Claude agents (synthetic subject-matter experts plus an instructional designer) built 27 authentic assignments from Maricopa course competencies
  • High-risk courses on purpose: nursing, criminal justice, art, music, information literacy, AI courses
  • Every step run in an under-18 Gemini test account and an 18 and older account, side by side
  • What almost went wrong: measuring competencies; switched to “can the student finish it, and what does the instructor change” with a day left
  • Recommendations: library databases alongside Deep Research for every student; “Under 18: do this instead” steps (reference tracks for music, a new prompt for image editing, a storyboard for video)
  • Result: 26 of 27 completable under 18, 8 with an alternate step; tentative January rollout approved
