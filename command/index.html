The vocabulary, and what each one means here

Reading the cards below. This is the part I am studying, so the definitions are on the page rather than in my head.
Product goal
The single outcome this product exists to reach. Not a feature list. If it is met, the product worked.
Release plan
The increments, in order. What has shipped, what is shipping now, what comes after.
Sprint backlog
Committed for the current sprint. Short, finishable, and the only list I am allowed to work from today.
Product backlog
Wanted but not committed, ordered by value. The parking lot with a priority on it. Items move up into a sprint, they do not just accumulate.
Impediment
Blocked, and not on me to unblock. A decision, an approval, or a person I am waiting on. Naming it is the point: an impediment nobody names never gets removed.
Definition of done
What has to be true before I am allowed to call it finished. Built is not done. Done means there is evidence it worked.
Increment
A usable slice that could stand on its own. Every pilot here is an increment, not a launch.
Product owner
The person accountable for what gets built and in what order, and for whether it delivered value. On these ten, that is me.
