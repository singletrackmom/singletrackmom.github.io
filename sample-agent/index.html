What Alex chose to build

The export contains everything from the semester. Which parts become the agent is the student’s decision, and it should differ from this one. Alex is going freelance in motion design, so the sections above are the three that carry that plan.

Built

Open roles, because the search is active. Professional development, because three of four target postings named a skill the resume could not answer. Mentors and contacts, because the freelance work is arriving through people rather than through boards.

Skipped, on purpose

Salary research, already settled for this market. Interview prep, nothing scheduled yet. A portfolio update tracker, because the reel is the portfolio and it lives in one file.

What a different plan would need

A student going in house at an agency would build interview prep and a company watchlist, and might drop the contact tracker entirely. Someone freelancing from day one needs a client pipeline, a rate sheet, and invoices due. Someone applying to graduate programs needs deadlines, portfolio requirements, and recommenders, which is the contact tracker wearing a different label.

There is no correct set. The question is which three or four things you will actually open every week.

How this was built, and how it stays current

Render is training wheels. It runs for one semester, and the last assignment is to stop needing it.

  1. Render exports a zip. Resume versions, the job and client log, contacts, goals, the skills list, and a Markdown career agent assembled from all of it. Everything from fifteen weeks, in files the student owns.
  2. The student builds this page. Not Render. Taking the export and turning it into something readable is the capstone, and it is the part that proves the system was understood rather than filled in.
  3. After that, the workflow is ordinary. Resume plus a job posting, pasted into whichever AI tool they use, and a file that carries what the AI cannot remember. No Render, no login, nothing to renew.
  4. Update the file, republish the page. Check off what is done, add the link to what was made, shrink the gap that closed, add the one that just appeared. Same address, new content behind it.
