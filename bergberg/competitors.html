What This Tells You

  • You’re not too expensive. Photographers with less experience than you charge more. Top 10 in Colorado and 200+ weddings is worth a middle price, not a discount.
  • Watch Evergreen Photo Co. and LANZ. Both give two photographers for about what you charge, and Evergreen Photo Co. includes film and a USB drive. Make sure couples know everything that comes in your packages.
  • Others use off-season prices. Amazing Colorado and Tommy Rhyan both charge less for off-season dates. That’s the same idea as Vanilla Bean on the Pricing tab.
  • A 4-hour package around $2,200 to $2,750 is normal. You don’t have one yet for a regular wedding.
  • Don’t chase $999. Those couples want the lowest price, not your experience.
