Venues

How You Get on a List

For a venue that doesn’t know you yet: email, visit with an album, leave brochures, follow up.

Show the steps and the first email

This is how you got on the list at most of your venues, and it still works:

  1. Reach out to the person who plans events there. Email first. Say you’re local Golden photographers with more than 20 years of weddings and a top 10 ranking in Colorado.
  2. Ask for 20 minutes. “Could we stop by and show you our album?”
  3. Bring a printed album. Weddings at their venue if you have them. If not, weddings at a venue that looks like theirs.
  4. Leave brochures and a card. Ask how many they go through in a season.
  5. Offer photos of their space for free, with credit, for their website and Instagram.
  6. Ask two questions. “How do photographers get on your recommended list?” and “Do you host open houses or vendor nights?”
  7. Follow up in two days with a thank-you and a link to your venue page or a wedding at a similar venue.
  8. Add them to the rotation on the Calendar tab, so you check in every couple of months.

New venues are the best bet. Their lists are short or empty, and the planner there is still deciding who to trust.

We’re also happy to give you photos of your space for your website and Instagram, free with credit. Here are a few of our weddings: [link to your venue page or a similar wedding] Thank you, [Your names] Berg Berg Photography · bergbergphotography.com

What to bring

  • One printed album, best work first.
  • Ten or more brochures and a stack of cards.
  • Your award, if it’s easy to carry.
  • A phone or iPad with your venue page open.
  • A one-page list of planners you’ve worked with. Coordinators love to see names they know.

Winter Park and Grand County

You’re strong at Headwaters Center. Use it to get into the venues nearby.

  • Grand Lake Lodge has no photographer list.
  • Snow Mountain Ranch (YMCA of the Rockies) hosts lots of weddings with no photographer list, and you’ve shot there before.
  • Winter Park Resort does mountaintop weddings. Ask the weddings office.
  • Hidden Springs Ranch in Granby has a long list. Lead with your Headwaters weddings.
  • Join the Grand County wedding vendor group listed on Destination Granby.

Getting Into Blackstone Rivers Ranch

A private riverfront mountain venue about 35 minutes west of Denver, one wedding a day. It fits your style, and several of your planners already work there.

  • Through a planner. This is the fastest way in. Ask Mag Events or Flora Moon: “If you have a Blackstone couple looking for a photographer, we’d love to be on your short list.”
  • Through search. Post a few mountain and river photos from other venues with “similar to Blackstone Rivers Ranch” in the alt text and pin descriptions. It worked for you at Headwaters.
  • Directly. Once you have one Blackstone wedding, send the venue the gallery and ask to be on their list.

Album deals, when there’s money

Albums aren’t a top priority right now, since there’s no budget for them. When there is, wait for a WHCC sale. WHCC runs sitewide sales a couple of times a year. The last one was their early Black Friday sale (up to 15 percent off, with a $100 cap), and it ended 22 November. Watch the WHCC emails in your inbox for the next one, and price a Headwaters or Applewood album then.

For a venue album, use weddings from that venue in every season, put your name on the cover or spine, and tuck a few brochures inside the back cover.
