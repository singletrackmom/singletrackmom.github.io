Skip the giant expos at the convention centers. They cost a lot, and you book through real conversations, not a 250-booth room.

Every Month

On the first of each month, about 20 minutes:

  • Read the Google Search Console email. Are venue searches getting more clicks?
  • Ask the five AI questions on the SEO tab and write down the answers.
  • Count how inquiries said they found you.
  • Check off this month on the Start tab and pick next month’s three biggest jobs.

Keep a Simple Log

After each venue or planner note, write one line in a note on your phone: the date, who, and what they said. Next time, start where you left off. If two notes in a row get no answer, move that name to the bottom and spend your time on someone else.
