Dial Your Course

Case study · AI Tools & Strategy · Learning design

OverviewPRDSample reportStudent workload

The writing and interface rules the tool follows when it reports a finding back to an instructor.

Twelve palettes. Every one meets WCAG AA contrast, on every text pair. Choose a color and a typeface, and the whole course matches. The contrast ratios update as you choose, so a combination a student cannot read will never reach them.
