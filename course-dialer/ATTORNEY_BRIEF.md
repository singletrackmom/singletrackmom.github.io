I want to (a) confirm my ownership is clean, (b) understand what protection realistically applies, and (c) get timing advice before I present it publicly, so I do not accidentally forfeit any rights.

Residential (full-time) faculty in Digital Media Arts at Glendale Community College, part of the Maricopa Community Colleges District in Arizona. That committee work is separate from this tool and did not request or fund it.

## 3. What the tool is
"Dial Your Course" is a web-based tool for higher-education faculty and instructional designers. It does two things:
- Audits an existing course against accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA), Quality Matters / SUNY OSCQR / OLC quality rubrics, credit-hour and seat-time rules, learning-outcome-to-assessment alignment, syllabus policy requirements, Regular and Substantive Interaction, and authentic / AI-resistant assessment design.
- Helps build a course from scratch, starting from the instructor's objectives and projects.
It is not tied to any single learning management system. Current status: working prototype, in testing. A larger version 2 is planned (AI-assisted alignment suggestions, a synthetic student-agent usability test, a continuously updated research library, and an institution-settings layer).
