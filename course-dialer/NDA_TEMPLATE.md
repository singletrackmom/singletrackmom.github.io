# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (template)

**NOT legal advice.** This is a plain, standard starting template for showing Dial Your Course to a company under confidentiality. Fill in the brackets. Before you rely on it in a real negotiation, have an IP/edtech attorney review it. Send it as a PDF (from a Word/Google Doc), not as this markdown.

Key protections already built in for you: the other side gets no license and no ownership of anything you show (Section 6), and signing does not obligate anyone to do a deal (Section 7).

---

## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of **[DATE]** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Michelle Blomberg**, an individual, of **[CITY, STATE]** ("Party A"), and

**[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]**, a **[STATE] [corporation/LLC]**, of **[ADDRESS]** ("Party B").

Party A and Party B are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

### 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship relating to **Dial Your Course**, a software tool created by Party A for reviewing and improving online courses (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose certain confidential information to the other.

### 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient"), in any form (oral, written, visual, electronic, a live demonstration, or source code), that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances. It includes, without limitation, the Dial Your Course software, its source code, methods, checks, reference library, designs, user interface, roadmaps, pilot data, and business plans, as well as the fact and content of the Parties' discussions.

### 3. Obligations
The Recipient shall: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) hold it in strict confidence and protect it with at least the same care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; (c) not disclose it to any third party except to the Recipient's employees, advisors, or agents who have a need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement; and (d) not copy, reverse engineer, decompile, or create derivative works from the Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose.

### 4. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show: (a) was publicly available at the time of disclosure or later became public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was rightfully in the Recipient's possession without confidentiality obligation before disclosure; (c) was rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligation; or (d) was independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

### 5. Required Disclosure
If the Recipient is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so only to the extent required, and shall give the Discloser prompt written notice (where legally permitted) so the Discloser may seek protection.

### 6. No License, No Transfer of Rights
All Confidential Information remains the sole property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any license, ownership, or other right in the Discloser's Confidential Information, intellectual property, patents, copyrights, trademarks, or trade secrets, whether by implication, estoppel, or otherwise. Dial Your Course and all associated intellectual property remain the exclusive property of Party A.

### 7. No Obligation
Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to proceed with any transaction, partnership, employment, purchase, or business relationship. Any such relationship, if any, would be set out in a separate signed agreement.

### 8. Term
This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for **[2]** years. The Recipient's confidentiality obligations survive for **[3]** years after disclosure of the relevant Confidential Information (or, for information that is a trade secret, for as long as it remains a trade secret under applicable law).

### 9. Return or Destruction
Upon the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and copies, and certify destruction in writing, except for one archival copy retained solely for legal-compliance purposes.

### 10. No Warranty
All Confidential Information is provided "as is." The Discloser makes no warranties regarding its accuracy or completeness.

### 11. Remedies
The Parties agree that a breach may cause irreparable harm for which money damages are inadequate, and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or equity.

### 12. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **[COLORADO]**, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. It is the entire agreement between the Parties on this subject and supersedes prior discussions. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronically.

### How to use it
1. Fill in every **[bracket]** (date, your city/state, the company's legal name and address, the signer's name/title, governing-state, and the two term numbers).
2. Put it in a Word or Google Doc, export a clean PDF.
3. Send it before you show anything beyond the public video, and get it signed before a live demo of the working tool.
4. Governing law: your home state (Colorado) is a reasonable default; a larger company may push for theirs. This is fine to negotiate.
5. Have an attorney review before you use it in an actual deal conversation.
