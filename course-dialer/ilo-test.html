How the tool maps a course to the institutional learning outcomes it already evidences, and which are assessed elsewhere.

Same data every time: AVC 100 already grades work that evidences 6 institutional outcomes (VC3, VC4, VC5, TE1, TE2, W4). The goal is to make what the green means obvious in the output, without a hover or a click, while keeping it graphical. Pick a direction, or mix. Not linked anywhere.

Version D

What you asked for: each candidate outcome shown against the specific AVC 100 lesson that would evidence it, then the instrument recommends the one or two strongest to actually attach. Fewer, stronger, defensible. This is also the shape v2’s AI fills in by reading the real content.

Which lesson maps to which outcome, and the strongest to attach

The instrument names the evidence and recommends. You decide.
Recommended · strongest fit
VC3
Execution: Technical Skills
Maps to Photographic Postcard, Step 3: Refine and Social Media Animation, Step 3: Refine, where students execute in Photoshop and After Effects and choose the right tool and workflow.
Strongest because technical execution across the three programs is what this course is built to teach, and both final projects already grade it.
VC5
Analysis: Critique & Interpretation
Maps to Course Reflection (10 pts), where students analyze and interpret their own finished work.
Strongest analysis evidence in the course, and it sits at the end of the term when a student’s thinking is most mature.
Also possible · weaker or overlapping
VC4
Delivery: Visual Production
Would attach to the same Refine steps as VC3. Overlaps, adds little.
TE1
Acquiring: Evaluates Creative Process
Would attach to the same Course Reflection as VC5. Overlaps.
TE2
Risks: Seeks Untested Approaches
No current criterion rewards creative risk; a rubric line would need to be added first.
W4
Job Search: Marketing Self to Employers
AVC 100 has no published portfolio yet, so there is no criterion to attach it to. Weakest fit for this course.
