CopaMigo

AI tools & strategy

A bilingual AI triage tool that routes a student’s plain-language problem to the right human service with a warm handoff card.

What it is

CopaMigo is a stigma-aware triage tool. A student describes their situation in plain language, in English or Spanish, and it routes them to the right campus service, such as financial aid, food, childcare, or counseling, with a warm handoff card showing the contact, hours, and what to ask for. It is anonymous with no login, and the answers are written and maintained by the campus staff who would field these questions in person.

Who it’s for

Students hitting life barriers, especially first-generation and Spanish-speaking students, who are not sure where to go for help. The ones who most need connection are often the least likely to seek it out: they do not know which office handles what, or what to even call the thing they need. CopaMigo meets them in their own words first.

The goal

Get a stuck student to the right person quickly, without asking them to name the office or know the right vocabulary. Every successful handoff is a potential persistence intervention, connecting a student to real help at the moment that often decides whether they stay enrolled.

How it works

A student types or speaks their situation and CopaMigo reads it for urgency and need, then answers directly or hands off to a human with empathy and no delay. It grew out of my student-success committee work, and language access is built into how it receives and answers a student rather than bolted on as a translate button. It is an early prototype in testing, being dialed in with department input before any wider rollout.
