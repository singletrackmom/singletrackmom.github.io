CopaMigo

Case study · AI Tools & Strategy

OverviewLive toolEmbedded exampleStaff knowledge builderPRD

A multilingual routing tool that helps students find the right campus service, even when they do not know what to call what they need. It answers in the student’s own language, typed or spoken.

Students often stop asking for help because they cannot name the office or service they need. CopaMigo lets a student describe their situation in plain language and routes them to the right place with a warm, human handoff.

Goal, audience, and process

Goal
Connect students to the right campus service quickly, lowering the barrier of not knowing where to start.
Audience
GCC students, answered in their own language, typed or spoken, developed with campus Student Services.
Process
Plain-language description in, a routed, warm handoff card out, built on verified campus information.

How it works

  1. A student describes their situation in plain language, in English or one of several built-in languages.
  2. CopaMigo routes them to the right campus service across 14 service modules, using more than 100 verified GCC links so the information is current, not generic.
  3. It returns a warm handoff card with the contact, hours, location, and exactly what to ask for, and leads with empathy, including a warm handoff for students in crisis.

Privacy by design

CopaMigo collects no personally identifiable information. That is deliberate: the district declined a vendor AI add-on specifically because it would collect student data, so CopaMigo is built to connect students to help without gathering their data.

Why it matters

It improves on a generic website chatbot by meeting students who do not know what to call the help they need, connecting them to the right person rather than returning text, and keeping the information current and trustworthy. The aim is to take routine, repetitive questions off student-services staff so they can spend their time on the individual problems and human connection only a person can provide.

Status: CopaMigo is an early prototype in testing. I am evaluating it with student-services colleagues to understand honestly whether a no-data tool could serve students better than the current automated chatbot. This is an exploration toward what serves students best, not a decision to adopt.
