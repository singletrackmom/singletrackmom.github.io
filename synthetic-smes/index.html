Building a course with synthetic SMEs

League for Innovation capstone · AI course design

A modern, standards-driven way to build an online course: a panel of AI agents cast as a subject-matter expert and an instructional designer draft it from vetted open resources, a human designer directs, and the faculty member who will teach it finishes and owns it. The proof is a complete graduate data-science course, built to standard and handed off with directions.

The course → Instructor prep kit → The case for synthetic SMEs → The course tutor (Gem) →

Goal

Demonstrate that a rigorous online course can be built the way the field is starting to build them, with AI agents doing the tireless first-draft work and a human always closing the loop, without cutting a single corner on standards. I deliberately chose a graduate subject outside my own expertise, data science, so I could not judge the course by my own content knowledge. If it turned out good, only the method and the standards could have made it good, not me knowing the material. That is the honest test.

Audience

Learning designers and teaching-and-learning centers moving into AI-assisted course development, and anyone evaluating whether that can be done responsibly and to standard. It is also a working answer to the field’s live worry, that AI course-building means shipping unreviewed content, by keeping a named human subject-matter expert accountable at the end.

Technology

  • A panel of AI agents (a subject-matter expert and an instructional designer) packaged as a reusable skill, the equivalent in Google’s Gemini environment is a Gem.
  • A course-grounded Gemini Gem as the student tutor, loaded only with the course’s own materials.
  • Open educational resources plus interactive, slider-driven explorables (MLU-Explain, TensorFlow Playground, and others), correctly licensed.
  • Eight simulation-based assessments with a live defense, engineered to resist generative substitution.
  • Eight instructor slide decks with speaker-note scripts; a full instructor prep kit.
  • Static HTML and CSS. Designed to a recognized quality standard (OSCQR), to Regular and Substantive Interaction, and to WCAG 2.1 AA.

Status

My League for Innovation capstone: a demonstration and a working method. The course is a reviewed, standards-aligned draft; the build skill is a prototype; and by design nothing reaches a student until a human subject-matter expert has signed off. The related course-audit tool, Dial Your Course, is a separate product for the different setting where faculty build their own courses.
