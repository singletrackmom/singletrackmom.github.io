What the role is

From ASU’s own jobs page, these part-time pool positions are teaching positions in which the associate will “teach one or two courses per semester under the guidance of a course/program coordinator,” who provides the generic syllabi and assignment guidelines.

This is adjunct teaching, not course building. The master course already exists. Lead with teaching, presence, feedback and student support. Ask about the design line, do not assume it.

Classification and pay

  • ASU classifies Faculty Associates and Academic Associates as Variable employment, capped at 0.40 FTE or less. 0.41 to 0.48 needs Provost approval. 0.50 and above must use a different title.
  • Postings for these roles state non-benefits eligible, fixed-term appointment.
  • Per-course pay is not published anywhere findable. Ask in the interview, or at offer.

Sources: ASU HR Employment Manual Definitions · MLFC jobs page

What it actually pays, researched 22 September 2026

Most likely for one 3-credit graduate course as Faculty Associate, instructor of record: $4,500 to $6,000, centred near $4,500 to $5,000. ASU does not publish a central rate. This band comes from ASU’s own live postings in another college.

What ASU publishes

  • “$1,500 to $2,000 per credit hour, depending on the component, over the length of the session.” ASU Herberger Institute, Graphic Design Faculty Associate posting, live for the 2026 to 27 cycle. The same language appears on their Architecture posting. Posting
  • “$1,500 per credit hour over the length of the session.” ASU FIDM Faculty Associate posting, 2024.
  • The rate is set by the unit, not centrally. ASU Academic Personnel’s summer pay guidelines say Faculty Associates should receive “the standard unit FA rate.” So Herberger’s band is evidence of the ASU-wide range, not a guarantee of what MLFC pays.
  • MLFC does not publish its rate. Every MLFC posting says only “Salary is based upon qualifications and assigned teaching load.” That asymmetry is itself leverage.

The title distinction, and it matters a lot

Faculty Associate is instructor of record, priced per credit hour. MLFC postings describe the FA as lead instructor for online courses, guiding co-instructors.

Academic Associate, in MLFC’s own postings, is co-teaching under a lead instructor: grading, managing Canvas, moderating discussion, office hours, “rather than serving as instructor of record.” ASU’s School of Life Sciences prices its Academic Associate role at $25 to $30 an hour, which over a 7.5-week course could land well under $3,000.

Establish which title and which role before discussing money. This is the single most consequential thing.

FTE and session length, official

  • One 3-credit course, 15-week Session C: no more than 0.20 FTE.
  • One 3-credit course, 7.5-week Session A or B: no more than 0.40 FTE.
  • No assignments between 0.40 and 0.50 FTE in an academic-year semester.
  • No pay premium for the compressed session is documented anywhere. The posting language is “per credit hour, over the length of the session,” which reads as the same money paid across a shorter window. The 7.5-week course is not paid double, it is worked at double intensity for half as long. A fair question to ask.

ASU ACA FAQs, part-time teaching

Benchmarks

  • National median part-time faculty pay: $3,121 per course section, AAUP preliminary 2025 to 26 survey, about 555 institutions reporting. ASU’s documented band is above the national average, so do not go in assuming a lowball by sector standards.
  • Only 32.7 percent of reporting institutions contribute to retirement for part-time faculty, and 30.6 percent to medical premiums. Non-benefits-eligible is the norm here, not an outlier.
  • NAU publishes $2,000 per credit hour, but that is for tenured faculty teaching overload, not adjuncts. Still useful: it is the top of ASU’s band.
  • No per-student or enrollment-scaled component exists. MLFC postings say enrollment can cancel an assignment, not raise the rate. A 45-student section pays the same as a 20-student one.

How to ask without undercutting yourself

Do not name a number first, and do not ask what it pays. Make them state their own published structure.

  • “Can you confirm whether this appointment is Faculty Associate as instructor of record, or Academic Associate supporting a lead instructor? And is it Session A or B at 0.40 FTE, or Session C at 0.20?”
  • “I have seen ASU postings state Faculty Associate assignments are paid $1,500 to $2,000 per credit hour depending on the component. For a 3-credit graduate course in the compressed 7.5-week format, where does this one fall in that band, and what determines the component?”
  • “Does the rate differ for a 7.5-week session versus 15 weeks?”
  • “Is there separate compensation for course development, or is this teaching an existing master course shell?”
  • “What is the enrollment cap, and what happens if the section runs large?”

Do not quote Glassdoor. Those figures are self-reported annual totals across several courses, and naming one invites them to anchor a single course at a fraction of it.

Courses an associate may be assigned

The graduate core, any of which a coordinator could hand over:

  • LDT 501 Foundations of Learning Design and Technologies
  • LDT 502 Design and Development of Instruction
  • LDT 503 Design of Effective Communications
  • LDT 504 eLearning Design and Development (the 2025 to 26 handbook calls it Modalities of Learning)
  • LDT 506 Evaluation of Learning Systems
  • LDT 508 Design of Accessible and Inclusive Digital Learning (built by Steve Salik)
  • LDT 513 Needs Assessment for Learning Design
  • LDT 523 Design Principles of Online and Blended Learning (the handbook calls it Issues in Online and Distance Education)
  • LDT 593 Applied Project, the culminating course, B or higher required

Undergraduate and adjacent: LDT 301 Foundations of Instructional Design, LDT 320 Digital Learning Theories, LDT 330 Design of Digital Learning, LDT 410 Assessment and Evaluation for Digital Learning, LDT 484 Internship, LDT 511 Emerging Trends and Technologies for Learning Design, LDT 598 Special Topics.

Course titles differ between ASU Degree Search and the degree handbook, and the handbook itself says titles are subject to change. Do not assert either title too firmly.

What to ask about the terms

  • What is the per-course rate, and is it the same for a first-time associate?
  • How many sections per session, and which courses would you likely assign?
  • What is the onboarding and training process for a new associate?
  • How much latitude does an associate have inside the master course?
  • Is there any path from teaching into the design or revision work?
