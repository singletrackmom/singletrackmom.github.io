## What the role is

From ASU's own jobs page, these part-time pool positions are described as teaching positions where the associate will **"teach one or two courses per semester under the guidance of a course/program coordinator,"** who provides the generic syllabi and assignment guidelines.

**So this is adjunct teaching, not course building.** The master course already exists. This matters for how to pitch: the instinct to lead with redesign and learning-experience work is the wrong opening here. Lead with teaching, presence, feedback and student support. Ask about the design line, do not assume it.

## The program

**MEd in Learning Design and Technologies** (degree code EDTECHMED), 100% online through ASU Online. 30 credit hours, 10 courses, **7.5-week sessions**.

| Course | Title |
|---|---|
| LDT 501 | Foundations of Learning Design and Technologies |
| LDT 502 | Design and Development of Instruction |
| LDT 503 | Design of Effective Communications |
| LDT 504 | eLearning Design and Development |
| LDT 506 | Evaluation of Learning Systems |
| LDT 508 | Design of Accessible and Inclusive Digital Learning |
| LDT 513 | Needs Assessment for Learning Design |
| LDT 523 | Design Principles of Online and Blended Learning |
| LDT 593 | Applied Project (culminating, needs a B or higher) |

Undergraduate and adjacent: LDT 301 (Foundations of Instructional Design), LDT 320 (Digital Learning Theories), LDT 511 (Emerging Trends in Technology and Learning Design), LDT 598 (Special Topics).

**Who it serves:** explicitly broader than K-12. ASU names K-12, higher education, corporate, healthcare, nonprofit and government. Corporate L&D is a first-class audience.

**AI in the program:** the program's student-facing site says students **"work with AI as both a creative partner and a personal mentor,"** integrated across coursework and portfolio. That connects to Salik's AI mentors project. The college also runs everything through ASU's **Principled Innovation** framework.

**LDT 593 detail:** students partner with an external organization to design, develop and evaluate a real instructional program. Framed as the move from student to practitioner.
