~4.5 hrs · US-6 / US-50 westUpper Lehman Creek CG, NPS · Site 5⛅ loading…

Head south to US-6 and pick up US-50 west through central Nevada — the "Loneliest Road in America." Wide empty skies, distant mountain ranges, almost no traffic. Great Basin National Park is a genuinely underrated gem right on the Nevada-Utah border. Upper Lehman Creek sits at 7,500 ft on Wheeler Peak Scenic Drive — three miles up from the visitor center, closer to the alpine action.

~6 hrs · US-50 west → US-395 north → I-80 westDonner Memorial SP · Ridge Site 024⛅ loading…

Continue west on US-50 across central Nevada — the Loneliest Road in America — through Eureka, Austin, and Fallon. At Carson City pick up US-395 north, then I-80 west over Donner Pass to Truckee. Yosemite — The Waterfall Hike
Mist Trail to Vernal Fall · classic Yosemite
No drivingShuttle to Happy Isles — Stop 16⛅ loading…

The one everyone comes for. Take the shuttle from the valley to Happy Isles and hit the Mist Trail. The climb to the top of Vernal Fall involves steep granite steps cut directly into the cliff face and you'll get completely soaked from the spray — in the best possible way. Ponchos or rain gear are not optional in early June. The view from the top of the fall is staggering.

★ Today's Hike
Mist Trail to Top of Vernal Fall
2.4 miles RT · moderate · 1,000 ft gain · steep granite steps + heavy mist spray · bring ponchos · trailhead at Happy Isles (Shuttle Stop 16) · allow 3–4 hours

Afternoon: recover, swim in the Merced River below the valley, wander Yosemite Village. El Capitan Meadow at dusk is worth the drive back — the wall turns golden.

Roadside Find — Drive Out via Tunnel View
Tunnel View — the iconic Ansel Adams shot
On Wawona Rd just outside the Wawona Tunnel · El Cap, Bridalveil Fall, Half Dome all in one frame · best light at sunset · 2 min stop

Yosemite — Glacier Point Road Day
Last day in the park · two short hikes, one massive view
Easy day · short hikesCrane Flat (last night)⛅ loading…

Up Glacier Point Road for what may be the best concentration of short, high-payoff hikes in the entire Sierra. Two trailheads off the same road get you two completely different perspectives on the valley — and then Glacier Point itself for the full Half Dome / Nevada Fall / Vernal Fall panorama. Pack a lunch and make a day of it.

★ Today's Hike — both from same trailhead
Sentinel Dome + Taft Point
2.2 mi RT each · easy-moderate · share the parking lot off Glacier Point Rd · Sentinel = 360° summit view, Taft = stand on the cliff edge with 3,000 ft of air below your feet · do both, allow 4 hours
Roadside Find — End of the Road
Glacier Point Viewpoint
15 min from the parking lot to the lookout · Half Dome straight across, Vernal + Nevada Fall pouring down, valley floor 3,200 ft below · sunset light is the move if you can swing it
⚠ On the climbing plan
YMS Yosemite climb dropped — going to Bishop instead

Yosemite is all trad/crack climbing — not sport. Owens River Gorge near Bishop (Eastern Sierra, on US-395) has 400+ bolted sport routes and is what the kid actually wants. Plan a day at Owens on the return trip — local guides include Sierra Mountain Guides and Sierra Mountain Center. Budget kept at $350 for that day.
