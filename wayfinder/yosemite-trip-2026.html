Yosemite — The Waterfall Hike
Mist Trail to Vernal Fall · classic Yosemite
No drivingShuttle to Happy Isles — Stop 16⛅ loading…

The one everyone comes for. Take the shuttle from the valley to Happy Isles and hit the Mist Trail. The climb to the top of Vernal Fall involves steep granite steps cut directly into the cliff face and you'll get completely soaked from the spray — in the best possible way. Ponchos or rain gear are not optional in early June. The view from the top of the fall is staggering.

★ Today's Hike
Mist Trail to Top of Vernal Fall · 🗺 Map
2.4 miles RT · moderate · 1,000 ft gain · steep granite steps + heavy mist spray · bring ponchos · trailhead at Happy Isles (Shuttle Stop 16) · allow 3–4 hours

Afternoon: recover, swim in the Merced River below the valley, wander Yosemite Village. El Capitan Meadow at dusk is worth the drive back — the wall turns golden.

🪨 Bouldering — Yosemite Valley, at the base of the giants
Camp 4 Boulders + El Cap Boulders + Swan Slab · 🗺 Map
Yosemite Valley is the historic heart of American bouldering. Camp 4 boulders — the original training ground for Yosemite's big-wall era, problems V0–V13, polished granite right at the campground. El Cap Boulders — sit at the very base of El Cap and pull on the same rock the wall climbers are on, easy to mid-range problems, kid can boulder while watching pros 2,000 ft overhead. Swan Slab boulders across from Yosemite Lodge — easier moderates and slab problems, good for warm-up. All free, all walk-up. Bring a pad and shoes.
🔭 Watch the Wall Climbers — El Capitan Meadow
El Capitan Meadow · 🗺 Map — bring binoculars
The big lawn directly across from El Cap on Northside Drive. In early June there will be portaledges visible on The Nose and Salathé. Park rangers often set up spotting scopes around midday. Combine with the El Cap Boulders 5 min away — boulder a little, watch a little, repeat. Pack a tailgate lunch.

Heat warning: Atlatl Rock sits at 2,000 ft in open desert. Daytime highs 95–105°F, overnight lows 65–78°F. Bring extra water (5+ gal). Pre-cool the van during the drive, run the DC fans overnight, and plan the Fire Wave / Atlatl petroglyph hike for sunrise. Pre-trip prep: freeze a stack of soft ice packs at home and pack them in the Dometic 12V cooler/freezer before leaving — extra cold mass = the compressor cycles less in the desert heat and the food stays cold even if the battery dips.
