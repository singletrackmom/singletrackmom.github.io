Wayfinder

Personal Projects · Case study

OverviewLive toolTrip & campground builderPRD

A road-trip planner that puts the scattered logistics of a long drive on one page, with a builder that makes a version for any trip.

Goal

A long drive scatters its logistics across a dozen tabs: weather, fuel, road clearance, campsites, backup plans. The goal is one calm page that holds all of it and still works late in the day, with no signal, on the road.

Audience

Road-trippers and van travelers who want a real plan without living in a spreadsheet. It started as the plan for one trip, then grew a builder so the same structure works for someone else’s trip.

Process

One full van trip came first, planned end to end as the working example: live weather, fire-restriction links, a fuel strategy, road clearance for the van, and mapped overnight stops with a “sleep where you are” rule. The builder came second. It takes a traveler’s interests and produces a plan in the same format.

What Is in It

  • Trip planner. One page for the whole drive: weather, fuel, road clearance, overnight stops and backup plans. Open the planner
  • Campground finder. A search form for candidate sites, plus a scheduled watcher that checks named campgrounds for cancellations and reports when a site opens. Open the trip and campground builder
  • Packing list. A checklist for one trip that keeps its state in the browser, so what is packed stays checked. Open the packing list

Technology

  • A self-contained HTML planner plus a static builder page: vanilla JavaScript, no backend.
  • An inline SVG route map, no tile server and no map token, so it renders offline.
  • Keyless Open·Meteo weather, fetched live in the browser.
  • localStorage for packing-list state.
  • A scheduled agent for the campground cancellation watcher.
  • Hosted on GitHub Pages.

Outcomes

One full van trip is planned end to end on the page. Nothing in it needs a paid map or weather service, so it keeps working without an account or a key.

Status

A working prototype in personal use. The planner and the builder are live. The campground watcher ran daily ahead of past trips and is switched off between them. There is no account and no server: trip and packing details stay in the browser on the traveler’s own device.
