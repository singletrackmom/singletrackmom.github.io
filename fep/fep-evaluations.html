Student Evaluation Summary

Faculty Evaluation Plan · Michelle Blomberg · Spring 2026

The FEP requires student evaluation from at least one class. This page presents two complete evaluations in full, nothing omitted. The first is the official Maricopa department course evaluation for my Commercial Art Internship (AVC 297AC). The second is my own anonymous in-course survey for Graphic Design II (AVC 182), which I run because response rates on the emailed department survey are low; I offer extra credit in every class for completing an evaluation, either the department’s or mine. Every rating item and every written comment for both classes is shown below exactly as submitted.
