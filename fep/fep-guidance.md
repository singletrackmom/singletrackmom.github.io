# FEP Section Guidance (official prompts, captured from the GCC FEP Canvas site)

Reference for writing `fep-2026.md`. Each narrative section should ANSWER its prompt below. The FEP is reflective: what I did, what I learned, where I am going. Not a list (the list lives in the appendix). Explain to a reviewer who is NOT in my field.

## Required Area, Teaching, Learning, and/or Service

**Teaching:** Preview the objective or goal. Show how I teach a "unit." Review the takeaway. Show the teaching-learning process is carried out systematically. Demonstrate how I teach students to connect theories and concepts to actual practice. Give examples of applied skills in the classroom. Three musts: (1) identify the thing being shown, (2) use clear language to explain the parts of my teaching strategy, (3) explain it so a non-expert understands what is great about my teaching.

**Learning (demonstration of learning):** What goals/objectives did I set at the start of the three-year cycle? Did I meet them? What did I learn? Where am I going next? Acceptable demonstrations include oral presentations, video documentaries/multimedia, works of art, online publications/websites/blogs, photography galleries, physical products, portfolios, and summary slideshows. Connect to real practice and community problems.

## Required Area, Course Assessment and/or Program Development/Revision

Use assessment tools to show student progress AND my own growth across the cycle. Address: formative tools I designed; what guided my mastery and where I focused on student/self needs; summative tools that gauged outcomes; what I learned about my courses/program and how I revised; objective standards (national standards, universal design) I measured my course/program/self against. Ask: What courses did I assess? Was I a team lead for the assessment? What assessment training did I acquire? What new assessment approaches did I employ?

## Required Area, Governance and/or Committee Participation (College and/or District)

Document the committee name, my role, and how I assisted. Show examples: agendas, minutes, events, activities, supporting links. Explain my communication, participation, leadership, and where I took initiative. Note I know the chair, the committee's responsibilities, GCC practices/policies, the reporting procedure, and that I set realistic attainable goals and give recognition to others. Committees succeed through the right people, a mission, good leadership, and good staff work.

## Elective Area, Professional Development

Learning to earn/maintain credentials: formal coursework, degrees, conferences, and informal learning situated in practice (consultation, coaching, communities of practice, mentoring, reflective supervision). Ideally includes an evaluative stage. Examples: attend conferences, participate in workshops, complete CE/CPE, microlearning, shadow a colleague, read a field book. Show how it expands knowledge, builds credibility, networks, and keeps me current on trends, and how it benefits GCC.

## Elective Area, Acquisition of New Skills

Hard skills (technical, e.g., programming, data analysis) and soft skills (collaboration, communication, work management). Show how each NEW skill benefits my teaching, my students, and my growth. Microlearning and mentorship are valid paths. Take advantage of GCC training; find a mentor.

## Optional, Enhancement of Diversity (DEI elective)

Intentionally address diversity, equity, inclusion in course content from the position of teacher. How did I collaborate to build inclusive structures that remove barriers and encourage all students to participate? Textbook/material selection so students feel included; working with Disability Resources & Services (DRS) so students of differing abilities can complete the course; collaborating with Student Services for students facing financial challenges.

## Optional, College-Level Assessment of Learning Outcomes (ILO elective/related)

Map work to GCC's Institutional Learning Outcomes (adopted Fall 2017): Communication (oral, written, visual), Thinking (critical, creative), Quantitative, Well-Being, Diversity, Information Literacy, and **Career** (clarification of career goals + workplace hard and soft skills, via the AIM process: Assessment, Investigation, Management). NOTE: the Career ILO maps almost exactly onto Render's structure, strong related-area option.

## Optional, Service to the Community / College

List committees, clubs, events served. Find evidence (minutes, photos, reports, results). Reflect: What was my contribution? What major themes ran through the work? Why did I enjoy it? Why continue? How is this service a passion of mine as a lifelong learner? Service guidance also: identify GCC's primary concerns and address them; show communication skills; show problem-solving with a team/committee/students; land on a win-win; show how I reached across departments, disciplines, or colleges.

## Related Areas (optional add-ons)

Beyond the three Required + two Elective, Residential Faculty may add Related Areas to show full professional involvement (e.g., Program Coordination, Mentoring Students, Fine Arts Instructor/Artist, Other customized to role).

## The FEP TEAM (RFA Article 18.3)

The FEP is reviewed by a team the faculty member builds and leads (you are the lead/coach, not just collecting signatures):
- **The faculty member (Michelle)** directs and is the active participant, carries the major responsibility for gathering info and completing the plan.
- **A faculty peer** chosen by Michelle, may be outside her discipline, department, or college.
- **A third person** chosen by Michelle from: Department/Division chair, another appropriate professional, another faculty member, administrator, staff developer, advisory committee, alumni, or college committee members.
- **Students (or service-area recipients)** always provide input on teaching/service via a customizable questionnaire or other instrument.
The team reviews the documents and discusses them with Michelle; deeper teams discuss didactics and innovations, not just schedules and materials. Goal is demonstrating added value to GCC and improving performance, not just completing the form.
