# Walkthrough video rollout

**Goal.** Put a short video on every project that is load-bearing for a job search, without turning the portfolio into 19 mediocre videos nobody finishes.

**Audience.** Hiring committees (Mines OLED first), instructional designers, district colleagues, the ARC.

**Process.** Three tiers. Tier 1 gets a video. Tier 2 gets a video if there is time. Tier 3 never gets one, because there is nothing to watch, and those pages get better text instead.

---

## The rule for deciding

A project earns a video when **watching it beats reading about it**. A tool you can see run earns one. A study, a program, or a document does not, because a still image and a good overview page say the same thing faster.

19 projects. Seven earn a video. Twelve do not, and saying so is a decision, not a shortfall.

## Tier 3 · No video. Better text instead.

**Agentic & personal builds** · Wayfinder, Soar, Focus, Devan, Summer Work, Find Your Flow
**Learning design** · Design history, taught by students
**Experiential learning** · The Traveler, Campus Cares Hub, Murals & client projects, Internship program, Rough Cut

For the experiential learning group specifically: the fix is **more real examples in the text**, not video. Name the actual clients. Name the actual murals. Say how many students, what they shipped, where it went. Concrete nouns do the work a video would have done, and they are searchable, which a video is not.

## The five beats · every video, same shape

1. **Cold open.** Do the thing on camera in the first ten seconds. No introduction, no logo, no "hi, I'm Michelle."
2. **The problem.** Two sentences. No throat-clearing.
3. **Two features.** The two that would make a skeptic sit up. Not five.
4. **The limits.** What it will not do, and what it refuses to guess. This is what makes the rest credible.
5. **Status and the ask.** What it actually is right now, and what you want from the viewer.

## Rules

- Say the number, then show the number. Never the reverse.
- No claims about other products or what the market does or does not have.
- State prototype status once, plainly, at the end. Do not apologize for it.
- If something misfires on camera, keep it and say so.
- Video sits **below the fold** on the overview page, behind the existing cover image as a poster frame. The screenshot stays. It is what earns the click that the video then rewards.
