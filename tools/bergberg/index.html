Let Claude Do the Website Work

This is the biggest job in the whole plan, and you don’t have to do it by hand.

  1. Open Chrome on the computer that has the Claude extension.
  2. Log in to Squarespace (bergbergphotography.com).
  3. Open Claude, paste the prompt below, and stay nearby. Claude asks you to say yes before it saves anything.
I’m logged in to my Squarespace site, bergbergphotography.com. Please help with these jobs, one at a time, and show me each change before you save it. 1. On the Vendors/Venues page, remove Right as Rayne Events, Erica Whatley Events, Ivory Events and Sweetie Pie Weddings & Events, and add Exquisite Events & Productions (exquisiteeventsandproductions.com) under Planners. 2. Remove Rhize Mountain Retreats from the venue list and from the venue links at the top of the blog. Keep the Rhize blog post. 3. Starting with the newest Evergreen Lake House post, replace alt text that is empty or just a file name with one plain sentence naming what is in the photo, the venue and the town. Give each post its own SEO title and SEO description naming the couple, venue and town. Do three posts, then stop and ask me if I want more. 4. Open my ShootProof Weddings list (bergbergphotography.shootproof.com/category/109585) and compare it with my blog. List the weddings that have no blog post yet, newest first. Skip the August 15, 2025 Baldoria on the Water wedding; that couple’s contract says no blog post. Then help me post them one at a time, starting with the late-October Evergreen Lake House wedding. Ask me for the vendors, then draft each post with a title naming the couple, venue and town, about 500 words, file names and alt text, and show me before you publish. 5. Fix the broken links listed in “Broken Links on Your Website” at michelleblomberg.com/bergberg/seo.html. Show me each fix first. 6. Make my short blog posts longer, five per session, using the list and the instructions in “Make Short Posts Longer” at michelleblomberg.com/bergberg/blog.html. Look at each post’s photos, keep my words, and show me each new version before you save it.

How couples find you

  1. Someone tells them. A venue, planner, caterer or past couple says your name. It works when those people see you often.
  2. They search. They type “Evergreen Lake House wedding photographer” into Google or ask ChatGPT. It works when your website and other sites say your name next to the venue.

Both are free. Both come back when you show up every week.
