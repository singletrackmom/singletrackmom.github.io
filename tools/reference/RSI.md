# Regular and Substantive Interaction (RSI)

Grounding for the RSI writer. RSI cannot be read from a course package, so the tool generates a drop-in interaction plan + statement the instructor edits and pastes in, checked against this material.

## Why it matters

RSI (34 CFR 600.2, effective July 1, 2021) is what legally separates **distance education** from **correspondence**, which protects Title IV financial-aid eligibility. It must be **instructor-initiated**. Auto-graded quizzes and "post if you have questions" do not count.

## The two required halves

**Substantive:** engaging students in teaching, learning, and assessment consistent with the course content, including **at least two of these five** instructor activities:
1. Providing direct instruction.
2. Assessing or providing feedback on a student's coursework.
3. Providing information or responding to questions about the content.
4. Facilitating a group discussion on course content/competency.
5. Another instructional activity approved by the institution or accreditor.

**Regular:** the institution provides the *opportunity* for substantive interaction on a **predictable and scheduled** basis commensurate with the course, **and** the instructor **monitors** engagement and proactively/promptly reaches out when a student needs it.

## Documentable practices the RSI writer can offer (each instructor-initiated, scheduled, leaves a record)

- Weekly instructor-authored announcement that **teaches**, not just administers (direct instruction, #1).
- Substantive, personalized feedback on graded work on a stated schedule (#2).
- Instructor-facilitated discussion where the instructor synthesizes and responds, not just grades presence (#4).
- Scheduled synchronous or recorded mini-lectures / office hours tied to content (#1).
- Proactive outreach triggered by monitoring, stated as policy: "I contact students who have not submitted or engaged in X days." Use Canvas New Analytics **by hand**. No student-data bot (stays inside the no-PII line).
- A published **response-time commitment** and interaction schedule in the syllabus. This is the documentable artifact reviewers look for.
