What it checks

District required elements (MCCCD Regulation 3.6)

  • College name, campus or site
  • Instructor name and contact information
  • Course title, prefix, number, and section
  • Academic term and year
  • MCCCD course description or overview
  • Course format (online, hybrid, face-to-face)
  • Delivery modes for course materials
  • Credit hours
  • Contact hours and out-of-class expectations
  • Link to the MCCCD Official Course Competencies
  • Grading standards and practices
  • Attendance requirements
  • Required and recommended texts and technologies
  • Student responsibility for syllabus information
  • Student responsibility for college policies
  • Availability of accommodation services
  • Third-party tools, LTI, proctoring, plagiarism disclosures
  • Subject-to-change statement
  • Instructor communication response-time expectation
  • Disability services statement or link
  • Title IX sexual harassment statement or link

Any additional requirements set by your program are included here automatically.
