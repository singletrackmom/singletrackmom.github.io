Spring options near your classes

The goal is cheap, close and decent: aim for about $1,400 a month or less, with utilities included where you can get them. These are ranked by best value, meaning price, what’s included and distance together. The pricey student towers and places where you pay every utility were left off. The bars show scooter time to the Moby B Complex (Health and Exercise Science), the Biology Building, where you work, and Academic Village (Honors). Times are estimates at about 12 mph plus a couple of minutes to park, and red means more than 10 minutes. Most are normal leases, so ask each one for January to May, or a lease takeover, before you visit.

Keep searching

  • CSU RentalSearch subleases. Sign in with your CSU eID to see student subleases. Staff review every post, so scams are rare. This is the best place to find students leaving for spring.
  • CSU RentalSearch apartments, for whole studios and 1-bedrooms with walk times built in.
  • Craigslist sublets near campus. Never pay a deposit before you’ve seen the place in person.
  • Off-Campus Life, in Lory Student Center 281. They keep a list of short-term housing and help you check a lease.

Before you sign

  • Get the exact end date in writing, and ask what it costs if you leave early.
  • Ask what’s included: internet, heat, electric, water, parking and laundry.
  • Find out whether it’s furnished. For five months, furnished saves you buying and moving furniture.
  • Ask where you can keep and charge your scooter safely, and whether a locked bike room or garage is an option.
  • Check that the person subleasing to you has the landlord’s OK in writing, so you aren’t on the hook if they skip rent.

Thinking about transferring?

If another school is on your mind, apply now. Transfer spots for next year fill up, so earlier is better. Applying doesn’t commit you to anything, and it keeps the option open while you decide.

Colorado option

CU Boulder, Integrative Physiology

The second-largest major at CU Boulder, built around how the human body works in health and disease, with a pre-PA track and pre-health advising. He’d keep Colorado in-state tuition with no residency paperwork. The spring deadline has passed. For fall 2027, apply by March 15 for an early decision or June 1 at the latest.

Arizona: pre-health majors

University of Arizona, Physiology and Medical Sciences

The only undergraduate physiology degree in the country taught inside a medical school, the UA College of Medicine in Tucson. It has a Medical Sciences emphasis for future PAs and an Exercise & Extreme Physiology emphasis that’s close to sports medicine. UA also has a BS in Emergency Medical Services, which fits your EMT work, and it’s opening its own PA master’s program. Transfer deadlines are December 1 for spring and June 1 for fall. Resident tuition and fees stay frozen at $13,906 for fall 2027.

Arizona State, Medical Studies or Kinesiology

ASU’s BS in Medical Studies is its pre-med degree, built to cover the prerequisites for medical and PA school, with concentrations in biomedical sciences and physiology. ASU also has a BS in Kinesiology, the closest match to sports medicine. ASU admits transfers on a rolling basis, and the Medical Studies application already lists spring 2027 starts.

Getting in-state tuition in Arizona

  • Arizona’s universities follow one rule for students who are dependents. You can get in-state tuition if a parent lives permanently in Arizona and could claim you as a dependent on their taxes, and you also make Arizona your home.
  • Your dad living in Arizona is what counts, not a parent who only works there. You’d also need to move your own home base to Arizona, with things like an Arizona driver’s license and address, and file a petition with proof.
  • If the parents involved are married, both have to live in Arizona for this exception. Check the details with the residency office before you count on in-state rates.
