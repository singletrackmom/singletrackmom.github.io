Summer Work Board

A private work board, refreshed by a small AI agent.

Product Requirements · one of the family job-search agents · Michelle B.

Goal

Find Devan work he can start right away near Golden, ranked by pay, after his summer job fell through, plus a few remote options and a winter watch list for the December and January break.

Audience

Devan, looking for seasonal and part-time work close to home (and open to remote). Pay matters and time is short, so the board leads with what he can begin this week.

Process

  1. Sweep local employers’ own pages for openings he can start now; never a job board or a login page.
  2. Verify each posting and re-check prior leads; rank everything by pay.
  3. Group it: start-now work first, then remote work-from-home, then a winter high-pay watch list.
  4. Where a place has no careers page, point to its real contact form instead.
Every link goes straight to the company, never a job board or login. Pay labels are confirmed at the source where possible and change often.
