Goal

Next Steps turns a long, loosely mapped path to a graduate health-professions program into clear next actions. Graduate programs in this field expect hundreds to thousands of hours of hands-on patient care, shadowing and specific prerequisites, and the information lives on dozens of sites with their own rules and deadlines. A missed deadline can close a door for a whole year. The board puts every option the student can use in one place, verified at the source, with the nearest deadline first.

Audience

One university student on a pre-health path who also wants to study abroad. Research on guided pathways shows that students progress further when the route is mapped and the next step is explicit, so the board leads with what is due soonest. It is a private, single-user build tailored to one set of goals, not a general feed.

Process

The board is one living page rebuilt in place at the same link, never archived. A scheduled agent runs twice a week from a written rules file.

SectionWhat it holds
Deadlines coming upEvery verified deadline in the next 90 days across all sections, soonest first.
Paid patient careSummer roles that build hands-on patient-care hours, nearby first, with pay, dates, which credentials are accepted and whether the work counts as direct patient care.
Shadowing and clinical programsShadowing programs, summer clinical research roles and pre-health pipeline programs, marked paid or unpaid.
ScholarshipsPrivate and outside awards, sorted by deadline, with amount, eligibility and whether each renews.
Two paths at a glanceA table comparing two graduate health-professions paths: degree, prerequisites, expected hours, tests and Colorado programs.
Study abroadUniversity programs by term and the national awards that fund them, noting which fit around a summer patient-care job.

Each run opens every link, confirms the page is live and the deadline current, drops anything past, carries forward everything still open, and tags new finds and anything closing within 14 days. Links go only to the employer, program, university or funder’s own page, never an aggregator. Anything it cannot confirm says so on the card. It links once to the companion gap-year planner, which holds the map and savings plan.

Privacy

The board collects nothing. The pages are static, with no forms, logins or tracking, and they are noindexed and unlinked. No personal data is stored or transmitted.

Status

A personal build for family, in use twice a week. It began as a summer work board, became a study abroad board, and was reshaped into a pre-health pathway board as the goal sharpened. The agent, rules and link carried over each time. It is not a pilot and not headed for rollout.
