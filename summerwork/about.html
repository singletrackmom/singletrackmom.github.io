OverviewBoardPlannerPRD

A private weekly board of study abroad programs, scholarships and deadlines for one university student, with a collapsed section of related work, rebuilt in place by a scheduled agent.

Version
2.0
Status
In use
Owner
Michelle Blomberg
Runs
Weekly

Goal

Help one university student find and pay for study abroad in any term. The problem it solves is scattered information: programs, university scholarships, national awards and deadlines live on a dozen different sites, each with its own eligibility rules, and a missed deadline closes a door for a whole term. The board puts every option he can use in one place, verified at the source, with the nearest deadline first.

Audience

One person, a university student on a pre-health path who wants to study abroad. The board leads with study abroad; work is one small collapsed section of related jobs. It is a private, single-user build tailored to one university and one set of goals, not a general study abroad feed.

Process

The board is one living page rebuilt in place at the same link, never archived, because it should only show what is open now. A scheduled agent runs every Monday morning from a written rules file.

SectionWhat it holds
Deadlines coming upEvery verified application and scholarship deadline in the next 90 days, soonest first.
Education abroadHis university’s programs by term (faculty-led, exchange and partner), with health, science and EMS programs flagged, plus cost, deadline and eligibility.
Paying for itUniversity scholarships and financial aid for study abroad, then national awards such as Gilman, Fund for Education Abroad, Boren and provider scholarships, each with eligibility stated plainly.
How to get startedAdvising and info sessions, fairs, and the application portal.
Related work (collapsed)Camp, outdoor, event and expedition roles, abroad first, with whether a US credential is accepted and whether a visa is needed, plus a watch list of summer camps that post in winter.

Each run opens every link, confirms the page loads and the deadline is current, drops anything past, carries forward everything still live and tags new finds. Links go only to the university, funder or employer’s own page, never an aggregator. Anything it cannot confirm says so on the card. It links once to the companion gap-year planner, which holds the map, savings plan and work-exchange model.

Privacy

The board collects nothing. It is a static page with no forms, logins or tracking, noindexed and unlinked. It shows no last name, student ID, contact details or financial information.

Status

A personal build for family, in weekly use. It began as a summer work board and was reshaped into a study abroad board when his plans changed; the agent, rules and link carried over. It is not a pilot or a product and is not headed for any rollout.
