Summer Work

Agentic & personal build

A rolling agent that finds high-paying, flexible summer gig work, verified live on employers’ own sites and refreshed each morning.

Open the board → How it works →

Goal

Turn a short summer window into real savings without endless searching. Put ready-to-start, higher-than-minimum work in one place, ranked so that fewer hours at better pay beats more hours at low pay.

A daily-refreshing work board that finds well-paid, flexible summer gig work near Golden and the Denver metro, sorted highest pay first. Every lead links straight to a real application, verified live on the employer’s own site, so nothing on the board is stale or made up.

Audience

Devan, for the summer when his planned job fell through. It is the companion piece to the gap-year travel planner: the planner maps the year abroad, this board funds it.

Process

A rolling agent rebuilds the board each morning, ranking openings by pay-per-hour and flexibility, checking each posting is still live at the source, and grouping the fastest-to-start work at the top. It is a static page with no logins, refreshed daily and built to be opened and acted on in a minute.
