Study Abroad

AI Tools and Agents · Case study

OverviewBoardPlannerPRD

A weekly agent and a planner that work together for one college student who wants to study abroad: the board finds programs, scholarships and deadlines, the planner lays out the year, and a small work section helps pay for it.

Goal

Make studying abroad feel reachable and fundable instead of out of reach. Put every real program, scholarship and deadline a student can use in one place, verified at the source, so the next step is always clear: an info session to attend, an application to start, or an award to apply for.

Audience

One university student who wants to study abroad in any term, not only summer, and who is finishing an EMT certification on the way to a health career. Two pieces serve him, and they started as two separate projects before being combined into one.

PieceWhat it doesHow often
The boardDeadlines in the next 90 days, his university’s education abroad programs by term, university and national scholarships, the steps to get started, and a collapsed section of work that uses his EMT certification, abroad firstRebuilt weekly by a scheduled agent
The plannerAn interactive map of home-base countries, a savings plan, and three ways to go for close to free: work exchange, a working-holiday visa, and study abroad for creditA standing guide he opens while planning

Process

Each week the agent checks his university’s own education abroad and scholarship pages, the major national awards, and employers that hire EMTs for camps, outdoor programs and events, in Europe and at home. It opens every link that run, confirms the page loads and the deadline is current, drops anything past, and flags new finds. Anything it cannot confirm says so on the card instead of guessing. The page stays one running board at the same link, so he never has to look for the latest version.

The project changed shape with him. It began as a summer work board when a planned job fell through, and a separate gap-year planner. Once he had steady work and his plans turned to studying abroad in any term, the two were combined: the page now leads with study abroad, and work moved to one small collapsed section aimed at jobs his EMT certification opens.

Technology

  • Scheduled AI agent. Runs weekly from a written rules file, searches the web, and verifies every program, award and posting on its own site before publishing.
  • Static pages. The board and the planner are plain HTML with no logins, forms or tracking, and the board is noindexed and unlinked from search.
  • Leaflet map. The planner’s interactive country map, with filterable country cards for visas, work options, cost of living and study abroad fit.

Status

In production, weekly. The board runs every week and the planner stays in use while the year abroad is being planned.
