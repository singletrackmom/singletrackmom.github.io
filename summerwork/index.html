CSU Education Abroad, Start Here
Steps

CSU’s five steps

  1. Explore: watch the Education Abroad 101 video and search programs in the MyEdAbroad portal.
  2. Meet three people: an education abroad advisor, your academic advisor, and a financial aid advisor for education abroad. Drop-in advising runs Monday to Thursday, 10 to noon and 1 to 4, in Laurel Hall, through Dec 10.
  3. Apply in MyEdAbroad. CSU-run programs charge a $50 application fee and a $200 deposit. Fill out the scholarship application inside it, get or renew your passport, and do the Transfer Credit Form if needed.
  4. Pre-departure guide and orientation, and register for SA 482.
  5. Book your flight.

Pre-health tip from CSU’s Health and Human Sciences page: health professional programs prefer that required admission courses be taken in the US, so plan to take electives and extras abroad, not your core prereqs. Credits transfer only with a C- or better.
