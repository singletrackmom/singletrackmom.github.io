Shadowing and clinical programs

Shadowing does not count as patient care hours, but PA programs like to see it, and the CU anesthesiologist assistant program requires at least 8 hours in the OR. All shadowing is unpaid. You find your own PA or doctor to sponsor you; the hospital handles the paperwork.

New Colorado PA programs

Four accredited programs, all apply through CASPA

  • CU Anschutz CHA/PA (Aurora): 35 months, no set hours, 3.0 GPA, CASPer; last deadline Sept 1
  • Red Rocks Community College (Arvada): 27 months, 2,000 paid healthcare hours, volunteer hours do not count; next deadline Sept 1, 2027

Three of the four count only paid hours, which is why an EMT job beats volunteering.

Study abroad, CSU

The summer trade-off: CSU’s summer health trips run late May into early June, right when camp jobs start. Japan (May 22 to June 7) overlaps Cheley’s June 5 training start; Honduras (May 29 to June 5) just touches it. A spring or fall semester, or a winter break program, keeps your summer free for patient care hours. Health professional schools prefer core prerequisites taken in the US, so take electives abroad.
