Job Search Agent

Student Success Tools · Case study

OverviewPRD

A search agent built from the student’s own goals that confirms every job is still open before showing it.

See a worked example

Goal

A search result is not proof a job exists. Students lose days applying to postings that closed months ago. The goal is a feed of openings that fit the student and are verified live.

Audience

Capstone students starting a first professional search, who run the agent in their own AI account.

Process

The student sets the titles, the location, a pay floor, and what to skip. Before a job reaches the feed, the agent opens the employer’s own careers page and confirms the posting is there. Fit is judged against the posting’s stated requirements, compared with the student’s resume and portfolio.

Status

Prototype, in the fall capstone pilot.
