Render

Section · AI Tools & Strategy · UX Design

A career-services tool that walks a student from no portfolio to a shipped one, in seven phases.

Built for students who need career materials and have no idea where to start. Seven phases, a single HTML file, vanilla JavaScript, and the Sonnet API. Authentication is a first name and nothing else. No personally identifiable information is collected and state lives in the browser, because a tool students are asked to trust should not be collecting them.
