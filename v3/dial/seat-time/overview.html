Seat Time

Overview · AI Tools & Strategy · Learning Design

OverviewPRD

Estimates the real time a module asks of a student, against the credit hours it claims.

Goal

Credit hours imply a time commitment, and nobody measures whether a module actually matches it.

Audience

The instructor building the module, and the reviewer checking the claim.

Process

Rule-based estimation across the module contents, reported against the stated credit hours.

Status

Built and running against a real course.
