OverviewPRD

Student-facing routing for campus services, so a student asking a question in their own words reaches the right office.

1. Summary

Student-facing routing for campus services, so a student asking a question in their own words reaches the right office.

2. Goal

Fourteen modules, multilingual, built on more than a hundred verified college URLs. The problem it addresses is that campus service information is organised the way the institution is organised, not the way a student asks. It collects no student data and no personally identifiable information, which is a hard constraint rather than a feature: the district declined a vendor AI add-on specifically because it would have collected student data.
