Build Your Course

Section · AI Tools & Strategy · Learning Design

Dial Your Course tells you what is wrong. This builds the replacement.

Checking a course and building one are different problems with different trust requirements, and collapsing them into one tool would damage both. The checks are deterministic. Building an assessment, or judging whether an open licence permits what you are about to do, is not. So these two tools use AI, and the human stays in every loop. Nothing is applied automatically: every suggestion arrives as a proposed replacement with four choices, apply, edit first, dismiss with a reason, or defer.

Authentic AssessmentIn buildTakes an assignment a student could hand to a model and proposes a replacement that asks for process evidence instead. OER FinderSpecified, not builtFinds openly licensed material for a module and records the licence that permits the use.

Status

Authentic Assessment is in build and the less finished of the two. OER Finder is specified and not built. Both have been run by hand on a full course, which is where the evidence comes from: a complete fifteen-week graduate data science course built from open educational resources in a field I do not teach, with four original simulations for its assessments and a recorded oral defence carrying the grade.
