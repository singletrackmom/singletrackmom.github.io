Build Your Course

Project · AI Tools & Strategy · Learning Design

OverviewDial Your Course

Dial Your Course tells you what is wrong. This builds the replacement.

Goal

Dial Your Course tells you what is wrong. This builds the replacement. Checking a course and building one are different problems with different trust requirements, and collapsing them into one tool would damage both.

The checks are deterministic: a published standard, a fixed rule, the same answer every time. Building an assessment or judging whether an open licence permits what you are about to do is judgement, so it needs a model. Anything a model writes needs a person to accept it before a student sees it. Separating the two keeps the checking trustworthy and puts the review exactly where it belongs.

Audience

An instructor who now knows what is wrong with a module and has to fix it, which is the point at which most quality processes quietly end. Faculty use the interface, never the underlying method directly.

Process

Nothing is applied automatically. Every suggestion arrives as a proposed replacement with four choices: apply it, edit it first, dismiss it with a reason, or defer it to next semester. Nothing leaves the tool until the before and after have been seen side by side. The dismissal reason matters as much as the acceptance, because it is the record of where the method was wrong.

The same method builds a whole course when there is nothing to start from. A panel of AI agents drafts it, a subject-matter expert, an instructional designer, and a student, and a human subject-matter expert reviews and owns every decision. The panel is not there to be right. It is there to disagree with itself in front of a person who can adjudicate, which is a different and more useful thing.

Tools

Two, both using AI, both gated on human acceptance.

1Authentic Assessment1 of 2 · uses AITakes an assignment a student can finish with a chatbot and returns one they have to perform and defend. 2OER Finder2 of 2 · uses AIFinds openly licensed material for a thin module and verifies the license.

Outcomes

Four things exist because of this method, and they are the reason to believe it rather than a claim about it.

A complete graduate data science course, fifteen weeks, built from open educational resources in a field I do not teach, with four original simulations for its assessments and a recorded oral defence carrying the grade. A hybrid photonics course for a second college, an online physics track paired with six safety-gated in-person laser labs. A new UX Design course for my own program, where I was the human subject-matter expert, backward-designed from eleven industry competencies. And five AI-resistant simulations written scenario by scenario.

The most useful result was negative. The simulations could not be generated. They had to be written by hand, because a task only resists a general-purpose model when it is specific enough that the model has nothing to reach for, and that specificity is still human work. The method drafts a course. It does not yet write the assessment that makes the course worth taking.

The panel also audited itself, and what it caught is published rather than summarised.

Status

Authentic Assessment is in build and the least finished of the two. OER Finder is specified and not built. Both have been run by hand on a full course, which is where the evidence below comes from.
