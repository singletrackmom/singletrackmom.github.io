Takes an assignment a student can finish with a chatbot and returns one they have to perform and defend.

See the related check runningDial Your CourseThe rule-based checks run here. The AI rewrite step described below is not in it yet.

In build, and the least finished of the tools. The method is worked out and five simulations exist; the rewrite step is not yet inside an interface.

What the method produces

Five built simulations for a graduate data science course. In each one the assessment is the student’s reasoning and defence, not a submittable artifact, which is why it holds up when anyone can generate a polished answer.
SimulationWhat the student has to do
Ridgeline RenewablesAudit a model that looks fine, find why it is not, and say what you would do about it.
How Many Segments, Really?Choose a number of clusters where the statistics disagree with each other, and defend the choice.
Model-Governance Review BoardSit on a review board and rule on whether a model ships, with the tradeoff stated.
RiverCity capstoneCarry a real problem end to end and defend the decisions to a panel.
Defence practiceRecord an answer, hear it back, and self-assess before the graded defence.

These were written scenario by scenario, by hand, not generated. That is the finding rather than a caveat: the task has to be specific enough that a general-purpose model cannot answer it, and specificity of that kind is still human work. The tool being built is the one that takes an assignment a chatbot can finish and proposes this shape instead, with a person accepting, editing, dismissing, or deferring every suggestion.
