Where content filters may bite

Seventeen activities carry medium or high risk of tripping the stricter under-18 content policies. They concentrate where difficult subject matter is the curriculum itself. Google publishes no list of restricted topics, so these are the most important prompts to test.

Where the documentation is unclear

Open questions in Google’s documentation
QuestionWhy it matters
File uploadThe age table says minors cannot upload files, but Google’s announcements describe under-18 students uploading documents to build quizzes and notebooks. Seven activities depend on the answer.
Google AI StudioAvailability to minors in education accounts is not documented. The working assumption is 18 and older only.
Admin controlGoogle describes the under-18 restrictions as on by default but documents no setting that changes them.
Restricted topicsStricter content policies apply to minors, but the restricted topics are not published.
