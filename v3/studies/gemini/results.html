The 18 and older runs are in progress, and the fourteen AI course assignments have not been run. The results shown are drafts from one run per step and still need a faculty member in each discipline to confirm them.

How to read these results

Definitions
YesEvery step that produces evidence for the competency completed.
Yes, with an adjustmentA step was blocked, and the competency can still be met with the “Under 18: do this instead” clause.
Not with Gemini as writtenThe steps that carry the competency were blocked. The student needs another route or an alternate assignment.
LimitsOne run per step. The two accounts sit in different domains. The 18 and older account is a staff account. Role-plays were run in a standard chat. All of this is drafted with AI assistance and awaits faculty confirmation. See the PRD.
