1. Summary

The Faculty Executive Council has paused the student rollout of Gemini because a meaningful share of Maricopa students are minors. The district is working to get students access, and the council’s sign-off is one of the steps. The council asked for this evidence at its last meeting. The study compares two test accounts, one designated under 18 and one designated 18 or older, so the council can see the difference for itself.

2. Goal

Show the council, plainly, what a student designated under 18 gets from Gemini compared with a student 18 or older, on the same assignment, so the accounts can be turned on as soon as the council is satisfied.

3. Users and context

Who uses the study and what each needs
WhoWhat they need
Faculty Executive CouncilA clear, short comparison of the two accounts, by subject, with charts.
Domain membersThe assignment instructions, so they can log in to a test account, try the assignment themselves, and back up or correct the result.
District administration and the steering committeeThe method, the limits of the evidence, and the references.

4. Research questions

The four research questions
RQ1Which documented feature differences are confirmed, contradicted, or undocumented when tested in the two accounts?
RQ2For the same faculty-sanctioned learning task, how often does the under-18 account refuse, redirect, or partially complete it, and in which disciplines does this concentrate?
RQ3When both accounts complete a task, do responses differ in substance, accuracy, depth, reading level, or safety framing?
RQ4Do any differences create an inequity for minors in the same course as adults, and which disciplines would need faculty guidance or alternate activities?

6. Controls

Model output varies even under identical settings. Each prompt runs in both accounts within one short window, in a fresh conversation, on the models both accounts can use, with the date and model recorded. Each scenario is repeated three times per account.

7. Ethics and governance

Safeguards
No minors take partAdults use the under-18 account. The under-18 condition comes from the account designation.
Synthetic students are test probesThey are not representations of real people, and their results are checked by domain members.
Google set up the accountsThe report says so plainly, so the findings are not read as Google’s own evaluation.
Predictions are labeledAnything drawn from documentation is marked Predicted until it has been run.

8. Open questions

  • Whether the test accounts sit in Maricopa’s setup or a Google demonstration setup.
  • The age labels and admin settings Google applied to each account.
  • Whether automated browser sessions are permitted on the test accounts.
  • Whether under-18 students can upload files in Gemini chat today.
  • The date the council needs the results.
