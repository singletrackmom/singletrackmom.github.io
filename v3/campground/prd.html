Campground Finder

PRD · Personal Projects · UX Design

OverviewPRD

Watches named campgrounds for a cancellation and reports the moment a site opens.

1. Summary

The good campgrounds are booked eleven months out and the only way in is somebody else’s change of plans. This is here as evidence of the method rather than as a hobby project: an idea taken through to a working build, which is the outcome Render is meant to produce in a student. Two halves, a search form and a scheduled watcher that writes what it finds to a calendar.

2. Goal

The good campgrounds are booked eleven months out and the only way in is somebody else’s change of plans. The goal was to stop refreshing a reservation page by hand.

3. Users and context

One household, honestly. It is on this site as evidence of the method rather than as a product: an idea taken through a specification to a working build, which is the outcome Render is meant to produce in a student.

4. How it works

Two halves. A search form for finding candidate sites, and a scheduled watcher that checks named campgrounds daily and writes what it finds straight to a calendar, so the alert arrives where the trip would be planned anyway.

5. Data, privacy, and governance

No student data and no personally identifiable information is collected. This is a hard constraint across every tool here, not a feature of one of them.

Status

Built and used. It ran every day for a month across a Yosemite trip and is currently switched off between trips. The watcher ran daily against Peak One Campground at Dillon Reservoir through June 2026, and a second instance watched Tahoe-shore sites through May. Both are disabled rather than deleted, because the pattern is the useful part.
