Campground Finder

Overview · Personal Projects · UX Design

OverviewPRD

Watches named campgrounds for a cancellation and reports the moment a site opens.

Goal

The good campgrounds are booked eleven months out and the only way in is somebody else’s change of plans. This is here as evidence of the method rather than as a hobby project: an idea taken through to a working build, which is the outcome Render is meant to produce in a student. Two halves, a search form and a scheduled watcher that writes what it finds to a calendar.

Audience

Documented on the section page.

Process

Documented on the section page.

Status

Built and used. It ran every day for a month across a Yosemite trip and is currently switched off between trips. The watcher ran daily against Peak One Campground at Dillon Reservoir through June 2026, and a second instance watched Tahoe-shore sites through May. Both are disabled rather than deleted, because the pattern is the useful part.
