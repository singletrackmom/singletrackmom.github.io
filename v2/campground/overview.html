Campground Finder

Tool · Personal Projects

OverviewPRDWayfinder

Watches one campground for a cancellation and tells me the moment a site opens, because the good ones are booked eleven months out and the only way in is someone else’s change of plans.

Screenshot needed: the Find a Campground form in wayfinder/builder.html.

Goal

Get a campsite at a specific campground on a specific weekend without checking a reservation site by hand every morning for months. The inventory is real but it moves, and it moves at times nobody is watching.

Audience

Me first. Anyone who has tried to book a popular campground second.

Process

Two halves. The search form takes an area, a number of nights, optional dates, and filters for developed sites, first-come-first-served, pet-friendly, ADA accessible, and near-town, then opens a live Recreation.gov search for exactly that. The watcher is a scheduled agent that checks one named campground every morning for a cancellation and writes what it finds to my calendar, so the answer is on my phone before I am awake.

It does not book anything. After I make a reservation myself, the confirmed dates feed into Wayfinder, which handles the trip once it exists.

It is deliberately separate from Wayfinder. Finding a site and running a trip are different problems, and joining them would make both tools worse.

Screenshot needed: a calendar entry the watcher wrote, or a results screen from the Recreation.gov handoff.

Status

Built and used, currently switched off. The search form is live inside the Wayfinder builder. The watcher ran daily against Peak One Campground at Dillon Reservoir through June 2026 and a second instance watched Tahoe-shore sites through May. Both are disabled rather than deleted, because the pattern is the useful part and it points at any campground.
