Review

14 of 27 graded items carry no outcome

Graded work that is attached to no course outcome, including the student success sequence, the industry inspiration set, and the course reflection. Not necessarily wrong. It is the kind of thing an instructor should see and decide about deliberately.

Course-wide

Fix

Outcomes exist but four rubrics do not bind to them

13 of 17 rubrics point at an outcome object. The remaining four grade work without reporting mastery, so Canvas cannot roll that evidence into the gradebook.
