Dial Your Course

Project · AI Tools & Strategy · Learning Design

OverviewLive tool

A Canvas course goes in. What to fix comes back. Each of the six tools runs on its own, or hands off to the next.

Video walkthrough goes here.
Six to seven minutes, one real course end to end.

Goal

Every online course has to clear a quality bar. At my college that standard is OSCQR, along with seat time, accessibility, and regular substantive interaction, and faculty check it by hand or not at all. Four of the six tools are rule-based checks with no AI in them. Only the last two use a model. The standard is not fixed to OSCQR either. Point the tool at whichever rubric your institution uses.

Audience

Any instructor building or revising an online course, and the instructional designer or program director who has to review it.

The six tools

1Syllabus Builder1 of 6 · no AIProduces a compliant syllabus from the course data you already have, and tells you what is missing. 2Quality Check2 of 6 · no AIRuns a course against a published quality standard and returns what fails, by module, with the fix. 3Style Guide3 of 6 · no AIGenerates Canvas-safe course HTML in a chosen palette, every style inline, because Canvas strips stylesheets. 4Seat Time4 of 6 · no AIEstimates the real time a module asks of a student against what the credit hours allow. 5Authentic Assessment5 of 6 · uses a modelTakes an assignment a student can finish with a chatbot and returns one they have to perform and defend. 6OER Finder6 of 6 · uses a modelFinds openly licensed material for a thin module, verifies the license, and prepares what you build for contribution back.
