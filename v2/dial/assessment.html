OverviewPRDDial Your Course

Takes an assignment a student can finish with a chatbot and returns one they have to perform and defend.

Screenshot or running tool goes here.

Goal

The take-home essay and the multiple-choice exam stopped being reliable the moment students had AI. Agreeing with that is easy. Rebuilding a real course around it, week after week, is the work.

Audience

Any instructor whose assessments were built for a world without AI, which is most of them.

Process

Uses a model, because rewriting an assignment into a defensible task is judgement, not a lookup. The model proposes and the instructor decides.

Status

Placeholder, and the least finished of the six. It is also the one worth the most.
