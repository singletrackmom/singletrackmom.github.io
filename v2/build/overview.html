Build Your Course

Project · AI Tools & Strategy · Learning Design

OverviewLive toolDial Your Course

Dial Your Course tells you what is wrong. This builds the replacement.

Goal

Checking a course and building one are different problems with different trust requirements. The checks in Dial Your Course are deterministic: a published standard, a fixed rule, the same answer every time. Building an assessment or finding open material is judgement, so it needs AI, and anything AI writes needs a person to accept it before a student sees it. Separating the two keeps the checking trustworthy and puts the review where it belongs.

Audience

An instructor who now knows what is wrong with a module and has to fix it. Faculty use the interface, never the underlying method directly.

Process

Nothing is applied automatically. Every suggestion arrives as a proposed replacement with four choices: apply it, edit it first, dismiss it with a reason, or defer it to next semester. Nothing leaves the tool until you have seen the before and after side by side.

The same method builds a whole course when there is nothing to start from. A panel of AI subject-matter-expert, instructional-designer, and student agents drafts it, and a human subject-matter expert reviews and owns every decision. That is how the graduate data science course, the photonics course, and the wind turbine simulations were made.

Status

In build. Both tools exist as methods and are being built into one interface.
