AI Opportunity Pipeline

AI Strategy · Case study

OverviewLive toolPRD

A front door for AI requests across ten colleges. A department describes a problem, the request lands in one shared Sheet, and each one is checked, scored, and answered with a reason.

Goal

Give the Student Support and Success domain of the Maricopa AI Resource Center one front door for AI ideas, and one written, consistent way to decide what happens to each. Every request leaves with an answer and a reason, and sometimes the answer is “this is not an AI problem.”

Audience

Student services staff and leadership across the ten Maricopa colleges, mostly advisors and support staff, and the domain members who assess what comes in. Requests start from problems like these, the barriers students hit when they try to reach support:

  • Advising takes about three days to answer an email.
  • Students never find disability resources, because nobody who needs them thinks of themselves as disabled.
  • Tutoring has limited hours and lives in Discord, and nobody knows it is there.

Process

One request is one row in one Sheet. The requester fills out the front half. The domain fills out the back half.

RequestGoal, problem, today’s process, who it touches and how many, stakeholders, the success measure, the data involvedDepartment

Technology

  • A single HTML request form: vanilla JavaScript, no framework.
  • Google Apps Script web app that receives each request and writes it to the Sheet.
  • Google Sheets for the record: dropdowns for status, gates, impact and confidence, and formulas for the RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) score and rank.
  • An email from Apps Script when a request arrives.
  • Hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • No AI model in the tool. People do the assessing.
  • Designed and built with Claude as the coding assistant.

Outcomes

The intake is in pilot. Requests land in one Sheet, already structured for gates, a RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) score and a ranked list, and a scored row carries what a PRD needs. Next: calibrate the scores against real past requests, then open the inventory round with the colleges.
