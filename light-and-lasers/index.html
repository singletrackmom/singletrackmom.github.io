Light and Lasers: a hybrid photonics course

Case study · Synthetic SMEs

Overview The course The Synthetic SMEs method

A hybrid photonics course for Rio Salado College, on the Photonics Technician Operator pathway. The online lessons teach the physics, the math, and laser safety; six required in-person labs are where each hands-on skill is demonstrated and signed off, with safety as a hard gate.

Goal

Build a rigorous hybrid course, MNT130 Introduction to Light and Lasers, that carries all seven Photonics Technician Operator competencies. The design keeps the physics and math online, where they can be practiced and graded, and puts every hands-on skill in a required in-person lab that is scored on a skills demonstration, with a laser-safety check that must pass before anything else counts.

Audience

Rio Salado workforce learners on the Photonics Technician Operator pathway, most of them working adults in a two-year program, not degree-seeking undergraduates. The course is written to be handed to a faculty subject-matter expert who reviews the physics, confirms the resource links, customizes it, and owns it before any student sees it.

Technology

  • The build method, the same one behind Synthetic SMEs, is packaged as a reusable skill that orchestrates an agent panel: a subject-matter-expert agent (an industry photonics practitioner), an instructional-designer agent, and a synthetic student modeled on a Maricopa two-year workforce learner. A human learning designer directs the panel and a named faculty subject-matter expert reviews and owns the result.
  • The design logic is backward design (Wiggins & McTighe) and constructive alignment (Biggs), with the standards frame scoped by modality: OSCQR for the online lessons, ANSI Z136.1 laser safety for the in-person labs, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility throughout, Regular and Substantive Interaction right-sized for the face-to-face labs, and Higher Learning Commission seat time itemized in every lesson.
  • Assessment is six in-person, safety-gated skills-demonstration labs (100 points each, safety a hard gate) paired with six summative homework sets (50 points each), 900 points total, aligned to the seven Photonics Technician Operator competencies and measured by skills-mastery sign-off and certification rather than seat time.
  • Open educational resources, each license-noted: LASER-TEC modules (CC BY-NC-ND, link-only via RioLearn), PhET interactive simulations (Bending Light, Lasers, Geometric Optics, Wave Interference), OpenStax University Physics Volume 3, HyperPhysics, and OSHA and ANSI Z136.1 safety references.
  • Six lesson slide decks with speaker-note teaching scripts, and an itemized seat-time plan written into every lesson.
  • Activities designed against an active-learning framework (ICAP) for the online work, with the hands-on skills demonstrated live and signed off in the lab.
  • Delivered as static HTML and CSS, a styled course view packaged for RioLearn.

Status

Draft. Six lessons, authored with point-valued rubrics totaling 900 points, itemized seat time, and safety-gated labs. It is a rigorous starting point for a faculty subject-matter expert to verify, not a course in production.
