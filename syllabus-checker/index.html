Syllabus Checker

Submit your syllabus no later than the Friday of the first week of your class start date. Every syllabus must follow the MCCCD Syllabus Standards. One syllabus per submission. Combined courses must list every class, including class numbers.

Step 1 · Your course info

Includes courses with separate lecture and lab sections. Combined syllabi must list all classes, including class numbers.
Typically the level-one / introductory course. Format: PREFIX###-#####
List every associated class, separated by commas.
Confirm your credits, load hours, periods, and activity type on the CCTA website. Total student hours = credits × 40 always. Load hours change the split not the total.

Step 2 · Attach your syllabus

PDF (.pdf) or Word (.doc.docx). Max 100 MB. Skip this if you use Simple Syllabus / Syllabus+ and use the field below instead.
In Canvas, submit your syllabus in editor mode. Two links appear (with confetti). Use the second one, titled “Syllabus PDF URL.”

Attach a file or paste a Syllabus PDF URL. One or the other, not neither.

Step 3 · Submit

You will get a reply right away: a thank-you if your syllabus is complete, or a short list of what to add before you resubmit.

What it checks

District required elements (MCCCD Regulation 3.6)

  • College name, campus or site
  • Instructor name and contact information
  • Course title, prefix, number, and section
  • Academic term and year
  • MCCCD course description or overview
  • Course format (online, hybrid, face-to-face)
  • Delivery modes for course materials
  • Credit hours
  • Contact hours and out-of-class expectations
  • Link to the MCCCD Official Course Competencies
  • Grading standards and practices
  • Attendance requirements
  • Required and recommended texts and technologies
  • Student responsibility for syllabus information
  • Student responsibility for college policies
  • Availability of accommodation services
  • Third-party tools, LTI, proctoring, plagiarism disclosures
  • Subject-to-change statement
  • Instructor communication response-time expectation
  • Disability services statement or link
  • Title IX sexual harassment statement or link

Required approved language

  • Approved seat-hours statement, verbatim · the exact language for your credits, load hours, and activity type, in the workload section (in Syllabus+, “Instructional Contact Hours & Out-of-Class Student Work”)
  • RSI, regular and substantive interaction · online and hybrid courses only. The statement describing instructor-facilitated discussions, timely feedback, interactive presentations, peer collaboration, and projects

Both statements must appear exactly as approved, not paraphrased. The approved wording depends on your credits, load hours, and activity type, so confirm those on the CCTA website before you submit. Department reference: approved seat-hours language ↓

Any additional requirements set by your program are included here automatically.
