The courseModulesAbout

A complete suite of summative assessments I built for a graduate Advanced Data Science course. Each one puts the learner in a real decision and grades the reasoning, not a retrievable answer, and each is uniquely seeded per student so no two attempts are alike.

Goal

Assess graduate data-science judgment in a way generative AI cannot complete for the student: catching a model’s hidden defects, defending an unsupervised result, weighing a governance trade-off, and defending a recommendation out loud.

Audience

Graduate data-science students (adult, online). Reusable by any instructor teaching model evaluation, clustering, AI governance, or capstone defense.

Process

Each assessment scenario is seeded per student, walks the learner through diagnosis and decision, and ends in a defended call. Built as single-file, no-backend, no-PII web objects, tested with synthetic-student usability passes.

The suite

Module 5 · Model evaluation

Ridgeline Renewables: audit a model before it ships

A contractor’s “0.97, deploy now” model for a wind-and-solar site hides target leakage, train/test contamination, a mismatched metric, and a subgroup collapse. The learner audits it, re-estimates honest performance, and defends a sign-off, condition, or reject.

Why they resist AI substitution

Each simulation grades judgment over private, per-student evidence, not a shareable answer. The leaked feature, the contaminated rows, the true cluster count, and the failing subgroup differ by seed, and the defenses are recorded responses to randomized questions. A pasted, generic answer cannot match a scenario that exists nowhere online.
