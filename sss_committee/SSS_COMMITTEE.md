# SSS Walkthrough, Co-Chair Working Session

**SSS Domain co-chairs · ~45 min**

Purpose: lock the plan for the district usability walkthrough before it goes to the full domain. Leave with a method, a crosswalk task, and owners.

## 1. Frame the win (3 min)
This study represents ~140K Maricopa students. Done right it's a district barrier-reduction roadmap and a serious portfolio piece. We're setting the bar high: a real usability study, not a survey.

## 2. Scope: everything that touches a student (10 min)
- Not just "student services." Every student-facing touchpoint: application, admissions, MEID/portal login, financial aid, advising, registration, Canvas, tutoring, disability services, basic needs, library, career services, Salesforce touchpoints.
- The other co-chair brings the silo terminology, how each college names and structures these. We map their language onto one common crosswalk so we're comparing the same function across all 10 colleges.
- Decide the master list of systems today (start from the test-account-request working set, expand).

## 3. The inventory crosswalk (10 min)
- The Student Services inventory sheet is in the shared folder. It has errors and gaps.
- Plan: each college names one person to review and correct their rows, what's wrong, missing, or named differently.
- Agree the correction deadline and who chases each college.

## 4. Personas + walkthrough method (10 min)
- Confirm the persona set. Current draft: first-gen applicant, returning adult working full time, disability/accommodations, veteran on VA benefits, bilingual Spanish-dominant. Add more, or align personas to different majors/fields of interest?
- Method: walk each persona through the full journey, application to enrollment to support to workforce, in the real systems, documenting every barrier with screenshots and notes.
- Test accounts: 5 synthetic accounts, IT contact named by July 1, accounts live by July 15. The ask doc is drafted and ready to send to the tri-chairs.

## 5. The deliverable (5 min)
- A full report/presentation: friction inventory by persona and system, then a prioritized list of where AI tools or integrations remove each barrier.
- That priority list is the takeaway, it's how we decide where to start.

## 6. Salesforce (3 min)
- A co-chair to set up a session with the Salesforce people so we understand what it does, and so we can run the personas through it like every other system.

## 7. Next steps + owners (4 min)
- Who does what, by when. Set the date to bring this to the full domain.

# Request: Persona Test Accounts for the SSS District Usability Study

**From:** Student Support and Success (SSS) Domain, Maricopa AI Resource Committee
**To:** ARC Tri-Chairs
**Date:** June 2026
**Decision needed:** Approve test account provisioning and name a district IT contact by **July 1** so accounts are live by **July 15**.

## What we're asking for

Five (5) synthetic student test accounts, provisioned by district IT, that the SSS Domain can use to walk the full student journey across all systems students touch, from application through program completion and into the workforce.

Each account carries one research persona:

1. First-generation applicant, no family college experience
2. Returning adult student, working full time
3. Student using disability services and accommodations
4. Veteran using VA education benefits
5. Bilingual student, Spanish-dominant at home

## Systems the accounts need to reach

The application/admissions process, MEID and student portal login, Salesforce touchpoints, Canvas, advising and class scheduling, financial aid, tutoring, disability services intake, basic needs referral, and career services. [Committee to finalize the complete system list; this is the working set.]

## Why test accounts, not real students or staff views

- **FERPA-safe.** Synthetic accounts contain no real student data, which is exactly why this is the cleanest path. No consent management, no records exposure.
- **Staff views hide the problem.** Staff-side access shows us the system as designed, not the system as experienced. Friction lives on the student side.
- **Auditable and reversible.** District IT can scope, monitor, and deactivate the accounts when the study ends.

## What this unlocks

A district-wide usability report covering every student-facing system at all 10 colleges: a documented inventory of friction points and barriers by persona, followed by a prioritized list of where AI tools or integrations would remove those barriers. The SSS Domain delivers the instrument and fieldwork; the study is already scoped for summer form-building and fall fieldwork. Without student-side access, the study cannot start, and the domain's summer deliverable is blocked on this single dependency.

## The ask, restated

By **July 1**: a named district IT contact and tri-chair sponsorship for provisioning.
By **July 15**: five accounts live.
We're glad to meet with IT to scope access, and we'll provide the persona specs and a data-handling agreement in whatever form IT prefers.

Michelle Blomberg, Co-Chair, SSS Domain
