The Traveler

Case study · Teaching/Program Design

GCC’s award-winning student literary and arts magazine, made end to end by students and advised through Volumes 44 to 58.

Read the back issues →

Goal

Give students authentic, end-to-end publishing experience: soliciting and editing work, designing and setting type, and shipping a finished, printed piece for their portfolio.

The Traveler is Glendale Community College’s student literary and arts magazine, a real annual publication of student writing, art, and photography, produced entirely by students.

Audience

The campus community and the students who make it. It is a genuine publication with a real audience. Back issues live in the GCC library archive, and are preserved in the Arizona state library’s digital archive through the Arizona Memory Project.

Process

It is student-run from submission to press, guided by faculty advising, across Volumes 44 through 58: students solicit and select the work, edit it, design and set the type, and take a finished piece to print.

Technology

  • Adobe InDesign for layout and typesetting, Photoshop for preparing art and photography, produced as a printed annual.
  • A full student editorial pipeline: soliciting, selecting, and editing submissions of writing, art, and photography, then designing to press.
  • Back issues archived in the GCC library and preserved in the Arizona Memory Project, the Arizona State Library’s digital archive.

Outcomes

A multiple Pinnacle Award winner from the College Media Association, national recognition for excellence in student publications, sustained across Volumes 44 to 58. Every volume ships a real printed piece students can carry into a portfolio, and their work is preserved in a state archive: an authentic audience and a permanent record, not an assignment that ends at a grade.

Status

Ongoing. A live annual publication advised across Volumes 44 to 58, with a standing student editorial process, sustained national recognition, and a permanent place in a state archive. Each cycle is a new cohort taking a real magazine to press.
