Coming soon. These are early ideas for the next edition, and none of them is final. Right now we are building the team.

The idea

The next edition coincides with the opening of the college’s new STEAMworks building, so we are exploring how much science, technology, engineering, art and math a printed book can carry, and what a book can be beyond the page. Each piece in the magazine could hold a story the page alone cannot.

Ideas we are exploring

IdeaWhat it could addWho might make it
3D artwork in augmented realityReaders point a phone at a printed sculpture or ceramic piece and turn it around in 3D, in its true colorThe 3D modeling class, or students capturing the piece with photography
Creator interviewsShort videos of artists and writers talking about their process or inspirationDesign Studio student workers
PodcastsConversations with some of the artists and writersStudents in a journalism course
Rule-generated pagesA page generator that builds layouts in InDesign from rules the design team writesThe design team
A chip in the coverReaders tap the cover with a phone to reach the digital pieces, with a QR code as a backupThe design team, with the printer

How it could start

In January, a call for participation would go to the contest winners, asking each creator which of these they would like to join: a 3D model of their work, an interview, or a podcast conversation.
