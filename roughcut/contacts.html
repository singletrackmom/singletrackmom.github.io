This is the working spreadsheet with the names, email addresses and phone numbers taken out. The private version holds each person’s name, role and address, a copy-and-paste mailing block per school and per district, and the outreach status. The research was done with AI, one school at a time, from school sites, staff directories and program pages, and each record is checked before anything is sent.

Goal

Send the newsletter to the adults who teach the students we want, not to a front office inbox.

Audience

The program faculty who do the outreach. They need to know who to write to at each school and what that school already teaches.

Process

Each school was researched for its media, art and career programs, then for the people who run them. Where a name could not be confirmed from a public source, the role is listed as one to identify by phone.
