## What it is

A donor collaborative managed by Panorama Global, backed by eight foundations (Ascendium, College Futures, ECMC, Gates, Haas Jr., Lumina, Raikes, Strada). It funds organizations *operating a postsecondary model* that rethinks how education is designed, delivered, financed, and assessed for U.S. learners.

- Grants available: 7 to 10
- Grant size: $750,000 to $1,000,000 each
- Unit of funding: an education MODEL that enrolls learners and awards credentials. Not a tool, not an individual.

## The four themes (align with at least one; you do not need all four)

1. **Reimagining instructional design, delivery, assessment, and student support.** Personalized/adaptive instruction, competency-based assessment, better persistence and completion, especially for underserved learners, at maintained or lower cost.
2. **Advancing affordability and financial sustainability.** Learners spend less and carry less risk; the model also keeps the cost of *delivering* quality education sustainable.
3. **Career and workforce alignment.** Skills tied to credentials and real jobs; work and learning more connected.
4. **Removing barriers for underserved learners.** More learners from the highest-barrier backgrounds access, complete, and benefit.

All four are grounded in student-centeredness.

## How my work maps to the four themes (thematic fit is strong)

- **Theme 1, strong.** Render (competency-mapped, personalized career-launch), Dial Your Course (assessment/quality automation), CopaMigo (student support routing), AVC 100 redesign (persistence, measured pre/post). This is the core of my practice.
- **Theme 2, partial.** No-PII, single-file, no-license-cost builds and OER pathways lower delivery cost, but this theme is really about lowering the cost of a *credential to the learner*, an institutional lever.
- **Theme 3, yes.** Render maps skills to jobs and exports a portable career agent.
- **Theme 4, strong.** HSI context, bilingual CopaMigo, the barriers the ten-college journey study surfaces.

## The eligibility wall (this is the real issue)

Minimum eligibility gates every applicant:

1. **Credentials.** The proposed model awards, or will award, bachelor's/associate degrees or credentials of value (industry certs, stackable, competency-based) tied to employer demand, licensure, or labor outcomes.
2. **U.S. students.** Serves U.S. learners (org need not be U.S.-incorporated).
3. **Active enrollment by March 31, 2027** in programs leading to those credentials.
4. **Learning and evaluation.** Participate in shared learning if selected.

Also required at application: proof of legal status and a current organizational budget. Post-baccalaureate-certificate-only models are NOT eligible. Nonprofit status not required (for-profits, fiscally sponsored orgs, and international orgs are eligible). Accreditation not required. Tech/AI not required.

**Why a solo application does not clear it:** Render and Dial Your Course do not award credentials and do not enroll learners; they support courses that do. Forming an LLC or using a fiscal sponsor fixes the legal-entity box but NOT the credential-awarding and active-enrollment boxes. A $750K to $1M / 18-month grant is scaled for an organization running a program, not a solo builder hardening a prototype.

## Verdict

- **Thematic fit: strong, especially Theme 1.**
- **Solo eligibility: not viable.** It would be screened out at eligibility, on the merits, not a technicality.
- **The only honest path is institutional.**

## The institutional path (how it could actually work)

The "institution-wide" nature is the intended vehicle, not an obstacle to route around.

- **Applicant = GCC or Maricopa. Model = my integrated, student-centered redesign. Me = architect / project lead / PI.**
- The DMA program already enrolls students and awards AAS degrees and certificates (credentials of value), so it clears eligibility #1 and #3 *today*.
- The tools become the *innovation inside* an enrolling, credential-granting model: AI-scaffolded, no-PII, career-launch-embedded, services-integrated, evidence-measured. The package (AVC 100 redesign + Render + CopaMigo + journey study + Dial Your Course) is a stronger IPM story than any one tool alone.
- My ARC role and the district AI CIO relationship are the doors for the sponsorship conversation.

## Reality checks

- **Timeline is tight.** Sept 14 deadline is ~6 weeks out; an institutional grant this size needs the grants/foundation office, a budget, and sign-offs. Doable only if the college wants to move fast.
- **If GCC/Maricopa can't or won't anchor it by the deadline, this is not the right vehicle for solo work.** Better-fit funders exist for individual builders and tools (smaller ed-tech innovation grants, fellowships). Don't sink weeks into an application that fails eligibility.

## What the application asks for (all institution-level)

Organization info, current postsecondary work, finances, the proposed model, expected outcomes, learners served, barriers addressed, what's innovative, accreditation/QA approach, how success is measured, growth plans, org finances. Uploads: proof of legal status, current organizational budget, optional learner-outcome/evaluation data.
