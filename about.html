About

I’m a learning strategist and AI innovator working at the intersection of AI, design, and the future of education. I build because I teach: two decades in the classroom show me exactly where learners get stuck, and I design, prototype, and pilot the tools that meet them there.

I’ve always been an early adopter, at the forefront of whatever medium comes next, and right now that means building with AI every day. As McLuhan knew, every new medium reshapes how we see, so the durable skill is the thinking underneath, carried into whatever tool comes next.

The judgment I care about most is where AI accelerates learning and where human connection is what keeps a learner moving, measured not by features shipped but by learning, retention, and engagement.

My path runs through design and educational technology: graphic and web design, product management at an EdTech startup, instructional-technology leadership, and, for over a decade, designing and teaching in higher education. That range is why I can take an idea from a real classroom problem to a working, evidence-grounded pilot.

I think the future of learning is agentic and adaptive, AI that reads what a learner already knows and personalizes the path, with authentic assessment and the human kept firmly in the loop. That is the future I am building toward.
