What I do

I turn frontier AI into practical tools people actually use, then set the standards that keep them safe. I am a daily builder on the Claude API and agentic workflows, I co-chair the Student Support and Success domain of a ten-college district AI strategy, and I bring twenty-plus years in instructional technology, design, and faculty development. I move non-technical people from fear of AI to confident, independent use, and I write the governance that lets a prototype become a sanctioned, supported tool.

A few things I have built

Dial Your Course, Canvas course-quality auditor

In testing

A drop-in tool that reads a Canvas course export and runs nineteen standards checks (Quality Matters and OSCQR, accessibility, measurable objectives, AI-resistance), names the standard and location for every finding, and writes approved fixes back into a reimportable package. No student data leaves the browser.

Render, student career-launch tool

In a student pilot this fall

Resume and interview practice with an AI that asks real follow-up questions, a skills-gap analysis against a chosen job, and a portable career agent the student keeps and runs after graduation. First name only, no personal data stored.

AI adoption governance, prototype to production

In use with district AI leadership

The framework that moves a builder’s AI prototype into a governed, FERPA-aware pilot and then production: a lightweight intake, an approved-tool menu, and human-in-the-loop guardrails, adapting the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and EDUCAUSE guidance.
