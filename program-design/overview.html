Digital Media Arts and Animation Programs

Courses and Programs · Case study

OverviewDegrees

Two career programs at Glendale Community College in the Maricopa district, designed backward from what industry hires for: cross-disciplinary courses in design, animation, UX, gaming, video and sound, real client work before graduation, AI across the program and the new STEAMworks XR lab for augmented and virtual reality.

Goal

Graduate students ready for the industry jobs open now and for where the work is going next. The programs are built from an industry advisory board’s target, kept current through regular review, and moved forward by adopting new tools early, from AI agents to augmented and virtual reality.

Audience

Career starters and career changers who want a creative career in animation, graphic design, UX, gaming, video or sound design. They learn side by side, and every pathway includes 2D and motion. Students finish a two-year degree that leads straight to a job or transfers to ASU, with one-on-one advising along the way.

GCC is one of 10 colleges in the Maricopa Community Colleges, one of the largest community college districts in the country. Tuition is $97 per credit for Maricopa County residents, among the lowest community college tuition in the country. The entire program can be taken online, with on-campus options as well, and many students swirl between GCC, Mesa Community College and Phoenix College, which share programs. No equipment is needed to start: students can borrow a MacBook Pro or a high-end gaming PC for the semester through GCC’s Student Technology Request.

Course Method

Every course in the program is built on competencies I just redesigned with faculty colleagues, and goes through the same build and review method, from those competencies to a faculty sign-off, so each one is checked the same way before a student sees it.

The panels and testers in steps 5 and 6 are AI agents, not real faculty or students. Testing with real students begins when each course launches; AVC 100, already taught, was evaluated with real students through a pre and post survey.

Roadmap

AI now runs through every redesigned course. Each one carries an AI in This Course plan: every use is assigned, tied to a specific assignment and bounded, and Gemini writes, questions and checks but never designs. The courses are built; the first offerings launch when the district turns on Gemini for student accounts.

Technology

  • Adobe Creative Cloud: Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, After Effects, Premiere Pro and Audition.
  • Figma and Figma Buzz for interface design, prototypes and brand templates.
  • Blender for 3D, and Unity, Unreal and Godot for games, interactive and VR work, with the snap-in Unity code modules.
  • Students use Gemini through their Maricopa accounts, under Google’s Gemini for Education terms.
  • Gemini for Education: the Gemini app, Gemini Notebook, Gemini Canvas, Gemini Live and Gemini skills.
  • The STEAMworks XR lab: 3D printers, laser cutting, a 3D scanner, and extended reality (AR and VR).
  • Canvas, Discord for critique, and Google Workspace, with each student’s portfolio on Google Sites.
  • MacBook Pro and high-end gaming PC loans through the Student Technology Request.
  • Planning data: the industry advisory board, Maricopa IRIS, Bureau of Labor Statistics data and the district Guided Pathways course bank.

Outcomes

Graduation nearly doubled while enrollment stayed roughly flat, so the gain came from students finishing.

  • Real work for everyone. Every student completes the Design Studio or a 120-hour internship, where industry partners give real work, and graduates with a portfolio and reel.
  • Hired. A recent student was hired by a major tech company after taking advantage of everything the program offers: a paid studio year, leading The Traveler’s design team, and an internship with a program sponsor.
  • Awards. A student’s Rocky Mountain Emmy for animation and visual effects; most of the Animation awards in the district’s Artist of Promise competition most years; College Media Association Pinnacle Awards for The Traveler.
  • Resources. Staffing grew to three full-time faculty, and three siloed labs became one central lab open early, late and on weekends.

Status

Ongoing program work. Six new course builds are designed and fully built, AVC 100 is built and taught, and the AI roadmap launches as Gemini reaches student accounts.
