Credentials

Courses and Programs · Case study

OverviewCredentials

Stackable fast-track certificates for Animation and Digital Media Arts, so students earn a resume-ready credential early and keep every credit on the way to the degree.

Goal

Turn the program’s credentials into on-ramps. The redesign replaces an adopted certificate that asked for nearly a degree’s worth of credits without granting a degree.

Audience

Career changers, first-time college students and dual-enrollment high schoolers. Each gets an early win and a visible next step, and no credit is wasted if they keep going.

Process

The credentials were mapped to their degree outcomes, then broken into three blocks, each under the district’s 15-credit fast-track cap, and submitted through the college’s Instructional Council curriculum workflow.

Structure

Three blocks stack to 37 credits per track, and every credit counts toward the AAS.
