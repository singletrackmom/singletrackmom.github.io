# Program notes · Digital Media Arts (GCC)

Working notes for the program. Not linked from the site.

## Course materials

Set by Michelle, 25 Oct 2026.

- The DMA program was GCC’s first Near Z Degree (no textbooks).
- The program builds its own OER and does not use or require textbooks.
- No course may require students to buy a book.
- Commercial books can be discussed, or shown through free previews, interviews or library copies, but are never required.
- Example: AVC 150 is inspired by Amy Kurzweil’s *Artificial: A Love Story*; students meet it through her free video preview and an NPR interview (see `canvas/avc150/AVC150.md`).
- Course-build rule: `canvas/GETTING_STARTED_STANDARD.md`, “No required textbooks: Near Z Degree program; use OER and free sources.”
