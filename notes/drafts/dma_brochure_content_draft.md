## Digital Arts at GCC (umbrella overview)

Prepare for a growing career in the digital arts. Our hands-on program equips you with the training and multi-industry skills to become an expert in fundamental design principles, imaging techniques, graphic design, video production, creative branding, and web and interactive design, among other areas.

At Glendale Community College, you develop both creative vision and technical expertise through hands-on, project-based learning. You work with industry-standard software and tools while building a professional portfolio that showcases your design, storytelling, and digital production skills.

Whether you earn a certificate or an associate degree, GCC's digital arts program gives you a strong foundation and the hands-on skills to create compelling media designs and communicate effectively with target audiences. Flexible pathways let you build skills step by step toward a certificate, or work on an associate degree, either to enter the workforce or to prepare for further study. Tuition starts at just $97 per credit hour, about 75% lower than a public in-state university, and it is free to enroll.

**What you can study:** visual design and layout, digital imaging and illustration, video production and editing, creative branding and visual identity, and web and interactive design. Careers span marketing, media production, branding, web design, content creation, and multimedia storytelling.

## Digital Media Arts (AAS)

Academic Plan 3567 · 66–74 credits · Catalog 2026–2027 · Can be completed fully online or in a hybrid format.

The Digital Media Arts degree builds professional competence in research, planning, production, and presentation of media designs. Students move through the full design process, from concept to a portfolio that reflects current industry practice, and finish with a client-based comprehensive project for an industry, organization, or community partner.

**What you'll learn**

- Create media designs to project specifications, with professional competence in research, planning, production, and presentation.
- Apply critical thinking, the design process, and historical and media literacies to solve design problems.
- Critique media designs to evaluate their effectiveness.
- Research and apply industry-standard tools to develop effective media, designs, and workflows.
- Demonstrate professional and collaborative practices: communication, time management, organization, and legal and ethical considerations.
- Communicate design ideas and solutions that recognize diverse opinions and cultural and individual differences.
- Build a portfolio of work that reflects current industry practices.
- Contribute to a client-based comprehensive project for real-world use.
- Apply the knowledge, skills, and habits of mind to pursue career opportunities in the field.

**Foundational courses include** AVC100 Introduction to Digital Arts, AVC169 2D Media Design (gateway course), AVC142 Introduction to Digital Photography, and AVC248 Design Self-Promotion. (Full course sequence available on the program page; students must earn a C or better in all program courses.)

**Where it leads:** graphic design, digital and print media, creative branding, and web and interactive design, plus transfer toward a bachelor's degree. Related occupations include art directors and graphic designers.

## Animation and Time-Based Media (AAS)

Academic Plan 3199 · 69–77 credits.

This degree focuses on storytelling through animation, still, interactive, and time-based media. Students learn to develop and critique complex narratives for diverse audiences and finish with a collection of professional work through a capstone, internship, and/or portfolio.

**What you'll learn**

- Critique narratives and complex still, interactive, time-based, and animation media to evaluate their effectiveness for diverse audiences.
- Apply industry-standard tools and research to develop effective complex time-based media or animations and workflows.
- Demonstrate professional and collaborative practices in diverse settings: communication, time management, organization, and legal and ethical considerations.
- Communicate narrative through animation, still, and time-based media while recognizing diverse opinions and cultural and individual differences.
- Create a collection of works (capstone, internships, and/or portfolio) that reflect professional competency.
- Apply the knowledge, skills, and habits of mind to pursue career opportunities in the field.

## Film & Media Production

Create a new and exciting future in media and film production. Our program encompasses screenwriting, budgeting, directing, sound design, cinematography, editing, and animation, and can help you get started in this highly competitive industry, introducing you to the latest technologies and filmmaking techniques.

Students get hands-on experience across all major production areas, writing, producing, cinematography, audio, directing, and editing, in small classes with easy access to faculty. Class work is supplemented by lectures from working film professionals, field trips to production studios, and participation in local film events. As film and television production returns to Arizona, GCC partners with the Arizona Commerce Authority to run 5-day Production Assistant workshops that build the state's "Film Ready Crew."

**Degree:** Film and Media Production (AAS), plan 3570.
**Certificate:** Documentary Film Production (CCL), plan 5059.

**Where it leads:** writing, producing, cinematography, audio, directing, and editing roles across the film and video industries, plus transfer toward a bachelor's degree.

## Photography

Learn the artistic, technical, and business side of photography. Our program prepares you for a variety of professional positions, including freelance work and photo editing, as well as further study.

The Freelance Photography certificate builds skills in both still photography and video production, exposure principles, camera operations, digital workflows, video-based strategies, and studio lighting, plus the business and marketing practices essential to freelance success. (The related Commercial and Freelance Photography track also covers film-based strategies and studio lighting.)

**Degree:** Photography (AAS), plan 3195.
**Certificate:** Freelance Photography (CCL), plan 5784.

**Where it leads:** freelance photographer, wedding photographer, commercial photographer, photo editor, photography assistant, or photo lab technician.

## Shared selling points (use throughout)

- Affordable: tuition from $97/credit hour, ~75% lower than a public in-state university; free to enroll.
- Flexible: certificate or degree pathways; Digital Media Arts can be completed fully online or hybrid.
- Portfolio-first: project-based learning with industry-standard tools; graduates leave with a professional portfolio.
- Transfer-ready: extensive transfer partners, including Arizona's public universities.
- Support: personalized advising and free student services.
