# Mines OLED, interview + positioning strategy

_July 4, 2026. Written as if I were the hiring manager for the Online Learning Experience Designer role at Mines Online, then turned into Michelle's positioning. Companion to mines-flagship-project.md and PORTFOLIO_REDO_PLAN.md._

## What I (the hiring manager) actually want to see

I am hiring someone to sit next to a faculty subject-matter expert and BUILD their online graduate course. In priority order, I am looking for:

1. **Proof you can build a whole course, not describe it.** A real Canvas course you designed end to end: outcomes, aligned assessments, scaffolded modules, accessibility. Show me one.
2. **Instructional-design craft.** Backward design, alignment, adult-learning theory, UDL, and a recognized quality standard (Quality Matters / OSCQR). I want rigor, not vibes.
3. **You can work WITH faculty experts.** Most of the job is guiding a brilliant, busy professor who has never built online. I want someone who trains and coaches faculty and makes them look good.
4. **LMS and tooling command.** Canvas at a power-user/admin level, templates, content reuse, packaging, and the design tools we use (DesignPLUS). I do not want to teach you the LMS.
5. **A designer's eye + media.** Storyboarding, Adobe, video scripting. Rare in IDs and it lifts every course.
6. **Modern practice.** Learning analytics to iterate, and genuine AI-assisted development. This is where the field is going.
7. **Independent project management.** You run several builds at once, on a 16-week cycle, without hand-holding.

- 1: the AVC 248 course build (now a case study on the site) + AVC 100 redesign.
- 2: Quality Matters and OLC/OSCQR lead reviewer since 2011. LEAD WITH THIS. It is exactly the rigor signal.

- 7: runs concurrent redesigns, a 10-college study, and builds to deadline.

## Lead order (what goes first, everywhere: resume summary, cover letter, interview open)

Instructional designer / learning experience designer who has **designed and built online courses for decades**, with a Quality Matters/OSCQR rigor standard, a designer's eye (BFA, Adobe), and real AI-assisted building. NOT "community college faculty." NOT "instructional technologist who knows STEM." They do not need a STEM person who knows instructional technology; they need an **instructional-design expert who builds courses**. Every document opens on that sentence.

## The perceived negatives, handled honestly (do not spiral, do not volunteer)

- **Community college.** Not a weakness for this role, an asset. CC means high teaching loads, the most diverse adult learners, accessibility-first, doing more with less. Mines Online serves **working professional adults**, exactly who she already designs for. Own it: "I have designed for the highest-volume, hardest-to-serve adult learners in the country." Being LOCAL in Golden is a plus, name it once.
- **The "they are a high-level university" worry.** Her craft is discipline-agnostic. Translating complex material into learning that works is the job in any discipline. Mines Online is graduate/professional, not 18-year-old prodigies. She designs the learning experience; the faculty SME owns the physics.
- **Age / longevity.** Reframe as EXPERTISE and stability: decades of building courses is the exact asset. Seniority reads as judgment, reliability, and mentor capacity. Her AI builds and vibe-coding prove she is current, which beats most candidates half her age. **Do NOT mention retirement timing or age, ever.** It is private, they cannot ask, and it can only hurt. If the worry leaks into her tone it becomes real, so the counter is confidence in the expertise.
- **Energy.** Not the frame. Substitute "focus, reliability, a track record of shipping." She finishes things. That is what a manager actually wants.

## Interview, concrete moves

- **Show, do not tell.** Bring the AVC 248 build and the interactive assessment demo. A live thing plus a two-minute "problem, what I built, what I learned" beats any answer.
- **Speak their language:** OLED, MODLs, Foundations of Curriculum Design, the Online Course Design Guide (course map), DesignPLUS, HIVE, Quality Matters, backward design, UDL.
- **The frame is "extend, not indict":** "You already run a strong OLED/Trefny build process; here is the next rung, examinable, AI-resistant simulation-as-assessment, built to drop into Canvas."
- **Ask to see a sample MODL / course shell.** Signals she knows the process and gets ground truth.
- **Have three stories ready:** (1) a course she built and its outcome; (2) a time she guided a resistant or overwhelmed faculty SME to a finished course; (3) how she used data to change a design.
- **Close on fit + local + longevity of contribution**, not on needing the job.
