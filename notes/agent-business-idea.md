# Agent setup as a side business (idea, parked)

Goal: test whether the agents Michelle builds could become a side business or a real one.
Audience: small businesses, and job seekers.
Process: sit down with a small business, ask what they do every week, and set up agents to run those tasks for them. For job seekers: job search agents, resume building agents and similar.

Status: idea only, not started. Thinking time set aside for the weekend of Sat 31 Oct and Sun 1 Nov.

Questions to stress-test before committing:
- Who pays, and how much? A small business setup fee vs. a monthly fee to keep agents running.
- Who maintains the agents when tools, sites or logins change? Ongoing support is the hidden cost.
- Liability and data: client logins, customer data, mistakes an agent makes on their behalf.
- Conflict with GCC and ASU employment, and time against the job search.
- Is anyone already selling this locally (AI automation consultants, virtual assistant firms)?
