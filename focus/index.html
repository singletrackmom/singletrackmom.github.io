Do this · step by step

Photograph visiting families in the mountains

 ~$350 to $600 / session

Your own business · family and couple mini-sessions for tourists

Families visit Golden, Evergreen, and Rocky Mountain National Park and want photos while they are here. You are already in the perfect spot. Here is exactly how to set it up, in order:

1. Make one new page on your photography website. Title it “Colorado Family Photos While You Visit.” Put 8 of your best outdoor family and couple shots on it. 2. Write three short lines: what it is (a one-hour photo session for visiting families), where (Golden foothills, Evergreen Lake, Lookout Mountain, Rocky Mountain National Park, Estes Park), and the price (start at $350 for one hour and 30 edited photos). 3. Set up a free Google Business Profile at business.google.com. Use your business name. Category: Photographer. Service area: Golden plus Evergreen, Estes Park, and RMNP. Add 10 photos. Text three past clients and ask them to leave a Google review this week. 4. Make two Pinterest boards: “Rocky Mountain National Park family photos” and “Colorado vacation photos.” Pin 10 of your photos to each, and link every pin back to the new page from step 1. 5. When someone books: meet at the spot, shoot one hour, deliver 30 edited photos in an online gallery, and ask them to leave a Google review. Done.

List a photo session on Airbnb

 Airbnb sends the clients

Airbnb Services · photography is a category again as of 2025

Airbnb brought back Experiences and Services in 2025, and photography is one of the categories. Guests staying near Golden can book a session with you right inside the app, and Airbnb handles the payment. Steps:

1. Go to airbnb.com and search “host a Service,” then choose the Photography category. (Or Google “Airbnb host a photography service” and click their host page.) 2. Create the listing. Title: “Vacation Photo Session in Golden and the Foothills.” Length: one hour. Price: start at $350. Upload your 10 best outdoor photos. 3. Set your meeting spots: downtown Golden, Lookout Mountain, the Red Rocks area, and Evergreen Lake. List the days and times you are free. 4. Submit it for Airbnb to approve. Once it is live, when a guest books, meet them, shoot one hour, and deliver the gallery. Airbnb pays you, so there is no invoicing. 5. After each session, ask the guest to leave an Airbnb review. Reviews are what get you the next booking.

Get more weddings from venues that already love you

 Warm referrals, free

Your own preferred-vendor relationships

You are already a preferred vendor at great venues. Couples ask these venues who to hire, so staying top of mind there is the fastest path to more weddings. Work the ones that actually send you couples, not the ones that do not. Steps:

1. Email each venue coordinator a short, warm note this week: Boettcher Mansion, Mount Vernon Canyon Club, Evergreen Lake House, The Golden Hotel, Table Mountain Inn, Footprints at Fossil Trace, The Vista at Applewood, and Red Rocks. Thank them for keeping you on their list, and attach 5 recent photos from a wedding you shot at THAT venue so they have fresh images to show couples. 2. Fix up your profile on The Knot. Make sure the photos and packages are current, then text your last three couples and ask each to leave you a Knot review. Reply to every Knot inquiry within an hour, that response time is most of the battle. 3. Send the planners who actually refer you (Dotting the i, Paige Lane Designs, and the others on your preferred list) a gallery from a wedding you shot together so they have your images to share, and offer to grab coffee. 4. Do NOT spend time chasing Harper Bond or Ivory Events. They recommend other photographers and have never sent you a couple. Put that energy into the venues and planners above who do.
