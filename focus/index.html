Get certified as a bike mechanic
  • You don’t need a certificate to build bikes, but shops pay mechanics more, and training is often listed as “preferred.”
  • Close to home: Bikes Together in Denver runs a 4-part mechanics series (four weekly 2-hour classes, no experience needed, scholarships available). The cost isn’t posted, and the shop is closed from November 22 through the end of 2026 while it moves, so 2027 dates aren’t set yet. Check their class page in January.
  • The U of Q Bicycle Institute (formerly Barnett) closed in 2023, so there’s no professional mechanic school in Colorado right now.
  • The nearest professional course is United Bicycle Institute in Ashland, Oregon: Professional Repair and Shop Operation, two weeks, $2,800 total. Next sessions: December 7 to 18, January 11 to 22, and February 8 to 19.
  • Many shops (including Mike’s Bikes and Trek above) train builders on the job, so ask about training when you apply.

Drone license

Get your drone license (FAA Part 107)
  • Any drone flight for work or business, including real estate, architecture and wedding photos, needs the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate.
  • Study is free on the FAA’s site: the Remote Pilot Study Guide, the Airman Certification Standards and sample test questions.
  • First get an FAA Tracking Number by creating a profile in IACRA. Then book the knowledge test through the FAA’s testing partner, PSI, at an FAA-approved testing center. It’s taken in person with a government photo ID. PSI lists the fee as $175, and it can vary by location.
  • Nearby testing center: McAir Aviation at Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport, in Broomfield, which says on its own site that it gives drone pilot tests. Other Denver-area centers show up in PSI’s locator.
  • After passing, finish the application in IACRA. A free online refresher course is required every 24 months.
  • Register the drone at FAADroneZone under Part 107: $5 per drone, good for three years.
  • Flying in controlled airspace near an airport needs an authorization through LAANC. Golden sits near Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport, so check the airspace map before every flight.
  • Wedding venues and some parks have their own drone rules, so check before the day.
  • This week: 3 of 6 photo postings mentioned drone work (1 requires a drone and Part 107, 2 list Part 107 as preferred).
