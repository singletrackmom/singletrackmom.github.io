Get certified as a bike mechanic
  • You don’t need a certificate to build bikes, but shops pay mechanics more, and training is often listed as “preferred.”
  • Close to home: Bikes Together in Denver runs a four-part mechanics series (four weekly 2-hour classes, no experience needed, scholarships available). Classes are done for 2026 and the shop is closed until early 2027 while it moves; no price is posted. Check the class page in January.
  • The U of Q Bicycle Institute in Colorado Springs (formerly Barnett) closed its classroom training in 2023, so there’s no professional mechanic school in Colorado right now.
  • The nearest professional course is United Bicycle Institute in Ashland, Oregon: Professional Repair and Shop Operation, two weeks, $2,550 tuition plus a registration fee (UBI’s pages say $150 or $250). Next sessions: December 7 to 18, January 11 to 22, February 8 to 19 and March 8 to 19.
  • Many shops (Mike’s Bikes above, for one) train builders on the job, so ask about training when you apply.

Drone license

Get your drone license (FAA Part 107)
  • Any drone flight for pay, including real estate, architecture and wedding photos, needs the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate.
  • Study is free on the FAA’s site: the Remote Pilot Study Guide, the Airman Certification Standards and sample test questions. Since October 26, 2026 the test also uses chart images, so practice reading sectional charts.
  • First get an FAA Tracking Number by making a profile in IACRA. Then book the knowledge test through PSI, the FAA’s testing partner, at an FAA-approved testing center. It’s in person with a government photo ID, and the FAA says centers charge about $175.
  • PSI shows the Denver-area testing centers when you book (we couldn’t confirm a specific center this week).
  • After passing, finish the application in IACRA. A free online refresher course is due every 24 months.
  • Register the drone on FAADroneZone under Part 107: $5 per drone, good for three years. It also has to meet Remote ID.
  • Flying in controlled airspace near an airport needs an authorization through LAANC. Golden sits near Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport, so check the airspace map before every flight.
  • Wedding venues and some parks have their own drone rules, so check before the day.
  • This week: 4 of 12 photo postings mentioned drone work (1 requires a drone and Part 107, 3 list Part 107 as preferred).
