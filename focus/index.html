Spare-time income, from a laptop

Legit AI-training and research gigs you can do on your own hours. Real companies that pay you, never a fee to join.

AI training & research tasks (rate AI answers, write, take research studies)

 ~$15 to $40/hr, varies

Remote and flexible · work anywhere with wifi, no set schedule

Real companies that hire people to improve AI by reviewing and rating answers, writing, and taking research studies. Good for picking up an hour here and there. The most reputable, no fee to join: DataAnnotation.tech (~$20 to $40/hr, beginner-friendly, pays via PayPal), Prolific (research studies, ~$8 to $15/hr, easiest to start), Outlier (higher pay for writing and technical work), and Mercor. The honest part: income is uneven, busy stretches then quiet, so treat it as flexible side cash, not a paycheck.

The scam test (this matters): a legit one never charges you to join, never asks for your bank login or password, and never mails you a check to deposit. They ask for a PayPal email or direct-deposit info only to PAY you, after you pass a sample task. Anything asking for money up front, gift cards, or onboarding over Telegram or WhatsApp is a scam, walk away.
