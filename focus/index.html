Safety rule for every freelance and per-shoot listing on this page. A real client or platform never asks you to pay to join, never asks for an equipment or training fee, and never mails you a check to deposit and wire part of it back. Anything asking for money up front, gift cards, or onboarding over Telegram or WhatsApp is a scam, walk away.

Growth ideas for slow wedding years

A standing section, refreshed every run. Not a job hunt, a plan to make the business earn when the wedding calendar is thin.

The idea in one line. Build a Colorado destination portrait business for families and couples who are already here on vacation, so the work comes to the mountains you already shoot instead of competing for Denver couples. Everything below serves that.
Do this one first

One web page per destination

 Free · highest leverage

Go past Denver, Golden, and Boulder into the places visitors actually stay: Rocky Mountain National Park and Estes Park, Grand Lake, Winter Park, and Frisco, Breckenridge, and Dillon. One real page each on bergbergphotography.com, named for the town, with your own photos of that town, what a session there costs, where you meet, and the best light. This does double duty now: it ranks on Google the old way, and it is what the AI tools read when a family in Texas asks “can we get family photos while we are in Breckenridge.” A single page covering “Colorado mountain portraits” will not do that. Seven small specific pages will.

Confirmed open this run

Airbnb photography service, apply as a host

 Bookings · 15% fee

Airbnb Services · photography is one of ten categories

Front Range is live · verified August 27

This is the one that is still a real to-do, and it is confirmed open today, not invite-only and not waitlisted. Airbnb relaunched Experiences and Services in 2025 and photography is a category, so guests staying nearby can book a session as an add-on to the stay. Boulder and Denver metro photographer pages already exist, so the market is switched on here. You need at least fifteen portfolio photos, two or more years of experience, and possibly proof of license and insurance, and listings go through a review before publishing. It is free to submit and Airbnb takes 15 percent of each booking. Honest read: the fee is real and the volume is unproven, but it puts you in front of visiting families at the exact moment they are planning their trip, which is the whole strategy above.

Paid ads, aimed at the feeder markets

 Costs money

Two different jobs, so run them differently. Meta ads on Instagram and Facebook build demand in the markets that send visitors here: Dallas, Houston, and Austin first, then Oklahoma, Kansas, Nebraska, Arizona, and Chicago. Google Search ads catch the people already looking, so bid on the high-intent phrases like “Estes Park family photographer” rather than anything broad. Do not start ads until the destination pages exist, or you are paying to send people to a page that does not answer their question.

Referral partnerships in the mountain towns

 Free · slow build

The offline version of the Airbnb hookup, and it does not cost 15 percent. Lodges, resorts, vacation rental managers, and wedding venues in Estes Park, Grand Lake, Winter Park, and Summit County all talk to guests before the trip. Give them something to hand over: a small card or a link for a session while the family is in town. Start with the places you have already shot at, since they have seen your work.

Earn from the archive, and turn wedding contacts into elopements

 Passive + warm

Twenty years of Colorado frames are already shot and already paid for. Adobe Stock is the place to start, and you know someone who does it, so ask for the warm intro before you upload anything and save yourself the beginner mistakes. Separately, your existing wedding contacts are the cheapest source of destination work you have: the couples you shot are now the families booking sessions, and the venues you know are the ones handling elopements.
