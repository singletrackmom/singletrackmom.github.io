Mortgage Field Inspector (1099)

~$5 – 15 per inspection

FAR Inspections

Denver County, CO and surrounding · set your own schedule

Honest read: this is a real company (Reno, NV, 9+ years) in a legitimate industry, not a scam, but go in clear-eyed. You are a 1099 contractor for the inspection firm, which holds the contracts with the lenders and servicers, so you do not partner with a lender yourself. The work is per-inspection: drive to a property, verify occupancy and condition, photograph it on a phone app, submit. Each takes only a few minutes, but pay is modest and per-inspection (commonly around $5 to $15), there is no mileage reimbursement, and online reviews are mixed on pay and how steady the work is. Worth it as fill-in income close to home, not a main paycheck. Safety rule for any gig like this: never pay an upfront fee and never cash a check then wire money back, a real inspection company never asks for that.
