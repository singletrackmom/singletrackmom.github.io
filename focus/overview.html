Focus

Personal Agents · Case study

OverviewLive toolPRD

A daily agent that keeps one private board of real, currently open jobs near home for a working photographer, verified on each employer’s own site.

Goal

Put a real, apply-today job in front of the reader every morning, one that fits the work and clears a firm pay floor, so there is always a concrete next step during slow stretches between bookings.

Audience

One working freelance photographer. General job boards are full of stale listings and roles that are not the work, so scrolling them is a daily time sink that rarely surfaces a real fit.

Process

A scheduled agent rebuilds the board every morning. It sweeps employers’ own careers pages across photo and creative work, outdoor and hands-on jobs, bike shops and bike brands, production and manufacturing, driving, local fill-in work and remote roles, confirms each posting is live that day, drops anything closed, and leads with the best of what is open. Every card carries a short note on why it fits and a direct link to the posting.

Technology

A scheduled AI agent working from a written rules file, publishing a static, noindexed page with no logins, forms or tracking.

Outcomes

In daily use. The board began as a photo-gig list and widened once it was clear that steady income between bookings comes from many kinds of work near home, all held to the same pay floor and the same live-posting check.
