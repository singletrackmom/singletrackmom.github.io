Focus

A private job board, refreshed daily by a small AI agent.

Product Requirements · one of the family job-search agents · Michelle B.

Goal

Surface real, currently-open jobs near home that fit the work and clear the pay floor, so there is something to apply to the same day, plus a few legit spare-time and grow-the-business moves for the slow weeks.

Audience

One photographer with a deep skill set: staged product, parts, and architectural photography and professional event work, plus a production-manager background from the action-sports world. Roles must be within about 30 minutes of Golden and clear a $20-an-hour floor. No influencer or content-creator gigs.

Process

  1. Sweep employers’ own careers pages for openings that fit, full-time and part-time, kept in separate groups.
  2. Verify each posting is live that day; anything that cannot be confirmed is left off.
  3. Write a short, honest why-it-fits for each, with the apply link going straight to the real posting.
  4. Keep a standing set of grow-the-business ideas and vetted spare-time income options for the slow stretches.
Only real, verified-live postings make the board. Pay floors and fit rules are enforced, and nothing that cannot be confirmed as a live job is shown.
