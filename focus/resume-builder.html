Make a resume & cover letter for any job

Agentic & personal build

You do not need any special app. You just copy a set of instructions, paste them into a free AI chat, add the job, and it writes both for you. Your resume is already inside the instructions.

This copies everything, including your resume.

  1. Tap the brown button above. It copies your instructions (nothing seems to happen except a “Copied” note, that is normal).
  2. Open a free AI chat. Go to claude.ai and start a new chat (ChatGPT or Gemini work too). A free login is all you need. Open Claude ›
  3. Paste and send. Tap the message box, paste (hold down and choose Paste, or Ctrl+V), and send it.
  4. Add the job. Copy the job posting, or the text from the job’s web page, and paste it into the chat as your next message. Send it. If you have the company name, include it. You do not need the street address.
  5. Read what it writes. It gives you a resume and a cover letter made for that job.
  6. Ask for changes. Type things like “make it shorter,” “add that I do drone work,” or “more formal.” It redoes them until you are happy.
  7. Copy them out. Copy the resume and the cover letter into a document, and apply.

Your instructions (you do not need to read this)

This is exactly what the button copies. If the button does not work, tap inside the box, select all, and copy it by hand.

One step at a time. You cannot break anything.
