You do not need to know anything about AI for this. Everything is already loaded, including your resume. You just tap one button, paste one job link, and answer a few easy questions in your own words. It does the writing. Follow the steps in order.

Do this

This copies everything you need, including your resume, so you never have to.

  1. Tap the big brown button above. It copies your instructions to your clipboard. You will not see anything happen except a little “Copied” message. That is normal.
  2. Open Claude. Go to claude.ai and start a new chat (or open the Claude app). Open Claude ›
  3. Paste. Tap in the message box, hold down, and choose Paste. Then send it. On a keyboard that is Command+V (Mac) or Control+V (Windows).
  4. Give it the job. It will ask you for the web link (URL) of the job. Copy the job’s web address from your browser and paste it in. The URL is the long text at the very top of the page, starting with https.
  5. Answer its questions. It will ask you a few simple things, one at a time. Just type the answer in your own words. If you do not know one, type “not sure” and keep going. It will ask things like why you fit, and if you have any skills that are not on your resume.
  6. Read what it gives you. It will hand you a finished cover letter and a finished resume, both built for that exact job.
  7. Tell it what to fix. Type things like “make it shorter,” “add that I shoot drone,” or “that part is wrong.” It will redo it until you are happy.
  8. Send it. Copy the final resume and cover letter into a document, or ask it to save them, and apply.
