Lunch

Where to eat on class days, living at Williams Village with classes in the Engineering Center.

← SoarThe planWhere you liveMain campusBusMoney

Every place has a number. The tag says whether it takes a meal swipe or Munch Money, and each card links to CU’s campus map or the place’s own page.

01

The short version

  1. Use a swipe for lunch whenever you can. CU on the Run 4 is grab-and-go, takes a swipe, and makes sandwiches to order. Fifteen swipes a week covers dinner every night, breakfast on weekdays, and lunch on your three long days. That is what they are for. Do this first.
  2. Make your own the rest of the time. Buy bread and fillings once at Village Market 2, keep them in your fridge, and build it in the morning. Cheapest by a mile and you already have to walk past the store.
  3. Buy a made sandwich only when neither works. Village Market 2 opens at 8 a.m. and takes Munch Money. Your $200 is about one bought lunch a week for the semester, so treat it as the backup, not the plan.
  4. If you forget everything: there is a cafe inside the Engineering Center 3. Food is already in your class building.

One catch on number 3. Violet Peak is run by Real Estate Services, not Campus Dining, so it is not on the meal plan list. It probably takes Campus Cash and may take Munch Money, but their page does not say. Ask at the counter the first week. If it only takes a card, it is a paying-out-of-pocket option, not a free one.

06

Getting there, Route W

Your Buff OneCard is your bus pass. Just tap it. Regular fall service starts Thursday, August 20.

Route W runs a clockwise loop and stops at:

  • Bear Creek
  • Williams Village (yours)
  • IMIG Music
  • Physics
  • Math (closest to the Engineering Center)
  • Colorado and 30th, south side

How often: Mon to Fri, every 10 minutes from 7 to 8:30 a.m., then every 5 minutes from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., every 8 minutes until 11 p.m. Weekends every 30 minutes, 10 a.m. to 11 p.m.

Heads up: there is no stop at the Engineering Center and no stop at the C4C anymore. Get off at Math or Physics and walk. Download the Transit app to see where the bus actually is.

07

Money, boxes and first-week questions

Swipes: 15 a week. Dinner 7, weekday breakfast 5, lunch on your 3 long days. That is 15 exactly. It works, but only if you actually spend a swipe on lunch instead of paying for one.

Munch Money: $200 for the whole semester. If a sandwich and a drink run about $12, that is roughly 16 lunches. Across 15 weeks that is about one a week, not three. It is a backup, not a lunch plan. Spend it like that and it lasts.

Groceries are the cheat code. A loaf of bread and a package of turkey from Village Market costs about what two made sandwiches cost and gets you most of a week. You have a fridge. Use it.

Munch Money does not roll over to spring and you do not get it back, so do not hoard it either. Just do not blow it all in September.

About taking a box out of the dining hall

CU has free reusable steel takeout containers through an app called USEFULL, but it will not solve your six-hour day:

  • Only at Alferd Packer Grill (8) and the SEEC Cafe (14). Not at Village Center, not at the C4C, so you cannot box up dinner at your own dining hall.
  • Return within two days or it is $1 a day, then $25 if it is gone two weeks.
  • Not microwave safe. It is stainless steel.
  • Return stations are only in the UMC and the SEEC building, both on main campus, so you would have to carry it back.

Good for carrying a hot lunch across campus the same day. Not a way to stock your fridge.

Four things to ask the first week

  1. Does Violet Peak in the Engineering Center take Munch Money?Ask at the counter. If yes, your lunch problem is completely solved.
  2. Can I use two swipes in one visit at Village Center?If yes, breakfast plus something to carry solves everything before you get on the bus.
  3. Can I take a piece of fruit or a wrapped item out with my meal?The policy is not published anywhere. The staff will know.
  4. Does Village Center do order-ahead?Sewall advertises it. Worth asking whether yours does too.
