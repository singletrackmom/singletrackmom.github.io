Housing

A quiet place of your own near engineering and East Campus. Spring sublets first, then fall by area.

← SoarSpringFall 2027Before you sign

Closest to campus first. Aim for $1,500 a month for a place of your own; never over $1,850 alone or $3,200 for a 2-bedroom. Close to campus beats cheap: a direct bus comes first, and a short bike or scooter ride counts too. If the commute gets long, living at home works. Everything skips University Hill and the big per-bedroom student towers, and CU students ride RTD free with a Buff OneCard. Places go fast, so call before you visit.

Before you sign

  • See it first. Never pay a deposit before a visit or a live video tour. Walk away from anyone who wants a wire, Zelle or gift cards, or who says the owner is out of state.
  • For a sublet, get the landlord’s written OK. Ask who holds the deposit, whether you sign onto the main lease, and the exact end date.
  • Ask the same questions every time: total monthly cost with fees and utilities, furnished or not, parking, laundry, nearest bus stop, and how quiet the building is at night.
  • Skip income-restricted buildings. Most affordable-housing buildings in Boulder don’t accept a household made up only of full-time students, so ask before applying anywhere that lists income limits.
  • Call property managers directly. Park Mesa, St. Moritz, Mountainaire and Creekside are the most likely to offer a true January to May lease.
