Soar

Personal Agents · Case study

OverviewLive toolHousingLunchPRD

A daily agent that brings the field and the first year to an engineering student: aerospace news, clubs, scholarships, internships, campus jobs and housing.

Goal

Support a first-year engineering student through the transition to college in two ways: build a sense of belonging in the field, and take the logistics of the first year off his plate. The field comes to him, with no pressure to network. Scholarships and summer internships surface early enough to act on, and a housing guide turns a lease decision into a side-by-side comparison of cost and commute.

Audience

One first-year engineering student. Research on first-year persistence ties staying in a STEM major closely to early belonging, through clubs, peers and contact with the profession, so the page leads with ways in rather than things to chase.

Process

A scheduled agent rebuilds one living page every morning: internships and scholarships with verified deadlines, aerospace news, launches and sky events, clubs and feeds, campus jobs and gigs, and a drone practice guide. Every link is rechecked and goes straight to the source. Housing compares spring sublets and next-fall options by full monthly cost, with a bar showing door-to-door time to each campus location. Lunch maps where a meal swipe works on long class days.

Technology

A scheduled AI agent working from a written rules file, publishing static, noindexed pages with no logins, forms or tracking.

Outcomes

In daily use. The page first led with news and clubs, because aerospace internships for a first summer post around January and an empty urgent list would have taught its reader to stop opening it. Now that postings are opening, internships lead, and scholarships carry the other live deadlines.

References

Strayhorn, T. L. (2019). College students’ sense of belonging: A key to educational success for all students (2nd ed.). Routledge.
