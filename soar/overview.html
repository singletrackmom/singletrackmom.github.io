Soar

Agentic & personal build

A daily agent that rebuilds an aerospace dashboard for a college-bound engineer: news, launches, clubs, and a live internship watch.

Open Soar → How it works →

Goal

Keep a future aerospace engineer current in his field heading into college, with zero pressure to network or chase things that do not exist yet. Surface a little flexible gig income now, and have the internship rail already in place when applications open in January.

Soar is a personalized, daily-refreshing aerospace dashboard built for one specific college-bound engineering student. It pulls space and aerospace news, upcoming launches to watch, the feeds worth following, and the CU Boulder clubs to walk into this fall, plus a live internship watch that stays quiet until January and a short list of weekend gigs. It is a personal build, an agent that rebuilds the same page in place each morning so the field comes to him.

Audience

A young person heading into aerospace engineering, an introvert who would rather have the field come to him than white-knuckle through networking events. Right now the job is simple: stay current, get settled at school, and show up to a club or two.

Process

A scheduled agent runs each morning to curate the day’s headlines and launches, keep the standing feeds and club lists current, and re-check every link. It is a rolling dashboard, one living page rebuilt in place and built to grow, hosted as a static site with no backend. Links go straight to each source or company, never a job board, and dates and pay labels are treated as general guidance to confirm at the source.
