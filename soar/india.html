India

01

Why January to March

North in January and February, south in February and March.

  • November to March is the cool, dry season across most of India. Delhi, Agra, Varanasi and Rajasthan are at their best now.
  • The south is always hot and gets stifling by May, so be in Kerala and Tamil Nadu between January and March.
  • From April it gets very hot, and the monsoon arrives around June. Delhi’s air quality is also at its worst in winter, so don’t base yourself there.

Visa

The 1-year e-Tourist visa is the one to get.

Multiple entries, valid for a year from approval.

US citizens can stay up to 180 days on each visit, with a cap of 180 days in India per calendar year. That easily covers eleven weeks. It can’t be extended or changed once you’re there, and it doesn’t allow any work. A small bank charge is added when you pay.

03

Stops

The numbers match the map. A suggested order, not a schedule.

1. Delhi

January · arrive here

Fly in, sleep off the jet lag, see Old Delhi and Humayun’s Tomb. Two or three days is plenty.

2. Agra

January

The Taj Mahal at sunrise. An easy train from Delhi. Drones are not allowed near it.

3. Jaipur

January

The Pink City: Amber Fort, the old observatory (Jantar Mantar) and markets. Cool, dry and sunny this time of year.

4. Udaipur

January to February

Lakes and palaces, slower and quieter. A good place to stay a week and catch up on rest.

5. Varanasi

February

The Ganges ghats at dawn by boat. Intense and unforgettable.

6. Rishikesh

February

Himalayan foothills, river trails, hiking and cheap long stays. Cold at night in winter.

7. Mumbai

Late February

Big, fast and coastal. The hub for flights south and west.

8. Goa

Late February to March

Beaches and old Portuguese towns. Warm and dry in January and February, hotter by March.

9. Hampi

March

Boulder fields and temple ruins. Great for hiking and bouldering. Go early in the day, before the heat.

10. Bengaluru

March

India’s tech capital and home of its space agency, ISRO. Worth a few days if you want to see the engineering side of India.

11. Kochi and Kerala

March · fly out from here or Bengaluru

Backwaters, spice markets and the green hills of Munnar. A calm way to end before Japan.

Costs and getting around

Hostel-style travel. Delhi and Mumbai cost more; small towns cost less.
ItemTypical
Daily budget, dorm bedsabout $33

SIM card with data, monthly$5 to $10
  • Trains are the classic way around. Book on the Indian Railways site well ahead; popular routes sell out.
  • Long jumps like Delhi to Mumbai are cheaper in time as a domestic flight.
  • Scams: people at stations may say your train is canceled and steer you to a car. Check with the station office or your app instead.
  • Food and water: drink bottled or filtered water, and eat where it’s busy and cooked fresh.
