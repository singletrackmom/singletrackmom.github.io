Goal

Soar exists to keep one future aerospace engineer current in his field as he heads into college, while quietly surfacing a little flexible gig income now. The problem it solves is not a lack of information, it is the wrong prescription. The standard advice for staying connected to a field is “network,” and for an introvert that advice is exactly backward. Cold outreach and events drain him, and there is nothing concrete to chase yet anyway, aerospace summer internships do not open until January. So the tool does the opposite of pushing him outward: it brings the field to him, one page he opens and reads, no calls to make.

Audience

One person, Jasper, an aerospace-bound CU Boulder student who is an introvert and needs things to come to him rather than chase them down. Right now his real job is small and specific: stay current, get settled at school, and walk into a club or two. The dashboard is built around that, not around some idealized confident networker. It is a private, single-user build tailored to how he actually works, which is why it can stay this simple.

Process

Soar is a rolling dashboard, one living page rebuilt in place each morning, not a dated publication and explicitly not a job-posting service. There is no archive, because the point is to show the current state of the field. A scheduled Claude agent, defined by a SKILL.md spec, runs around 6:00 AM daily, with a catch-up run when he signs on if the machine was off overnight.

SectionWhat it does
Today in aerospaceA few curated headlines worth knowing, each linked straight to its source.
LaunchesUpcoming launches to watch, with dates labeled as general guidance to confirm at the source.
Feeds and clubsThe feeds to follow and the CU Boulder aerospace groups to walk into this fall, linked directly.
Internship watchA running list of Colorado and national aerospace internships that stays quiet until January, then leads.
Gigs and drone guideA short set of weekend drone, DJ, and event gigs, plus a starter drone-practice guide.

Each run curates the day’s headlines and launches, refreshes the standing feeds and club list, keeps the internship watch current so it is ready when applications open, and re-checks links and labels. Everything links to each source or company directly, never to a job board. Launch dates and pay figures are treated as general guidance that changes often, with a note to confirm at the source. It is built for a dark, mission-control look, solid colors only, curly quotes, no em dashes.

Privacy

Soar collects nothing. It is a static page with no forms, logins, or tracking, and it is noindexed and unlinked, a private page just for him. No personal data is stored or transmitted anywhere.

Status

Soar is a personal build for family, in daily use. It is not a pilot, not a product, and not headed for any rollout. It grows over time as sections are added, but it stays a small, single-purpose dashboard for one reader. When January comes, the internship rail is already there, and until then the job is simply to stay current without a single cold call.
