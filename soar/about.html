Soar

A daily aerospace dashboard, kept current by a small AI agent.

Product Requirements · one of the family agents · Michelle B.

Goal

Keep Jasper current in aerospace heading into CU Boulder, the headlines worth knowing, the launches to watch, the feeds to follow, and the clubs to walk into this fall, so the field comes to him without cold calls or networking events.

Audience

Jasper, an aerospace-bound CU Boulder student. The job right now is simply to stay current, get settled at school, and show up to a club or two. Internships do not open until January, so there is nothing to chase yet.

Process

  1. Pull the aerospace headlines, upcoming launches, and feeds worth following.
  2. List the campus and local groups to join this fall, with links straight to each source.
  3. Keep a short set of weekend gigs and drone practice at the bottom to build skills and a little income.
  4. Re-verify links and labels each run; it grows over time, so check back often.
Soar is a personal dashboard and lead board, not a job-posting service. Links go straight to each source. Launch dates and pay labels are general guidance and change often, confirm at the source.
