Coverage dashboard
What is tested, what is not, where the gaps are

One screen to see the whole study at a glance: how much of each college has been walked, how much of each journey stage has been covered, and the worst barriers found so far. Coverage is the share of that college's or that stage's scenarios that have a completed run. The dashboard reads from the scenario-bank tracking sheet, so as testers log results it fills in on its own. The numbers below are sample data, clearly marked, to show the populated state before fieldwork begins.
