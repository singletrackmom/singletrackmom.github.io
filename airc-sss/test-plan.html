What to try if you have time to help: you play a student doing one real task on your own campus and note where you get stuck.

No runs have been completed yet. Do as many or as few tasks as you like. Every logged run adds to the map.

1. How a test works

One persona, one campus, one task. You attempt it from a real felt need. The student is never told where to go, because the question is whether they can find it and where they get stuck. Record where you got stuck, not just whether you eventually succeeded.

2. Use your student account, not your work login

Every Maricopa employee also has a student account. It signs in with your MEID and ends in maricopa.edu, not your college work address. Use the student account so you see exactly what a student sees. A login only reaches its own college’s systems, which is why every campus needs its own coverage.

3. A good set to start with

TaskWhat done looks like
Self-enroll in classes for the next termYou get into the registration system and add at least one class to a cart or schedule.
Drop a class and find the exact refund and withdrawal deadlineYou locate the drop function and the specific dates that affect your money and your transcript.
Log in to student email and find your official messagesYou reach the student inbox and can tell official college mail from noise.
Order an official transcriptYou reach the transcript-ordering flow and see the steps and the cost.
Apply for graduation and run a degree or certificate auditYou find the graduation application and the audit that shows what you still need.

4. A few more, if you are on a roll

5. Log each run

Record each task as a separate submission, right after you run it, while it is fresh. One task equals one submission. Log it in the capture form: open the capture form.

5.1 Logging rules

  • No student PII. Use your own student or test account, initials only, never a real student’s name or ID.
  • Record the barrier: where you got stuck, how many dead ends, whether you needed a human, and a severity score.
  • Capture the local name your college uses for the service. Names are translated, never standardized.
  • If you would have given up as that student, that is a finding. Log it and move on.
