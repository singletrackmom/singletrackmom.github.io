Test Plan

If you have time to help, here is what to try. You play a student doing one real task on your own campus and note where you get stuck. Do as many or as few as you like, every bit helps.

How a test works: one persona, one campus, one task. You attempt it from a real felt need. We never tell the student where to go; the point is whether they can find it and where they get stuck. Record where you got stuck, not just whether you eventually succeeded.
Use your student account, not your work login. Every Maricopa employee also has a student account. It signs in with your MEID and ends in @maricopa.edu, not your @yourcollege.edu work address. Use the student account so you see exactly what a student sees. A login only reaches its own college’s systems, which is why we need every campus covered.

A good set to start with

TaskWhat “done” looks like
Self-enroll in classes for the next term. CoreYou get into the registration system and add at least one class to a cart or schedule.
Drop a class and find the exact refund and withdrawal deadline. CoreYou locate the drop function and the specific dates that affect your money and your transcript.
Log in to student email and find your official messages. CoreYou reach the student inbox and can tell official college mail from noise.
Order an official transcript. CoreYou reach the transcript-ordering flow and see the steps and cost.
Apply for graduation and run a degree or certificate audit. CoreYou find the graduation application and the audit that shows what you still need.

A few more, if you are on a roll

TaskWhat “done” looks like
Get your MEID and first login working (password reset, DUO). MoreYou can reset a password and pass the DUO step without calling anyone.
Find when your financial aid disburses and when a refund lands. MoreYou find the actual dates in the student center, not a generic help page.
Set up a payment plan or pay a bill. MoreYou reach the payment-plan enrollment or the pay-my-bill screen.
Add a class after the term has started. MoreYou find the late-add path and what approval it needs.
Save your email, Drive, and coursework before the account closes. MoreYou find what happens to your account after graduation and how to export first.

Log each run

Record each task as a separate submission, right after you run it, while it is fresh. One task equals one submission.

Logging rules:
  • No student PII. Use your own student or test account, initials only, never a real student’s name or ID.
  • Record the barrier: where you got stuck, how many dead ends, whether you needed a human, and a severity of 1 to 5.
  • Capture the local name your college uses for the service. We translate names, we never ask colleges to standardize.
  • If you would have given up as that student, that is a finding. Log it and move on.
