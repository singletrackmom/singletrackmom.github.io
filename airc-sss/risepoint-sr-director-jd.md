## Full job description

**Senior Director, Digital and AI Tech Product Management** · Risepoint · US Remote · Full time · Req JR101253 · Posted 9 days ago (as of June 19, 2026).

Risepoint is an education technology company that provides support and expertise to more than 100 universities and colleges, primarily regional universities, helping them develop and grow high-ROI, workforce-focused online degree programs (nursing, teaching, business, public service). Mission: increase access to affordable education so more students, especially working adults, can improve their careers and meet employer and community needs.

### The Impact You Will Make
Risepoint is poised to transform the organization using technology and an AI-first mindset. Seeking a Sr Director of Digital Product & AI Strategy to lead the discovery, definition, and development of technology solutions that enhance the end-to-end lifecycle of online university programs. Own AI/digital use case development, work cross-functionally to drive build vs buy decisions, and identify solutions with a rigorous, ROI-first lens. Uncover friction points universities face, from student discovery and application to enrollment, persistence, and graduation, and turn those insights into scalable digital products that enhance outcomes for students and institutional partners. Strategically focused role, no direct reports in the initial year.

### What You Will Do

University Lifecycle Opportunity Identification & Product Strategy
- Conduct deep discovery to identify pain points, inefficiencies, and unmet needs across the online program lifecycle (marketing, recruitment, admissions, onboarding, student support, academic progression).
- Analyze partner processes, systems, personas, and workflows to uncover opportunities for innovation and digital transformation.

AI Strategy & Use Case Development
- Own the tech transformation roadmap (AI/Digital): intake, prioritization, sequencing of a focused set of high-impact initiatives.
- Identify and prioritize a small number of high-value opportunities tied to measurable outcomes (conversion, cost-to-serve, speed, quality).
- Evaluate build vs. buy decisions and assess AI/digital tools and vendors with a rigorous, ROI-first lens.
- Develop a measurement framework to baseline performance before AI/digital interventions and track impact post-deployment.

Workflow Transformation
- Redesign workflows end-to-end using a problem-led, future-back approach (not just layering AI or digital tools onto existing processes).
- Define how roles, tasks, and decision-making shift with AI embedded.
- Partner with functions to move from insight to pilot to scaled workflow changes.
- Ensure solutions are durable, repeatable, and embedded into day-to-day operations.

Product Strategy & Roadmapping
- Develop and maintain a clear product vision, strategy, and multi-quarter roadmap aligned with business goals, partner needs, and the student experience.
- Define success metrics and use experiments, insights, and analytics to drive continuous improvement.
- Translate university pain points into solutions.

Stakeholder Engagement & Cross-Functional Leadership
- Build strong relationships with university partners to understand operational models, technical ecosystems, decision structures, and growth goals.
- Collaborate with partnerships, engineering, operations, marketing, and student success teams to shape product requirements and deliver scalable solutions.

Execution & Delivery
- Own the end-to-end product development lifecycle, from discovery and prioritization through launch and iteration.
- Ensure documentation, communication, and change management to support adoption.
- Lead requirement definition (user stories, acceptance criteria, systems integration needs, user flows).
- Work with technical teams to ensure delivery meets scope, timeline, quality, performance.

Leadership & Influence
- Operate autonomously with minimal oversight; proactively identify issues and drive solutions.
- Influence decisions using data-driven reasoning, storytelling, and strong executive presence.

### What Success Looks Like
- Identify and deliver high-impact digital and AI-enabled solutions that reduce friction in the partner journey, improve operational efficiency, enhance the partner and student experience, and drive measurable business outcomes.
- Build and execute a clear product and AI strategy roadmap that aligns stakeholders, prioritizes highest-value opportunities, and moves initiatives from discovery through implementation and adoption.
- Redesign workflows and business processes in a scalable, sustainable way by embedding technology and AI into day-to-day operations, improving speed, quality, decision-making, and effectiveness.

### How Impact Will Be Measured
- Develop and gain alignment on a multi-quarter digital product and enterprise AI roadmap with clear business outcomes and success metrics.
- Lead discovery, design, and implementation of a focused set of digital and AI-enabled workflow transformation initiatives that improve efficiency, UX, speed, quality, or cost-to-serve.
- Establish cross-functional partnerships and scalable product management practices (repeatable intake, prioritization, measurement, stakeholder alignment, delivery).

### What You'll Bring (key reqs)
- 8+ years tech product management, significant time in digital product leadership (must have).
- Strong knowledge of university systems and online program operations (CRM, SIS, LMS, admissions workflows, student support models, marketing funnels), via EdTech firms or universities (must have).
- Proven experience (last 2 to 4 years) driving AI-led product development and workflow transformation inside an organization, not just deploying tools or pilots; point to workflows that changed and outcomes achieved (must have).
- Builder mentality, strong ownership, comfortable with ambiguity and establishing new processes.
- Nice to have: top-tier consulting background, product strategy/roadmap track record, executive communication, analytical rigor, higher-ed experience, familiarity with integrations/APIs/middleware/data pipelines.
