the district AI Resource Center · Student Support & Success (Domain 5)

Our goals, and the subgroup under each

A proposal for our next meeting. Draft, please push on it.

How we will work. Measured and deliberate, not AI for its own sake. We recommend and pilot ideas to prove they help before anything scales, we do it with the staff whose work it touches, and we never force a change on anyone. Most subgroups run small, two or three people, led by a domain member who can invite subject-matter experts that work only within the subgroup and skip the main domain meetings. The on-campus usability testing is the exception: it has a member at every college, since the in-person visits happen campus by campus. We use the domain meeting for updates and sharing out.

Goal 1 · Run the gap-analysis study

Map where students get stuck reaching the support that already exists, across all ten colleges, and rank the gaps by how many students each one affects. This is the evidence everything else rests on.

No subgroup

Run by the co-chair

The co-chair runs the study, with Strategy, Security and Governance (Domain 1) overseeing safety and data governance. This one is the co-chair’s to carry, not a subgroup.

Goal 2 · Test on campus with real people

Validate the study in person, at the offices students actually walk into, so the findings hold up beyond what the online testing alone can show.

Subgroup

On-campus usability testing

The one subgroup that spans all ten colleges: it has a member at every campus. If you are at a college, you are on it for your campus. Each member does the in-person visits to the offices at their own campus and records what really happens at the counter, checking it against the online findings.

Goal 3 · Recommend AI fixes, and pilot them before scaling

For the highest-priority gaps, recommend where AI could help, a small tool we build, an existing tool plugged into a system we already use, or a workflow or agent built with the department, then pilot the most promising to prove it works before anything scales. With staff, human in the loop, no student data.

Subgroup

Solutions and pilots

Turns the ranked gaps into recommended fixes and designs and runs the small pilots that prove them. Invites the department SMEs whose services a fix would touch. This is the group that keeps us measured and staff-led.

Goal 4 · Grow student AI literacy with Teaching and Learning

Partner with the Teaching and Learning domain so students learn to use AI responsibly, and explore an AI-focused institutional learning outcome so those skills are recognized district-wide.

Liaison, one person

Teaching and Learning liaison

A single liaison, not a group, who carries the relationship with Teaching and Learning and scopes whether the AI institutional outcome is worth pursuing this year.

Goal 5 · Build and fund student project teams

Get students involved right away and start the funding search now, then design the structure, policies, and paid opportunities and pilot a first team.

Phase 1: get students in, find the money, build the framework · Phase 2: create and pilot one team · Phase 3: scale it so any college or the district can use it, for work beyond AI too.
Subgroup

Student teams and funding

Works out what it takes to stand up student project teams: structure, support, policies, and the money. Opens the funding search now with the campus grant office and looks at AmeriCorps stipends, and invites internship or HR staff as needed.

Start now, do not wait for the framework. Bring one or two students into the domain meetings from the start of Fall for direct input, open the funding conversation with the grant office right away, and aim to create and pilot a first team rather than plan it indefinitely.

For discussion

  • The AI institutional learning outcome: pursue this year, or park it until the study has run?
