1. Findings, the Part 1 results are now in

The old draft had a single first-batch note. It now reports the full Part 1 run as three batches with real numbers.

2. Findings, new subsection on the enrollment funnel

A brand-new subsection, “The enrollment funnel, walked end to end,” carries the headline finding from walking all three personas through the full funnel.

  • All three personas hit the same login wall at the Register step.
  • The MEID that unlocks everything is created silently inside the application and never explained, so there is unexplained demand at four separate steps.
  • Residency and cost is the highest-anxiety and most-broken stage (legal-first language, no visible price, a page that can fail to load on a phone).
  • The wins are credited too, so it does not read as one-sided.

3. Guardrail fix you caught

Was: language about “renaming a service to student language.”

Now: the college keeps its own name. The fix is a student-language synonym in search and page metadata, plus a chatbot that maps the felt need to the local name. Nothing is renamed anywhere in the document.

4. Recommendations, new subsection tying findings to fixes

Added “What the Part 1 batches point to.” It lists the five recurring gaps, each with a cheapest-first menu of candidate fixes (getting in the door, finding a service, reaching a person, using a service, the account-and-cost wall). Framed honestly as directions the evidence points to, not selected pilots; the reach-ranking rule still decides the order once the full study is scored. Every option is screened against the no-student-data line.
