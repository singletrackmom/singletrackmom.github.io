Agenda

  1. Welcome and quick names. A fast round, especially for anyone new to the committee.
  2. Gordon: our focus and goals. Gordon opens by reining us in on what Domain 5 should be prioritizing and what success looks like, including his thoughts on training and on working with the people already doing training across the district. This sets our direction and keeps our scope tight.
  3. Why this domain exists, briefly. For anyone new: the ARC is working out how AI can genuinely help across Maricopa. Domain 5 is Student Support and Success, the student journey from applying to graduating, and making services easier to find and use. The goal is to take routine, repetitive questions off staff so people are freed for the human work only they can do. No one is being replaced.
  4. Where our study stands. Service crosswalk mapped across all 10 colleges; the usability test form is built. Honest status: nothing has been tested yet.
  5. Carried over from prior meetings. Salesforce: still no response from their team to set up a meeting or a rundown of what Salesforce and its admissions bot actually do. The staff-training thread and the student AI-literacy and buy-in ideas are also still open, and connect to Gordon’s focus point.
  6. Wrap and next steps. Quick recap and when we meet next.
  7. The test form: what to do. This is the one concrete way to help. When you have about 30 minutes this week (or now, if there’s time), open the test plan, take the persona you’re given, and try one real task on your own campus. Use your own student account (MEID, @maricopa.edu), not your work login, so you see what a student sees. Note where you get stuck and log each task in the form. One task helps; do as many as you like. Going into fall we’d also love a volunteer per campus and one test-student account per college. It is all optional, and AI can run some of the tests too.
The test form and how-to: the short instructions and the task list live on their own page, so we don’t read through it in the meeting. It also has the link for logging what you find.
