Course Schedule, Spring 2027

Fifteen weeks plus spring break, four projects, a Design History project and a portfolio. Fully online and asynchronous.

InstructionJan 16 to May 9
DueThursday and Sunday, noon
DeliveryOnline, asynchronous
Credits3

Everything is due at noon, Arizona time. Nothing is ever due at midnight.

There are two deadlines a week, Thursday and Sunday; Week 1 has one, on Thursday. Projects and project steps (Process Work and Projects) are posted to the class Discord by noon the day before they are due, and you leave three critique comments on classmates’ work by the due time. Exercises, readings, quizzes and reflections have no Discord step. Late work is accepted for 24 hours after a deadline with a 10 percent deduction. Nothing is accepted after noon the day after the due date. When a holiday falls on a deadline or its hard close, the deadline moves a day earlier, so nothing is due when campus is closed.

Comments on classmates who post during the 24-hour window are encouraged and ungraded. They are good community citizenship, not extra credit.

How the grade breaks down

Grading groups (weighted)
GroupWhat is in itWeight
Process WorkEvery project step, 10 points each, submitted with process evidence40%
ProjectsEach project final, 30 points each35%
ExercisesReadings, quizzes and reflections, 5 to 10 points each25%

Process outweighs the finished piece on purpose. Designers who show only final work cannot explain their decisions, and explaining decisions is the job.

Questions

Ask software and project questions in #avc183-graphic-design-1 on the Digital Media Arts Discord. For anything private, use Canvas Inbox. Both receive a reply within one business day.
