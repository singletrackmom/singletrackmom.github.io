Course Schedule, Spring 2027

Fifteen weeks plus spring break, four projects, and a design history thread. Fully online and asynchronous.

Read this once and you will not lose points all semester.

The date you see in Canvas is not the date your work has to be finished.

Every Canvas deadline covers two things at once: the assignment and the critique. What you actually turn in is one Miro page showing your work posted on Discord, the feedback you got back, and your comments on your classmates.

None of that can exist if you post at 11:55 on the due date. So:

Canvas deadlines are Thursday and Sunday at noon. Your original work goes on Discord by Wednesday and Saturday. The Canvas date is when the whole package is due, work plus critique. Every row below lists both dates.

You have 3 to 4 days between deadlines to make the work, plus the feedback window. Nothing is ever due at midnight.

How you turn work in, every step

  • Post your work to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord, a day or two before the deadline. Use a PNG or JPG, never a PDF, so people can see it without downloading.
  • Comment on 3 to 4 classmates’ posts. The assignment tells you which.
  • Screenshot your post and your comments and compile them in Miro as one page.
  • Submit the Miro screenshot to Canvas by noon. That is your process grade.
  • Submit the final file exactly as the assignment names it.

Ask software and project questions on the Discord channel, not by email or direct message. Public answers help everyone.

Process outweighs the finished piece on purpose. You cannot skip to the finish.

What holds all semester

  • Sketch by hand before you touch the computer. Every project.
  • Never stretch or distort type. It is scored on every project.
  • Accessibility is part of every critique, not a separate unit.
