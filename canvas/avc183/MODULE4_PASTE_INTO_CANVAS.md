```html
<p style="margin:0;">The Canvas submission includes both your assignment and your peer critique. Your work must be posted to Discord one day before the Canvas due date so that classmates have time to respond.</p>
</div>
<h3>Objectives</h3>
<p>By the end of this assignment, you will be able to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Interpret the logo, color, and typography rules of a professional brand guide.</li>
<li>Generate a range of poster layout concepts by hand that respect the brand’s clear-space requirements.</li>
<li>Evaluate classmates’ concepts and identify the strongest layout.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Project Brief</h3>
<p>The City of Milwaukee, Office of Visitor Welcome, will host a group of international graduate researchers who are spending the semester in Milwaukee studying Lake Michigan. The city has requested a welcome package that introduces the researchers to Milwaukee and helps them feel at home. All work must follow the <a href="https://city.milwaukee.gov/BrandGuidelines" target="_blank" rel="noopener">City of Milwaukee Brand Guidelines</a> exactly.</p>
<p>This project has four steps. You will design a welcome poster and an Instagram advertisement, then two welcome-kit items of your choice, and finish by revising all four pieces from critique and presenting them as a final set.</p>
<h3>Brand Requirements</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Logo:</strong> Use only the logo files downloaded from the brand guide. Do not redraw, stretch, recolor, add effects to, or crowd the logo. Follow the guide’s clear-space and minimum-size requirements.</li>
<li><strong>Color:</strong> Use only colors from the City of Milwaukee palette.</li>
<li><strong>Typography:</strong> Use Myriad Pro. If you are unable to activate it, use Montserrat or Bebas Neue, which the guide lists as approved alternates. No other typefaces are permitted.</li>
<li><strong>One logo:</strong> Do not include university, sponsor, or event logos.</li>
<li><strong>Credit line:</strong> Include the following on every piece: <em>Student project. Not affiliated with the City of Milwaukee.</em></li>
</ul>
<h3>Activating Myriad Pro</h3>
<ol>
<li>Open the Creative Cloud desktop app and sign in with your student account.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>fonts.adobe.com</strong>, search for <strong>Myriad Pro</strong>, and select <strong>Add family</strong>.</li>
<li>Restart Illustrator or InDesign and confirm that Myriad Pro appears in the font menu.</li>
</ol>
<p>If Myriad Pro does not activate, download Montserrat or Bebas Neue from fonts.google.com.</p>
<h3>Poster Copy</h3>
<p>Use the following text exactly as written. Your focus is the design, not the writing.</p>
<p><strong>Welcome to Milwaukee</strong><br>Lake Michigan Freshwater Research Fellows · Fall Semester<br>Welcome · Bienvenidos · Bienvenue · Willkommen · Benvenuti · Witamy<br>Researchers from around the world are spending the semester studying our Great Lake. Say hello, share your favorite spots, and help them feel at home.<br><strong>Welcome Reception</strong> · Thursday · 5 to 7 p.m. · Lakefront Pavilion<br>Learn more at milwaukee.gov<br><em>Student project. Not affiliated with the City of Milwaukee.</em></p>
<h3>Instructions</h3>
<ol>
<li>Read the brand guide, download the logo files, and activate Myriad Pro.</li>
<li>Sketch <strong>at least 10 poster layout thumbnails by hand</strong> on unlined paper, approximately 2 by 3 inches each. Indicate small text with lines. Show clear space around the logo in every sketch.</li>
<li>Select <strong>two welcome-kit items</strong> from the following list: T-shirt (front and back), tote bag, water bottle or sticker set, name badge and lanyard, wayfinding sign, folded city map or pocket guide, or notebook cover.</li>
<li>Post a photo of your sketches and your two selected items to Discord. Comment on three classmates’ posts, identifying their strongest sketch and explaining why.</li>
<li>Assemble your post and comments in Miro and submit a screenshot of the Miro page here.</li>
</ol>
```
