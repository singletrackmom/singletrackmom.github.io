Module 0 · Getting Started

Technology Setup

Objectives

  1. 0.5 Set up and verify every account and piece of software the course uses, and record and share a short narrated screen recording. (Course Competency 3; Apply)
  2. 0.6 Describe the audience and goal of two pieces of your own past work in a Discord introduction. (Course Competency 1; Understand)

This assignment measures Module Objectives 0.5 and 0.6, which support Course Competencies 1 and 3.

Overview

This assignment sets up the core accounts every Digital Media Arts course uses and confirms that you can record and share your screen. Course apps (Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, Figma) are set up in the module where you first use them. You are graded on the outcome, not on which recorder you choose.

Points: 10, Process Work

Estimated time: about 2 hours 30 minutes

Due: Thursday, January 21 at noon; post to Discord by Wednesday, January 20 at noon

Instructions

Your computer: you need a Mac or Windows computer. A Chromebook will not work for this course, because it cannot run the Adobe apps. A phone alone is not enough. You also need reliable internet, a phone or camera to photograph sketches, and a microphone. If you need a computer, GCC lends Macs and PCs: request one through the Student Technology Request.

Discord rules. Ask questions in the course channel, not in direct messages. Set your server nickname to the name you want to be called; screen names are allowed. Add a profile photo or avatar. Add alt text to every image you post, and a caption or transcript to every audio or video post. Post images as PNG or JPG, never PDF, so classmates can see them without downloading.

Submit one Google Doc, Google Slides page or Miro board with:

  1. Proof that each core account works: screenshots, or a short written note if you use a screen reader.
  2. A link to a 10-second test recording of your screen with your voice. Show the work you share in your introduction. Upload it to YouTube with your Maricopa account and set it to Unlisted, so only people with the link can see it (how to set a video to Unlisted).
  3. Your Discord introduction. Include the name you want to be called and how to say it, your program, and one thing you want to learn. Share two examples of past work, with the audience and goal of each, and add alt text. New to graphic design? Share any work that shows you as an artist.
  4. Screenshots of your three comments on classmates’ introductions.

If you completed OBS setup in a previous Digital Media Arts course, you may reuse that setup and go straight to the test recording.

AI use

No AI use. Log “No AI used.” Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
