Overview

This quiz checks that you know how the course runs before the first project begins. Read Getting Started, Course Information and AI in This Course first. You may retake the quiz until the due date.

Points: 5, Exercises

Estimated time: about 30 minutes

Due: Thursday, January 21 at noon

Instructions

The quiz covers:

  1. The two weekly deadlines: the noon due time, the 24-hour late window (minus 10 percent) and the hard close.
  2. When to post work to Discord, and how many critique comments you make (three).
  3. The three grading groups and their weights.
  4. The two rules that hold all semester: sketch by hand before the computer, and never stretch or distort type.
  5. When using AI is not cheating: it is assigned, recorded in your AI-use log, and you are graded on your own thinking.
  6. Which account to use for Gemini and why: your Maricopa account, because people do not review what you enter and it is not used to train AI.
  7. What AI does in this course: research, writing, feedback and planning. It never designs, except in Design History.

AI use

No AI use. Answer from the course pages. Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
