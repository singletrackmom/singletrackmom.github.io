Helvetica Reflection

Objectives

  1. 0.7 Summarize one argument from Helvetica and explain your own position on it, citing where it appears in the film and a typeface you photograph in your own surroundings. (Course Competency 1; Understand)

This assignment measures Module Objective 0.7, which supports Course Competency 1.

Overview

Helvetica (Gary Hustwit, 2007) is a feature-length documentary about typography and graphic design, centered on the typeface of the same name. The designers in the film disagree sharply: some call Helvetica the clearest, most neutral typeface ever drawn; others call it bland corporate wallpaper. This reflection asks you to take a side and support it with something only you can see.

Points: 10, Exercises

Estimated time: about 2 hours

Due: Thursday, January 21 at noon

Instructions

How to watch the film

  1. Open Helvetica on Kanopy, through the GCC Library.
  2. Select Watch Now, then Log into GCCAZ, and sign in with your MEID (your Maricopa student ID and login) and password.
  3. The film is about 80 minutes long. Turn on captions in the player if you want them.

What to submit

  1. A photograph you take of Helvetica, or a typeface used in its place, where you live or work: a sign, a package, a storefront, a form. Add alt text.
  2. 100 words, plus or minus 10, typed into the text box. Name one argument someone in the film makes about Helvetica and about when it comes up. Say whether you agree, and connect your view to your photograph.

Do not summarize the film. You may disagree with a famous designer, but explain why.

AI use

No AI use. Your photograph and your position are the evidence. Log “No AI used.” Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
