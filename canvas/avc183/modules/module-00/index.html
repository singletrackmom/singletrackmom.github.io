Instructor notes

  • Week 1: welcome everyone in Discord, reply to each introduction, and message anyone without an introduction by Wednesday.
  • Expect pushback on the AI Primer and welcome it. Grade the reasoning, not the opinion.
  • Students under 18: some Gemini features (for example Deep Research) are blocked. Nothing here needs one; confirm early in Week 1 that every student can open Gemini with the Maricopa account.
  • Grade Technology Setup within 48 hours. Missing software gets a direct fix, not a deduction, on the first try. Flag any student on a Chromebook and point them to the Student Technology Request.
  • Privately ask each student what tools and assistive technology they use. Refer accommodations to Disability Resources and Services and approve an equivalent recorder where needed.
  • Week 1 is heavy (about 12 hours). Watch for students who stall on the AI Primer before setup.

Objectives

Module 0 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  1. 0.1 Explain why employers expect AI skills, and the real costs of AI. (Course Competency 1; Understand)
  2. 0.2 Explain in plain terms how a large language model produces an answer, and why it can be wrong. (Course Competency 1; Understand)
  3. 0.3 Write, test and revise a prompt, and verify the result against a source. (Course Competency 3; Apply)
  4. 0.4 Explain your own position on using AI in creative work, citing evidence. (Course Competency 1; Understand)
  5. 0.5 Set up and verify every account and piece of software the course uses, and record and share a short narrated screen recording. (Course Competency 3; Apply)
  6. 0.6 Describe the audience and goal of two pieces of your own past work in a Discord introduction. (Course Competency 1; Understand)
  7. 0.7 Summarize one argument from Helvetica and explain your own position on it, citing where it appears in the film and a typeface you photograph in your own surroundings. (Course Competency 1; Understand)

Overview

Week 1 starts with AI, before any software. Read AI in This Course, then complete the AI Primer. Then set up your accounts, introduce yourself on Discord, and reflect on the documentary Helvetica. Its big idea: every typeface takes a point of view.
