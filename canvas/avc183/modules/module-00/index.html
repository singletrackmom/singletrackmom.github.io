Objectives

Module 0 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  1. 0.1 Set up and verify every account and piece of software the course uses, and record and share a short narrated screen recording. (Course Competency 3; Apply)
  2. 0.2 Describe the audience and goal of two pieces of your own past work in a Discord introduction. (Course Competency 1; Understand)
  3. 0.3 Summarize one argument from Helvetica and explain your own position on it, citing where it appears in the film and a typeface you photograph in your own surroundings. (Course Competency 1; Understand)

Overview

This module sets up the accounts and habits the whole course depends on, and introduces the argument at the center of graphic design: every typeface carries a position. Start with the Start Here page, then Course Information and AI in This Course.
