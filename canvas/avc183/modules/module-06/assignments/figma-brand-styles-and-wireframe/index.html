Figma: Your Brand Styles and Wireframe

Objectives

  1. 6.1 Convert your own brand into Figma color and text styles and wireframe three pages. (Course Competency 3; Apply)

This assignment measures Module Objective 6.1, which supports Course Competency 3.

Overview

The same move you made with the Milwaukee brand in Module 4, now with your own.

Points: 10, Process Work

Estimated time: about 3 hours

Due: Sunday, April 25 at noon; post to Discord by Saturday, April 24 at noon

Instructions

  1. New Figma file. Build color styles for your palette and text styles for your type system.
  2. Wireframe three pages (home, about, contact or services). Structure only: grey boxes, no color, imagery or decoration.
  3. Share the file so anyone with the link can view.

Turning it in

  1. Post your work to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord as PNG or JPG, with alt text, by noon the day before the due date.
  2. Comment on three classmates’ posts. Each comment names one design principle (or, in Module 4, one brand rule) and points to where it appears in the work.
  3. Screenshot your post and your three comments and compile them on one Miro board.
  4. Submit the Miro screenshot and your Figma link to Canvas by noon on the due date.

AI use

No AI use. Log “No AI used.” Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
