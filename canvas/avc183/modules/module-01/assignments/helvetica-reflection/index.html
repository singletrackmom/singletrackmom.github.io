Watch Helvetica, 100-word reflection

Due: Thursday, Aug 27, at noon.

Points: 20

Submit: your 100 words, typed into the Canvas text box.

Estimated time: about 1 hour 45 minutes, most of it watching the film.

Watch Helvetica (Gary Hustwit, 2007). It is available through the college library with your login. It runs about 80 minutes.

Then write 100 words, plus or minus 10.

What to write

Do not summarize the film. A summary tells me you pressed play. Instead:

  1. Name one specific argument somebody in the film makes about Helvetica, and say roughly where in the film it shows up.
  2. Say whether you agree, and why.

The designers in this film disagree with each other, sharply. Some of them think Helvetica is the clearest, most neutral typeface ever drawn. Others think it is bland corporate wallpaper that flattened design for fifty years.

Pick a side. You are allowed to think a famous designer is wrong. You just have to say why.

Why you are watching this

This is a course about type. Before you set a single word, it helps to know that people argue about this stuff with real feeling, and that a typeface carries an argument whether you intended one or not. Every choice you make this semester is a position. This is where you start noticing that.
