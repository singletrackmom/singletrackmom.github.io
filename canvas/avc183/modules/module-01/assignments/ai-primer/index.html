Objectives

  1. 0.1 Explain why employers expect AI skills, and the real costs of AI. (Course Competency 1; Understand)

This assignment measures Module Objectives 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 and 0.4, which support Course Competencies 1 and 3.

Overview

This assignment practices the AI skills the rest of the course uses: writing a prompt, revising it, and checking what comes back. It also asks you to state where you stand on AI in design, with evidence. You are graded on your reasoning, not on your opinion.

Points: 10, Process Work

Estimated time: 3 hours 30 minutes

Due: Thursday, January 21 at noon; post to Discord by Wednesday, January 20 at noon

Instructions

Your work stays yours. You use Gemini through your Maricopa account. That is Gemini for Education, the educational version of Gemini. Google states that what you enter there is not reviewed by people and is not used to train AI models (Gemini for Education; Google’s education FAQ). Your artwork and writing are not used to teach the AI. A personal Google account is different: depending on its settings, what you enter there may be used to improve Google’s AI. That is why every assignment uses your Maricopa account.

  1. Prompt and revisions. In Gemini with your Maricopa account, write a prompt about a graphic design topic (for example, “Explain the difference between kerning and tracking for a first-semester design student”). Revise it twice using role, task, context and format. Paste all three prompts. Add one sentence on what changed in the answer.
  2. Fact-check. Choose three claims from Gemini’s final answer. For each one, give a source that confirms or corrects it: a library database, Google Fonts Knowledge or the course reading. Record one check as a 60- to 90-second screen recording with your voice, showing you opening the source. Share it as an unlisted video.
  3. Position. In 100 to 150 words, explain where you stand on using AI in creative work. Cite two sources from the Module 0 readings.
  4. AI-use log. Start your AI-use log (a Google Sheet you keep all term). Fill in the first row: date, tool, what you asked, and what you kept or changed.

Post your position (part 3) to #avc183-graphic-design-1 by noon the day before the due date, then reply respectfully to three classmates who see it differently. Each reply names one point you find persuasive and one question you still have.

AI use

Assigned: Gemini, with your Maricopa account, for the prompt and answer only. The fact-check, the recording and your position are your own work. Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
