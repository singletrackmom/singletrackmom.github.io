Overview

You are here because you want a career doing this work. The people who will hire you have already decided AI skills matter: in Microsoft and LinkedIn’s 2024 Work Trend Index, 66 percent of business leaders said they would not hire someone without AI skills, and 71 percent said they would rather hire a less experienced candidate who has them. Our industry advisors asked us to teach it.

Many students have serious doubts about AI, and some of those doubts are right. AI is trained on artists’ work, often without permission. It makes confident mistakes. It reflects the biases in its training data. Data centers used about 1.5 percent of the world’s electricity in 2024, and the International Energy Agency expects that to more than double by 2030. This course does not hide those problems.

The case for learning it anyway: refusing AI does not slow it down; it removes your voice from how it is used. Designers who decide how AI is used in their studios are the ones who understand it well enough to be trusted. This course keeps the creative work in your hands, teaches you to use AI for what it does well, and to catch it when it is wrong.

Is this cheating? No. AI use here is assigned, disclosed in your AI-use log, and you are graded on your own thinking. How your work is protected: Maricopa account only; under the district’s Google for Education agreement, what you enter is not reviewed by people, not used to train AI models, and not used for ads. Your instructor never puts your work into a public AI tool.
