Objectives

Module 2 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  1. 2.1 Classify the anatomy of letterforms in lettering you photograph, using the vocabulary of the reading. (Course Competency 2; Understand)
  2. 2.2 Generate 50 descriptive verbs and at least 20 hand thumbnails for each of five chosen words within fixed constraints. (Course Competency 4; Create)
  3. 2.3 Construct five vector designs that express meaning through placement, scale, and positive and negative space. (Course Competency 4; Create)
  4. 2.4 Revise your designs in response to peer critique and justify each final design in one sentence that names the principle it uses. (Course Competency 6; Evaluate)

Overview

Make five words look like what they mean, using nothing but type: no color, no pictures. Isolating type first shows that placement and scale carry meaning before anything else can distract. The project moves from 50 handwritten verbs, to 100 hand thumbnails, to vector designs, to a refined final.
