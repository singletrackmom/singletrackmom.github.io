Reading: Letter and Type Anatomy

Objectives

  1. 2.1 Classify the anatomy of letterforms in lettering you photograph, using the vocabulary of the reading. (Course Competency 2; Understand)

This assignment measures Module Objective 2.1, which supports Course Competency 2.

Overview

Before you design with type, you need its vocabulary. This reading introduces the parts of a letterform and how type is classified.

Points: 5, Exercises

Estimated time: about 2 hours

Due: Sunday, January 31 at noon

Instructions

Read

  • Lupton, E. (2010). Thinking with type (2nd ed.). Princeton Architectural Press.: the Introduction, the type anatomy diagram (p. 32) and pp. 40 to 94. The book is on reserve at the GCC Library.
  • Open alternative: Typography Handbook (open textbook, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), the chapters on anatomy and classification.

Submit

  1. A photograph you take of lettering in your surroundings (a sign, a label, a menu).
  2. On the photograph, label three anatomy terms from p. 32 (for example, x-height, counter, serif) with arrows. Add alt text.
  3. Two or three sentences: what the anatomy of this lettering tells you about its purpose.

AI use

No AI use. The photograph and labels are your own. Log “No AI used.” Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
