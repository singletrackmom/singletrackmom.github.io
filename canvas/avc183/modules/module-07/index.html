Objectives

Module 7 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  1. 7.1 Evaluate two projects from this course against design principles and accessibility in written rationales on your Behance page. (Course Competency 6; Evaluate)
  2. 7.2 Explain how your design process developed across the course. (Course Competency 1; Understand)

Overview

Collect your strongest work, with its process, in a public Behance portfolio, and evaluate it in your own words. The course closes with a reflection on how your process has developed.
