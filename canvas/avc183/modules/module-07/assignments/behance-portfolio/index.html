Portfolio Site

Objectives

  1. 6.1 Evaluate two projects from this course against design principles and accessibility in written rationales on your portfolio site. (Course Competency 6; Evaluate)

This assignment measures Module Objective 6.1, which supports Course Competency 6.

Overview

Put two of your strongest finished projects, with their process, on your portfolio site in Google Sites, and evaluate them in your own words. Canvas access ends; your portfolio does not. Employers hire people who can show how they think.

Points: 30, Projects

Estimated time: about 5 hours

Due: Thursday, May 6 at noon; post to Discord by Wednesday, May 5 at noon

Instructions

  1. Open or start your portfolio site at sites.google.com with your Maricopa account. Add your name (or professional name), a headshot or avatar, a header image and a short professional description.
  2. Publish two projects from this course, each on its own page. Show the process work (sketches, steps, changes from critique), not only the final.
  3. For each project, write a rationale of 100 to 200 words. Judge the work against design principles and accessibility: what works, what you would change, and why.
  4. Add alt text to every image: select it, choose More, then Add alt text (Google’s accessibility help).
  5. Choose Publish at the top right, type a short web address, and choose Publish.
  6. Choose Share. Next to Published site, choose Restricted, so only people you add can see it. Add your instructor’s email. When you apply for a job, add the employer’s email.
  7. Submit the site link.

Who can see it: Restricted is the default. You may switch to Public whenever you choose. If you see an option for Maricopa, it limits viewing to people with Maricopa accounts. If you make the site public, you can tick “Request public search engines to not display my site” in Publish settings. Google says this is a request, not a guarantee (Publish and share your site).

Already have a Behance portfolio from another class? You may use it instead.

Turning it in

  1. Post screenshots of your portfolio pages to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord as PNG or JPG, with alt text, by noon the day before the due date.
  2. Comment on three classmates’ portfolios. Each comment names one design principle and points to where it appears in the work.
  3. Screenshot your post and your three comments and compile them on one Miro board.
  4. Submit the Miro screenshot and your site link to Canvas by noon on the due date.

AI use

Optional: Gemini may give feedback on your rationale drafts. Log it. The evaluation is yours. Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
