Design History: Recorded Presentation

Objectives

  1. H.1 Design History: describe the major movements, designers and visual language of one period of design history, with citations. (Course Competency 1; Understand)
  2. H.3 Design History: direct Gemini from a requirements document you write to build a presentation in the visual language of the period, and justify its typefaces, colors and layout. (Course Competency 4; Create)

This assignment measures Module Objectives H.1 and H.3, which support Course Competencies 1 and 4.

Overview

Teach your period to the class.

Points: 30, Projects

Estimated time: about 3 hours

Due: Sunday, May 9 at noon; post to Discord by Saturday, May 8 at noon

Instructions

  1. Practice out loud. Talk through it; do not read from the screen. Groups can split the sections or present together.
  2. Record your screen and voice (OBS or your approved recorder) for 5 to 8 minutes. Include a citations section.
  3. Upload to YouTube with your Maricopa account as Unlisted. Fix the automatic captions by hand (edit captions in YouTube Studio).
  4. Post the link to Discord and comment on three classmates’ presentations, following Deadlines.

Submit your YouTube link.

AI use

Your recorded explanation is your own work. Log any Gemini use for your notes. Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
