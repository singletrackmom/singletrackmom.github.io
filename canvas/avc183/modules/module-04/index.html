Overview

Most new designers start by following brand rules a client already wrote. Brand standards are a company’s rules for its logo, colors and type. Here you design a welcome package inside the real City of Milwaukee brand guidelines: a poster, an Instagram ad and two welcome-kit items. Then you load the brand into Figma as styles and revise the set from critique. The brief is made up; the brand is real.
