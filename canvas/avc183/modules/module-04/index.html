Objectives

Module 4 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  1. 4.1 Apply the City of Milwaukee brand rules to a coordinated poster, Instagram ad and two welcome-kit mockups from the provided brief and copy. (Course Competency 2; Apply)
  2. 4.2 Integrate downloaded brand assets at correct size, color mode and clear space, and produce a press-ready poster PDF with bleed, crop marks and CMYK color. (Course Competency 3; Apply)
  3. 4.3 Convert a brand’s colors and type into reusable Figma color and text styles. (Course Competency 3; Apply)
  4. 4.4 Critique classmates’ work against the same brand rules. (Course Competency 6; Evaluate)

Overview

Most junior designers start by working inside rules a client already wrote. Here you design a welcome package (poster, Instagram ad, two welcome-kit items) inside the real City of Milwaukee brand guidelines, load the brand into Figma as styles, and revise the set from critique. Applying a brand before you build one teaches consistency and rationale.
