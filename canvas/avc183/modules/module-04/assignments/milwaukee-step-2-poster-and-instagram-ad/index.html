Module 3 · Someone Else’s Brand: City of Milwaukee

Milwaukee Step 2: Poster and Instagram Ad

Objectives

  1. 3.1 Apply the City of Milwaukee brand rules to a coordinated poster, Instagram ad and two welcome-kit mockups from the provided brief and copy. (Course Competency 2; Apply)
  2. 3.2 Integrate downloaded brand assets at correct size, color mode and clear space, and produce a press-ready poster PDF with bleed, crop marks and CMYK color. (Course Competency 3; Apply)
  3. 3.4 Critique classmates’ work against the same brand rules. (Course Competency 6; Evaluate)

This assignment measures Module Objectives 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4, which support Course Competencies 2, 3 and 6.

Overview

Develop your strongest sketch into the poster, then adapt the same layout into one Instagram ad. You revise both in Step 5 from critique.

Points: 10, Process Work

Estimated time: about 4 hours

Due: Thursday, March 11 at noon; post to Discord by Wednesday, March 10 at noon

Instructions

Poster specifications

  • 18 by 24 inches, with 0.125-inch bleed on all sides. Bleed is extra artwork past the edge, so no white edge shows after trimming.
  • CMYK; any image at 300 ppi effective resolution
  • Press-ready PDF with crop marks, which show the printer where to cut (InDesign: File, Export, Adobe PDF (Print), Marks and Bleeds)
  • Step 1 copy, exactly as written

Instagram ad specifications

  • One feed post, 1080 by 1350 pixels (4:5), RGB, PNG or JPG
  • The poster layout, simplified, with this text: Welcome to Milwaukee / Lake Michigan Freshwater Research Fellows / Welcome Reception · Thursday · 5 to 7 p.m.
  • Logo and text at least 60 pixels from every edge

Post this caption with your Discord post, not on the image: Researchers from around the world are spending the semester studying our Great Lake. Stop by Thursday and say hello. #WelcomeToMKE

Under your Discord post, list five rules from the brand guide that your poster follows, with the page number of each.

Turning it in

  1. Post your work to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord as PNG or JPG, with alt text, by noon the day before the due date.
  2. Comment on three classmates’ posts. Each comment names one brand rule from the guide and points to where it appears in the work.
  3. Screenshot your post and your three comments and compile them on one Miro board.
  4. Submit the Miro screenshot, the poster PDF and the Instagram PNG or JPG to Canvas by noon on the due date.

AI use

No AI use for the design. Log “No AI used.” Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
