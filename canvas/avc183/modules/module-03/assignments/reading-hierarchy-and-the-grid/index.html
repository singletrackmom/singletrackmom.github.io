Module 2 · Recipe: Grids and Hierarchy

Reading: Hierarchy and the Grid

Objectives

  1. 2.1 Explain three ways to create typographic hierarchy by setting one line of your own recipe three ways. (Course Competency 2; Understand)

This assignment measures Module Objective 2.1, which supports Course Competency 2.

Overview

Hierarchy decides what a reader sees first. This reading covers kerning, tracking, line spacing and the grid.

Points: 5, Exercises

Estimated time: about 1 hour 45 minutes

Due: Thursday, February 18 at noon

Instructions

Read

  • Lupton, E. (2010). Thinking with type (2nd ed.). Princeton Architectural Press. Read pp. 103 to 171. Look closely at the kerning, tracking (space across a whole word or line) and line-spacing examples.
  • Free alternative: Typography Handbook (open textbook, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), the chapters on spacing and hierarchy; and Baseline, Grid Systems.

Submit

Choose one line from the recipe you will use in this project. Set it three times in InDesign. Each time, create hierarchy a different way (for example, placement, leading or weight). Leading is the space between lines. Export one PNG with all three versions. Label each with the method and one sentence on what now reads first.
