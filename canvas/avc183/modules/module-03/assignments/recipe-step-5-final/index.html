Module 2 · Recipe: Grids and Hierarchy

Recipe Step 5: Final

Objectives

  1. 2.3 Apply one hierarchy variable at a time (leading, kerning, weight, case, scale) and explain how each changes what reads first. (Course Competency 2; Apply)
  2. 2.4 Produce an eight-page, print-ready PDF that revises every design, after ranking all eight against the hierarchy goal. (Course Competency 5; Create)

This assignment measures Module Objectives 2.3 and 2.4, which support Course Competencies 2 and 5.

Overview

Evaluate all eight designs, then revise every one into a single print-ready PDF.

Points: 30, Projects

Estimated time: 4 hours

Due: Thursday, March 4 at noon; post to Discord by Wednesday, March 3 at noon

Instructions

  1. Rank your eight designs against the hierarchy goal: does the title read first, and do the descriptive words carry meaning? Write the ranking as a short list with one sentence per design.
  2. Revise all eight based on your ranking and the critique. Never stretch or distort type.
  3. Submit one PDF, High Quality Print, eight 8 by 8 pages, in order: pages 1 and 2 placement only; 3 and 4 weight and case; 5 and 6 scale; 7 and 8 image (color only in the image, never the type).

Turning it in

  1. Post your work to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord as PNG or JPG, with alt text, by noon the day before the due date.
  2. Comment on three classmates’ posts. Each comment names one design principle (or, in Module 3, one brand rule) and points to where it appears in the work.
  3. Screenshot your post and your three comments and compile them on one Miro board.
  4. Submit the Miro screenshot, the PDF and your ranking to Canvas by noon on the due date.

AI use

No AI use for the designs or the ranking. Log “No AI used.” Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
