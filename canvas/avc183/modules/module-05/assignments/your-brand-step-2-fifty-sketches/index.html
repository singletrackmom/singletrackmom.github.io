Your Brand Step 2: Fifty Sketches

Objectives

  1. 4.2 Generate at least 50 hand thumbnail concepts and refine the strongest four so each works in black alone. (Course Competency 4; Create)

This assignment measures Module Objective 4.2, which supports Course Competency 4.

Overview

Volume first. Quick, rough concepts explore far more ideas than careful drawings.

Points: 10, Process Work

Estimated time: about 4 hours

Due: Sunday, April 4 at noon; post to Discord by Saturday, April 3 at noon

Instructions

  1. In an unlined sketchbook, draw at least 50 thumbnail ideas for the mark, 9 to 12 per page. Work fast.
  2. Try many kinds: letter marks, symbols, combinations, abstract shapes. Fifty versions of one idea is still one idea.
  3. Photograph each page flat, full frame, in even light.

Turning it in

  1. Post your work to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord as PNG or JPG, with alt text, by noon the day before the due date.
  2. Comment on three classmates’ posts. Each comment names one design principle (or, in Module 3, one brand rule) and points to where it appears in the work.
  3. Screenshot your post and your three comments and compile them on one Miro board.
  4. Submit the Miro screenshot and your sketch pages as one file to Canvas by noon on the due date.
