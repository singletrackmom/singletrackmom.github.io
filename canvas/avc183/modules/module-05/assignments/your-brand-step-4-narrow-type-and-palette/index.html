Your Brand Step 4: Narrow, Type and Palette

Objectives

  1. 4.3 Design a finished mark, typeface and palette, each justified against the client’s audience and goals. (Course Competency 4; Create)

This assignment measures Module Objective 4.3, which supports Course Competency 4.

Overview

Narrow to one or two marks and lock the typeface and a palette of no more than three colors, each justified against the client’s audience and goals.

Points: 10, Process Work

Estimated time: 4 hours

Due: Sunday, April 11 at noon; post to Discord by Saturday, April 10 at noon

Instructions

  1. Narrow to your best one or two marks and refine them. Each must work in black and white and in your colors. No screens (lighter tints of a color) and no gradients. A strong mark is a simple one.
  2. Lock one typeface and a palette of no more than three colors, with CMYK and hex values. Check each text and background color pair for contrast with the WebAIM contrast checker.
  3. Write a rationale (your reasons) of 100 to 150 words. Tie each choice to the audience in your brief. “It looks good” is not a reason. “Most of the audience is under 18 and the goal is playful, so I chose a rounded sans serif” is.

Turning it in

  1. Post your work to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord as PNG or JPG, with alt text, by noon the day before the due date.
  2. Comment on three classmates’ posts. Each comment names one design principle (or, in Module 3, one brand rule) and points to where it appears in the work.
  3. Screenshot your post and your three comments and compile them on one Miro board.
  4. Submit the Miro screenshot and your rationale to Canvas by noon on the due date.

AI use

Optional: Gemini may give feedback on your rationale draft. Log it. The choices and the rationale are yours. Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
