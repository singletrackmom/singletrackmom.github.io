Module 5 · Your Own Brand

Your Brand Step 7: Three-Panel Instagram Campaign

Objectives

  1. 5.4 Apply your identity to a press-ready business card and a three-panel Instagram campaign built on one grid. (Course Competency 2; Apply)

This assignment measures Module Objective 5.4, which supports Course Competency 2.

Overview

Promote the brand you built. One message, split across three panels that share one grid.

Points: 30, Projects

Estimated time: about 5 hours 30 minutes

Due: Thursday, April 22 at noon; post to Discord by Wednesday, April 21 at noon

Instructions

  1. Write a three-part message, one part per panel. Gemini may help you draft the copy; record that in your AI-use log.
  2. Sketch the grid by hand first, all three panels on one page.
  3. Build three 1080 by 1350 panels in Illustrator or InDesign. Each carries a heading, an image you have the right to use, and the logo. The grid is identical across all three; the message and imagery change.
  4. Pull rhetorical elements (shape, color, type treatment) from the logo so the campaign reads as the brand.

Turning it in

  1. Post your work to #avc183-graphic-design-1 on Discord as PNG or JPG, with alt text, by noon the day before the due date.
  2. Comment on three classmates’ posts. Each comment names one design principle (or, in Module 4, one brand rule) and points to where it appears in the work.
  3. Screenshot your post and your three comments and compile them on one Miro board.
  4. Submit the Miro screenshot, the three panels and your grid sketch to Canvas by noon on the due date.

AI use

Optional: Gemini may help draft the three-part message. Log it. Gemini never designs the panels or generates the images. Record every use in your AI-use log. Gemini is used only with your Maricopa account.
