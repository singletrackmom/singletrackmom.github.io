Objectives

Module 4 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  1. 4.1 Formulate a business name and design brief for a client using the word-association process. (Course Competency 4; Create)
  2. 4.2 Generate at least 50 hand thumbnail concepts and refine the strongest four so each works in black alone. (Course Competency 4; Create)
  3. 4.3 Design a finished mark, typeface and palette, each justified against the client’s audience and goals. (Course Competency 4; Create)
  4. 4.4 Apply your identity to a press-ready business card and a three-panel Instagram campaign built on one grid. (Course Competency 2; Apply)

Overview

Choose a client, name the business, and build its identity (its logo, type and colors). You make 50 hand thumbnails, a mark (logo) that works in black alone, a typeface and palette chosen for the audience, a press-ready business card and a three-panel Instagram campaign.
