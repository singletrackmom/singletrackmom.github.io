## 5. Projects

### 5a. Identity project (Identity Design Intro module, ~6 weeks: 2/2 to 3/2)

In the live course this is a **pick-your-own client** project: "You may use a small business, it does not have to be real, it can be a fictitious company." Not something that already has a successful identity; not your own design company. The **Espressoism Identity Brief.pdf** sits in the same module as the given-brand option/example (a 23 MB PDF; its text could not be extracted through the API, see note at the end). Coffee shop is the running example throughout the overview.

Goal (from overview): work through a process to a comprehensive identity and final identity manual; logo/mark applied to several applications; "one of the most important pieces in your portfolio."

| Step | Due | Pts | What students do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Choose your client | 2/5 | 10 | Pick a small business (real or fictitious), narrow exactly what it is and who the audience is; be very specific (example questions: how is this coffee shop different from Starbucks, students or middle-aged, used books and couches, food, pets, price, boba). Name brands that would resonate; initial ideas on images, type, color. Post to Discord, comment on 4, Miro screenshot to Canvas. |
| 2 Mood board / persona study | 2/9 | 20 | "Visual positioning" board (Pinterest, Miro, or physical): key descriptive words; one sentence with two adjectives; color palette; audience; limited shapes/textures/patterns; inspiring imagery; one or two typefaces; competitor research. Suggested layout: rule of thirds or 4-column modular grid. Start planning the 6 applications. |
| 3 Logo/mark and rhetorical elements | 2/12 | 10 | At least 50 thumbnail sketches on paper (2x2 in. squares in a sketchbook), rhetorical elements, mind mapping; see Pencil to PDF. "Hundreds of iterations." Post 2+ times to Discord. |
| 4 Finalize logo/mark | 2/16 | 10 | Refine; sketch how it works on 3 possible pieces; post several times; feedback to 4. |
| 4.5 Step back and refine | 2/23 | 10 | Get the logo to its best version; revisit early sketches; name and mark work together; find rhetorical elements (paint splatter, stitching, coffee beans, game icons); works small and large, alternate lockups. Post 3x, comment on 6. |
| 5 Apply to all pieces; lay out manual | 2/26 | 10 | Single PDF identity manual (structure below). Feedback to 3. |
| 6 Final identity manual | 3/2 | 30 (Projects) | Revise, "make everything perfect," consistent grid, looks like the identity. PNG to Discord, PDF to Canvas. |

Six required applications: social marketing campaign; web app interface (not functional); product or package; three "your choice" (store signage, apparel, billboard, blimp, promo video/animation, other).

Manual spec: consistent grid; same size/typeface for headings; the identity's own typefaces and rhetorical elements; no background images; black, readable type. Cover "Identity manual for ___. By ___." p1 Design Brief (10 to 12 pt: company, audience, objectives, deliverables); p2 Process work (sketches, persona board); p3 Logo at different sizes, black/white and color palette, typefaces; pp4 to 9 Applications 1 to 6.

Points: 100 process + 30 final = 130, plus the 60 points of Wheeler readings in the same module.

### 5b. Social issue or nonprofit campaign (~6 weeks: 3/9 to 4/13)

Students choose a social issue (pick a side, research both sides from reputable sources via the Taking Sides library handout) or a nonprofit to promote (National Parks given as an example). Builds a color/type/image palette and applies it across media. Portfolio-awareness caution on controversial topics.

| Step | Due | Pts | Task |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Topic | 3/9 | 10 | Choose issue/org, research, post to Discord, feedback |
| 2 Message + brief | 3/12 | 10 | Design brief: messaging, audience, concise goal, media channels; sketches; feedback to 3 |
| 3 Imagery | 3/16 | 10 | Decide illustration/photo/infographic; ~50 sketches, some applied to likely finals |
| 4 Develop | 3/23 | 10 | Test message across media (video, app, billboard, brochure); AI allowed to help write content |
| 5 Social media | 3/26 | 10 | At least 5 images or videos, at least 2 social platforms, time-based element (After Effects or Photoshop sequence); feedback to 4 |
| 6 Print | 4/2 | 10 | Brochure, annual report or newsletter with rhetorical elements and a consistent grid; real or AI-generated content, cited; feedback to 4 |
| 7 Two more pieces | 4/6 | 10 | Billboard, extra social, print, clothing, etc. |
| 8 Critique / portfolio format | 4/9 | 10 | All pieces final; format for Behance as one long scroll or an InDesign manual with consistent pages |
| FINAL | 4/13 | 30 (Projects) | Under-3-minute OBS presentation (idea, narrowing, 7-step process, sketches), uploaded to student YouTube, link on Discord and Canvas |

Minimum deliverables (overview): social media with video/time-based media; printed piece such as an 11x17 poster or infographic poster; text-based document (newsletter, annual report, brochure); two more of student's choice.

### 5c. Design History Presentation (team of 2, ~6 weeks, 4/2 to 5/11)
Research and present designers and movements across an assigned period between 1895 and 1989. Overview 5, Part 1 20, Part 2 20, Part 3 final 30. Past examples in Prezi; extra-credit example built with AI.

### 5d. Website Design: UX & Responsive Mockups (~4 weeks, 4/16 to 5/7)
Design (not build) a site for the brand from the social issue campaign or the identity project. Figma mockups at desktop and mobile. Phase 1 Gather content (Google Doc/Word organized by Home, About, Action page; AI allowed to draft); Phase 2 Site map and thumbnail sketches; Phase 3 Wireframes in Figma (low fidelity); Phase 4 Desktop mockups; Phase 5 Mobile mockups / final (30); overview also lists a Phase 6 presentation. Navigation guidance included (where am I, where can I go, how do I get back; plain labels; hamburger on mobile).
