Competency Alignment and Time Budget

Course Competencies

By the end of AVC 248 you will be able to:

  1. Evaluate the appropriateness of your personal goals and objectives to market needs.
  2. Develop a self-marketing plan, including a resume and business card.
  3. Develop a leave-behind.
  4. Use networking skills in the classroom.
  5. Identify the appropriate markets to target.
  6. Identify points of discussion for a standard operating contract with a client.
  7. Demonstrate the ability to interview and communicate with clients, including professional interview etiquette.
  8. Develop a portfolio for online and printed presentation purposes.

Objectives and Revised Bloom’s Taxonomy

Every module objective is written to the revised Bloom’s taxonomy (Anderson and Krathwohl, 2001) and sits at or below the level of the course competency it supports. Every graded assessment has had an AI-exposure check using Oregon State University Ecampus’s Bloom’s Taxonomy Revisited, so that the graded evidence shows your own thinking, decisions and work. The Competency Alignment and Time Budget page lists every objective with its level and the assignment that measures it.

Levels follow the cognitive process categories of the revised taxonomy (Anderson and Krathwohl, 2001): Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate and Create. Competencies 5 and 6 are written at the Remember level (“Identify”); the course’s market, contract and copyright work identifies, then applies what it identifies in later projects, and the objectives tagged to those competencies use Remember-level verbs to stay at or below them.

AI-Exposure Check

Each graded assessment was rated with the Bloom’s Taxonomy Revisited lens (Oregon State University Ecampus, 2024): low where the graded evidence is a recording, a live act, hand work or real participants; medium where some evidence comes from the student’s own sources or process; high where Gemini could produce the evidence from the prompt alone. No assessment is rated high.

Time Budget

AVC 248 is a three-credit online course: 3 credits times 40 hours is about 120 hours. The build totals about 119 hours. Each item’s estimate assumes a student new to the tool and includes Discord critique and captioning time; study time (videos, slides and readings) is counted in the week it is assigned. Every instructional week falls between 6 and 9.5 hours.
