Module 2: Identity and Self-Presentation

Module 2: Identity and Self-Presentation

Weeks 5 to 7

Objectives

Module 2 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 2.1 Plan a personal identity system (audience, two typefaces, a palette with hex codes and a list of deliverables) in a one-page design brief. (Course Competency 2)
  • 2.2 Develop three business card directions and a designed resume from your identity design brief. (Course Competency 2)
  • 2.3 Develop a leave-behind concept from three sketches, and justify any scannable or tappable element. (Course Competency 3)
  • 2.4 Deliver a 10 to 20 second elevator pitch and two STAR answers on camera without reading from notes. (Course Competency 7)
  • 2.5 Identify who owns client work, which fair use factor a court weighed, and what a Creative Commons license allows. (Course Competency 6)
  • 2.6 Answer the weakness and salary-expectation questions on camera, citing a researched pay range for your target role. (Course Competency 7)

Overview

You are a brand. Your resume, business card, leave-behind and portfolio should look like they belong to one person. Your identity is the look you use on all of them: typefaces, colors and a wordmark (your name set as a logo). In this module you plan it in a design brief and apply it to print pieces. You also record more interview answers, learn copyright basics, and plan your midterm portfolio.

20.75 hours in all.
