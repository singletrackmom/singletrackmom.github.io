Module 2, Identity and Self-Presentation

Identity Design Brief

This assignment measures Module Objective 2.1, which supports Course Competency 2.

Objectives

  • 2.1 Plan a personal identity system (audience, two typefaces, a palette with hex codes and a list of deliverables) in a one-page design brief. (Course Competency 2)

Overview

Before you design a resume, business card, leave-behind or portfolio header, you need a brief that defines your audience, goals, typefaces and palette. Everything you make for the rest of the course follows it.

10 points, Process Work. 2.5 hours.

Instructions

  1. Name your audience: game studios, agencies, small businesses, galleries or another group from your Personal Goals Statement.
  2. Choose up to two typefaces that work well together: one for headings and one for body text.
  3. Choose a palette of four or five colors with hex codes, and state the tone you want (for example, dark and professional, or bright and energetic).
  4. Build a one-page brief in Illustrator, InDesign or Google Slides: your name, audience, goals, typefaces shown in use, and the palette.
  5. List every deliverable the identity will be applied to: business card, resume, leave-behind, LinkedIn or profile header, portfolio site header and any social profile.
  6. Gemini may give written feedback. It never designs; every visual choice is yours.
  7. Post the brief as a PNG or JPG to Discord by noon the day before the deadline. Leave three comments that each name a specific type, color or layout choice.

Example

Treat the brief as a one-page brand standards sheet for yourself. Browse personal brand style guide examples (Pinterest) for layout ideas.

Accessibility

Add alt text to every image you post in Discord: click the pencil icon on the attachment before you send it and write one or two sentences on what matters in the image. Do not begin with “image of.” Check your palette’s text and background pairs with the WebAIM Contrast Checker.

Submission

Upload the same PNG or JPG you posted to Discord, plus screenshots or links of your three comments.
