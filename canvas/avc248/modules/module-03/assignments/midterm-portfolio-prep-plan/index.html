Midterm Portfolio Preparation Plan

Module 2, Identity and Self-Presentation

Midterm Portfolio Preparation Plan

This assignment measures Module Objective 3.2, which supports Course Competency 8.

Objectives

  • 3.2 Plan a week-by-week production schedule that addresses the priority feedback on your portfolio. (Course Competency 8)

Overview

Your portfolio is the centerpiece of your job search. Before you present it at midterm, you need a plan: which projects you will show, what needs improvement, and when you will do the work.

10 points, Process Work. 1.5 hours.

Instructions

  1. Review all the work from your degree. Choose at least four projects to show at midterm.
  2. For each, write three to five sentences on the goal, your role, the process and the outcome.
  3. List everything each project needs fixed or updated before midterm.
  4. Build a week-by-week schedule from now to the midterm, naming the project and task for each week (for example, “Week 7: fix kerning on the poster title and re-export at 300 dpi”).
  5. Start or update your portfolio site in Google Sites. Moving it to a personal Google account is optional: a personal account keeps the site after your Maricopa account closes and lets you share it with employers outside the district. If you are not job hunting yet, or do not want anyone else to see your work, keep it in your Maricopa account. Either way, publish it as Restricted. See Which Google account to use. Gemini stays on your Maricopa account either way. If you already have a Behance portfolio from another class, you may use it instead. See Portfolio Platforms.
  6. Gemini may turn your task list into a draft schedule. Check every task against your own list, and add an AI-use log row.

Example

“Brand identity for a fictional sustainable clothing company. The goal was a cohesive visual system that felt modern, earthy and premium. I developed the logo, palette, type system, packaging mockups and a brand standards guide.”

Accessibility

Make the document readable by a screen reader: use real heading styles (Heading 1 and Heading 2 in Google Docs or Word, paragraph styles in InDesign), keep text contrast high, add alt text to every image or logo, and export a tagged PDF (InDesign: Create Tagged PDF; Word or Google Docs: keep document structure tags on when you save as PDF).

Submission

Upload the plan as a PDF with your AI-use log row.
