# The Three Render Personas

These three career agents are the running examples used throughout this class. Each one is built
with the same prompt and the same structure, but pointed at a different goal, so together they
demonstrate all three Render scenarios: a full job search, a 50/50 split, and an all-in freelance
business. When you build your own agent, you can see your situation in at least one of them.

Read all three. They prove that the recipe stays the same even when the field and the orientation
change.

---

## Maya Chen, graphic design, job search

**Orientation: 100% job search.** Maya is finishing her Digital Media Arts degree and is strong in
Illustrator, InDesign, typography, and print production. Her reach is a real **junior brand designer
job** at a creative studio doing identity and packaging work. Her whole plan, from closing her
motion gap to presenting work and activating her network, points at landing that role.

File: `example-career-agent-maya.md`

---

## Riley Torres, animation and games, 50/50

**Orientation: 50/50, freelance practice and job search at once.** Riley is finishing the Animation
and Time-Based Media degree and already ships 2D character animation for indie games. Riley is
running two tracks side by side: **growing a steady freelance animation practice** with repeat
clients and a retainer, AND **actively looking for a junior or staff animation job** on a studio or
in-house team. The same craft and the same reel serve both tracks, so the plan splits its effort
between them: rigging and sprite pipelines feed both, while the business modules cover contracts and
pricing on the freelance side and reel, case studies, and interview prep on the job-search side.

File: `example-career-agent-riley.md`

---

## Nina Okafor, photography, freelance business

**Orientation: 100% freelance business.** Nina is finishing her Digital Media Arts degree and is
confident behind the camera, but has almost no paid work. She is not chasing a job at all. Her reach
is a **sustainable freelance photography business** of her own, portraits, small-business and brand
work, and events, with her own portfolio site, pricing, contracts, client-finding and marketing, and
the money basics. Her plan turns skill into a business: build the book, set pricing, write contracts,
find and keep clients, and launch the site and booking flow.

File: `example-career-agent-nina.md`

## Why three

| Persona | Field | Orientation | Reach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maya Chen | Graphic and brand design | 100% job search | A junior brand designer job |
| Riley Torres | Animation and games | 50/50 freelance and job | Steady freelance clients AND a junior or staff animation job |
| Nina Okafor | Photography | 100% freelance business | A sustainable freelance photography business |

Most students will land somewhere on this spectrum: all-in on a job, all-in on freelance, or a
deliberate split. Whichever one fits you, the move is the same. You write one Markdown file that
names your goal and your gaps, fills in modules with real resources and applied tasks, and you run
and improve it after the course ends.
