Module 9: Build Your Career Agent

Module 9: Build Your Career Agent

Weeks 14 to 15

Objectives

Module 9 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 9.1 Evaluate your career goal against market needs, explain the five parts of an AI agent, and state the goal as one an agent can act on. (Course Competency 1)
  • 9.2 Identify the markets, organizations and sources your agent should target, and list the rules it will follow. (Course Competency 5)
  • 9.3 Test your agent against its own rules across three runs and judge which rules to revise from the evidence. (Course Competency 1)
  • 9.4 Justify your agent’s goal, rules and delivery choice in a recorded presentation, using two scheduled outputs as evidence. (Course Competency 1)

Overview

A chatbot answers when you ask. An agent does a job for you on a schedule and reports back. In this module you design, build, test and schedule your own career agent in Gemini, using your resume, portfolio and target market from earlier modules. It keeps working for you after the course ends. Avery, the student in the worked example, is fictional, and so is every studio on that page.

About 11.5 hours in all.
