Module 9, Build Your Career Agent

What Is an AI Agent?

Objectives

This lesson prepares you for Module Objective 9.1, which Step 1 measures. By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  1. Explain the five parts of an AI agent: a goal, where it looks, the rules it follows, what it reports, and when it runs.
  2. Compare working agents and identify which kind of agent fits your own career goal.

Overview

A chatbot answers when you ask. An agent does a job for you on its own, on a schedule, and reports back. In this module you will design, build, test and schedule your own career agent in Gemini. It will keep working for you after the course ends.

The five parts of every agent

PartThe question it answersExample
GoalWhat is this agent for?Find junior motion design jobs I can land in the next three months.
Where it looksWhich sources and which organizations?A list of twenty target studios and agencies, checked on their own careers pages.
RulesWhat counts, what gets cut, and how is each result checked?Must be entry level; no unpaid internships; open the employer’s page and confirm the posting is live.
What it reportsWhat does each result look like?One card per job: title, employer, link, why it fits, one gap to close.
When it runsHow often, and where do results land?Every Monday morning, as a notification on my phone.
