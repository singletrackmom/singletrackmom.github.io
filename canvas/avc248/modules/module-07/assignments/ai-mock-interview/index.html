AI Mock Interview

Module 7 , Interview Skills

Description

In this assignment you practice a spoken job interview with Gemini Live, using your Maricopa student account. Gemini plays the hiring manager for a real job you choose, asks one question at a time, and then gives feedback on your answers. You may practice as many times as you like; you submit one complete session and a reflection on the feedback.

Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment you will be able to:

  • Answer interview questions clearly and professionally out loud
  • Apply the STAR method to behavioral interview questions
  • Evaluate AI feedback critically, deciding what to keep and what to reject

Steps

  1. Choose a real job posting in your field. The posting you used in a discussion this semester works well. Copy the full posting text.
  2. Go to gemini.google.com or open the Gemini app, and sign in with your Maricopa student account. Work done with your Maricopa account is not used to train AI models.
  3. Paste the posting, then paste this prompt below it: “You are the hiring manager for this job. Interview me for it. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Ask six questions: two about my experience, two behavioral questions, one about my portfolio, and one about why I want this job. Do not give feedback until I say ‘end interview.’ Then rate each answer for clarity, specific examples and the STAR method, and name one improvement for each answer.”
  4. Select Live to answer out loud, the way you would in a real interview. If you prefer or need to type, stay in the chat and type your answers; typed sessions are accepted.
  5. Keep your portfolio open on a second screen, tablet or phone, and refer to specific projects in your answers.
  6. When you have answered all six questions, say or type “end interview” and read the feedback.
  7. Copy the full conversation, including the questions, your answers and the feedback, into a Google Doc. Share the Doc with your instructor.
  8. At the end of the Doc, write a reflection of 5 to 7 sentences: Which feedback do you agree with, and which do you reject, and why? Rewrite one of your answers using the STAR method. What will you do differently in a real interview?

Interview Checklist

  • A quiet room where you can speak out loud
  • Portfolio open and ready to reference
  • The job posting reviewed before you start
  • Answers of about one to two minutes each

Example

STAR Method reminder: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Use it especially for “Tell me about a time when” questions.

AI feedback can be wrong or generic. A strong reflection names at least one suggestion you decided not to follow and explains why.

Accessibility

Gemini Live keeps a written transcript of the spoken session, and typed answers are accepted, so the assignment works with a screen reader, a keyboard or speech. In your Google Doc, use a real heading for the transcript and one for the reflection.
