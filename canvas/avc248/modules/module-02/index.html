Module 1: Goals, Resume and Cover Letter

Module 1: Goals, Resume and Cover Letter

Weeks 2 to 4

Objectives

Module 1 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 1.1 Evaluate your career goals against three real postings or opportunities in your field, and describe your target job by industry, type of organization and typical workday. (Course Competency 1)
  • 1.2 Produce a one-page resume in the GCC Career Services format, and revise every bullet to begin with an action verb and correct each item flagged in feedback. (Course Competency 2)
  • 1.3 Write a cover letter for one real posting that connects two specific details of your own experience to its stated requirements. (Course Competency 2)
  • 1.4 Locate job postings and portfolios in your field, and identify the skills, software and presentation strategies they repeat. (Course Competency 5)
  • 1.5 Classify interview questions by type, and answer one behavioral question on camera with the STAR method. (Course Competency 7)
  • 1.6 Explain two ways a Gemini edit improved a section of your resume and two ways it misrepresented you, comparing your original with the edited version. (Course Competency 2)

Overview

Everything in this course builds from two documents you write yourself: where you want to go, and a resume that shows what you have done. This module also starts the interview practice series: six short exercises, from Week 3 to Week 12, that build to a recorded mock interview. Resume drafts come back with written feedback within five days.

20.25 hours in all.
