About this lesson

Now that you know why we use AI in this class, this lesson gives you just enough foundation to use it well. We will cover the handful of ideas that make the difference between fighting a tool and partnering with one: what AI is good at, where it falls short, how to write a prompt that actually gets you somewhere, and how to stay safe with your own information.

Take your time with the slides below. We are building habits here, not memorizing facts, so that when we open Render together you already know how to steer.
