Module 7, Final Package

Updated Portfolio

This assignment measures Module Objective 7.3, which supports Course Competency 8.

Objectives

  • 7.3 Write a description for each of five portfolio projects that states the goal, your role, the process and the outcome. (Course Competency 8)

Overview

Your final portfolio is the central piece of your professional identity: the version employers and clients see when they decide whether to contact you.

10 points, Process Work. 2.5 hours.

Instructions

  1. Review your midterm critique and Portfolio Check-In feedback.
  2. Work in your portfolio site in Google Sites, in your Maricopa account or a personal Google account (see Which Google account to use).
  3. Include at least five projects, each with a description of the goal, your role, the process and the outcome.
  4. Add your name and a professional tagline (for example, “3D animator specializing in creature and effects work”), a headshot and a banner.
  5. Show process work (sketches, storyboards, a design brief or a time-lapse) on at least one project.
  6. Make sure the address is short and easy to type.
  7. Add alt text to every image: select it, choose More, then Add alt text (Google’s accessibility help).
  8. Choose Publish at the top right, type a short web address, and choose Publish.
  9. Choose Share. Next to Published site, choose Restricted, so only people you add can see it. Add your instructor’s email. When you apply for a job, add the employer’s email.

Who can see it: Restricted is the default. You may switch to Public whenever you choose. If you see an option for Maricopa, it limits viewing to people with Maricopa accounts. If you make the site public, you can tick “Request public search engines to not display my site” in Publish settings. Google says this is a request, not a guarantee (Publish and share your site).

Already have a Behance portfolio from another class? You may use it instead.

Example

Browse working professionals on Behance. Every visitor should be able to answer one question: should I contact this person about work?

Accessibility

Make your portfolio accessible: alt text on every project image (in Google Sites, select the image, choose More, then Add alt text), captions on every embedded video, headings in order, and link text that says where it goes. Run your home page through the WAVE accessibility checker and fix every error it reports.

Submission

Submit your portfolio link.
