Module 7: Final Package

Module 7: Final Package

Weeks 13 to 15 and finals

Objectives

Module 7 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 7.1 Compile your final resume, business card, leave-behind and one digital asset into one PDF that uses a single typeface pairing and palette throughout. (Course Competency 2)
  • 7.2 Produce a print-ready PDF or a working digital leave-behind that follows your concept and identity system. (Course Competency 3)
  • 7.3 Write a description for each of five portfolio projects that states the goal, your role, the process and the outcome. (Course Competency 8)
  • 7.4 Present three portfolio projects in a two-minute recorded video, stating the goal, your role, one key decision and the outcome of each. (Course Competency 8)
  • 7.5 Produce a final resume that applies your identity system and corrects every item flagged across four drafts. (Course Competency 2)

Overview

Everything you built this term comes together: a finished portfolio, a final resume and leave-behind, an identity package that ties them together, and a final portfolio presentation. You will send these real materials to employers and clients after the course.

About 12.5 hours in all.

Watch

  • Your Final Package (slides, PDF), with the instructor walkthrough video posted in Canvas

Read

  • Your midterm critique and Portfolio Check-In feedback

Practice

  • Print your resume, business card and leave-behind together at 100% and check that they look like one family.
