Module 0, Getting Started

Technology Setup

This assignment measures Module Objectives 0.5, 0.6 and 0.7, which support Course Competencies 4 and 8.

Objectives

  • 0.5 Set up and verify every account and piece of software the course uses. (Course Competency 4)
  • 0.6 Record and share a short explanation of your work with the recorder that fits your field. (Course Competency 8)
  • 0.7 Introduce yourself and your work to the class on Discord and comment on four classmates’ introductions. (Course Competency 4)

Overview

This assignment sets up the core accounts every Digital Media course uses, rehearses the recording you will use all term, and introduces you to the class. Course-specific apps, such as LinkedIn, are set up in the module where you first use them. You are graded on the outcome, not on one brand of tool.

10 points, Process Work. 2.25 hours.

Instructions

  1. Set up each account in the table and show it works: a screenshot of each, or a short written note if you use a screen reader.
  2. Record a 10-second test clip with your voice and some of your work on screen. Share it as shown in Recordings.
  3. Post your Discord introduction in #avc248-design-self-promotion. Include the name you go by and how to say it, your program, one thing you want to learn, and two examples of past work with alt text.
  4. Comment on four classmates’ introductions.
  5. Collect everything in one Google Doc, Google Slides page or Miro board.

No one loses points for which tool they use. Students without a computer or reliable internet can borrow a laptop or hotspot through GCC’s Office of Information Technology (OIT) Student Technology Request.

Discord rules

  • Questions go in the course channel, not direct messages, so everyone benefits from the answer.
  • Set your server nickname to the name you want to be called. Screen names are allowed.
  • Add a profile photo or avatar.
  • Add alt text to every image you post, and a transcript or captions to every audio or video post.

Accessibility

Add alt text to every screenshot in your document. Caption or transcribe your test clip. If you use a screen reader and a step does not work with it, tell the instructor and an equivalent is set up.

Submission

Submit the link to your Google Doc, Slides page or Miro board (shared with Maricopa).
