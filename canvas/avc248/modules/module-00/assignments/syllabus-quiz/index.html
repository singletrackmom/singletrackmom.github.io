Syllabus and Course Overview Quiz

Overview

This quiz confirms that you have read the syllabus, Getting Started and AI in This Course. It does not measure a module objective. You may retake it until the deadline.

5 points, Exercises. About 1 hours.

Instructions

  1. Read the syllabus (open Syllabus+, the syllabus link in the Canvas course menu), Getting Started and AI in This Course.
  2. Take the quiz in Canvas. You may retake it until the due date. It covers:
    1. The deadline rhythm, the late window and the hard close.
    2. The Discord critique rule and how to get help.
    3. When using AI is not cheating: it is assigned, logged, and you are graded on your own thinking.
    4. Which account to use for Gemini and why: your Maricopa account, because people do not review what you enter and it is not used to train AI.
    5. What AI does here: research, writing, feedback and planning. It never designs.

Accessibility

The quiz has no time limit. Students with an approved accommodation receive it automatically in Canvas.

Submission

Submit the quiz in Canvas.
