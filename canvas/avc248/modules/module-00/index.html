Module 0: Getting Started

Module 0: Getting Started

Week 1

Objectives

Module 0 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 0.1 Explain why employers expect AI skills, and the real costs of AI. (Course Competency 1)
  • 0.2 Explain in plain terms how a large language model produces an answer, and why it can be wrong. (Course Competency 2)
  • 0.3 Write, test and revise a prompt, and verify the result against a source. (Course Competency 2)
  • 0.4 State a personal position on using AI in creative work, backed by two sources. (Course Competency 1)
  • 0.5 Set up and verify every account and piece of software the course uses. (Course Competency 4)
  • 0.6 Record and share a short explanation of your work with the recorder that fits your field. (Course Competency 8)
  • 0.7 Introduce yourself and your work to the class on Discord and comment on four classmates’ introductions. (Course Competency 4)

Overview

Week 1 starts with AI, before any software. Read AI in This Course, then complete the AI Primer. Then set up your accounts and recorder, and introduce yourself on Discord.

About 9.5 hours in all.

AI in This Course

Read the full AI in This Course page before the AI Primer. It explains how AI is used here and how your work is protected.

Watch

  • Getting Started in the Course: how the course runs, the deadline rhythm and the AI rules (posted in Canvas)
  • How AI works: it predicts the next word, it does not know facts, it makes things up, and the account you use matters (instructor video, posted in Canvas)
  • How to use it well: the prompt formula (role, task, context, format), then verify, then revise (instructor video, posted in Canvas)
  • The course rules: Gemini writes and gives feedback, it never designs; the AI-use log; check and label anything you keep (instructor video, posted in Canvas)

Read

Practice

  • Write a prompt for something in your field, then revise it twice using role, task, context and format.
  • Fact-check three claims in Gemini’s answer against sources you open yourself.
  • Record a 10-second test clip before you start the Technology Setup assignment.
