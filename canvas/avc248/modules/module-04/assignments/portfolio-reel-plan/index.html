Module 3, Midterm Portfolio

Portfolio and Reel Plan

This assignment measures Module Objective 3.2, which supports Course Competency 8.

Objectives

  • 3.2 Plan a week-by-week production schedule that addresses the priority feedback on your portfolio. (Course Competency 8)

Overview

Your midterm critique is in. Turn it into a concrete plan for the final portfolio: which pieces it includes, what each needs, and how you will use the second half of the term.

10 points, Process Work. 1.5 hours.

Instructions

  1. Review the critique on your midterm presentation and list the three changes that matter most.
  2. Confirm where the final portfolio will live (see Portfolio Platforms).
  3. List each project for the final portfolio with what it needs and a realistic deadline.
  4. Build a week-by-week schedule through the final presentation, with a project and task for each week.
  5. Gemini may turn your list into a draft timeline. Check every step against the course schedule and add an AI-use log row.

Accessibility

Make the document readable by a screen reader: use real heading styles (Heading 1 and Heading 2 in Google Docs or Word, paragraph styles in InDesign), keep text contrast high, add alt text to every image or logo, and export a tagged PDF (InDesign: Create Tagged PDF; Word or Google Docs: keep document structure tags on when you save as PDF).

Submission

Submit the plan as text or a PDF with your AI-use log row.
