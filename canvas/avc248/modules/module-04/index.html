Module 3: Midterm Portfolio

Module 3: Midterm Portfolio

Week 8

Objectives

Module 3 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 3.1 Present four portfolio projects in a two to three minute recording, stating the goal, your role, one key decision and the outcome of each. (Course Competency 8)
  • 3.2 Plan a week-by-week production schedule that addresses the priority feedback on your portfolio. (Course Competency 8)
  • 3.3 Summarize one current development in your creative field and explain how it affects your career plan. (Course Competency 1)

Overview

This week you present your portfolio so far on camera and get critique from classmates and your instructor. You have the second half of the term to act on it. You also plan the rest of the term and look at news in your industry.

7.25 hours in all.

Watch

  • Instructor midterm walkthrough, posted in Canvas: what a strong portfolio presentation includes

Read

  • Portfolio Platforms, the course guide to your portfolio site in Google Sites, and to Behance, Adobe Portfolio and ArtStation if you already use one

Practice

  • Do one timed run-through of your presentation before you record.
