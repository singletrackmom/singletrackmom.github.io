Module 6: Networking and LinkedIn

Module 6: Networking and LinkedIn

Weeks 11 to 12

Objectives

Module 6 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 6.1 Use LinkedIn (or a Google Sites profile page) to connect with five professionals and write three specific comments in your field. (Course Competency 4)
  • 6.2 Draft a mentor outreach message that names a specific person or role, where you found them and a small request. (Course Competency 4)
  • 6.3 Give three classmates portfolio critique that names a specific project, one strength and one concrete change. (Course Competency 8)
  • 6.4 Walk an interviewer through one project and ask two researched questions about the organization, on camera. (Course Competency 7)
  • 6.5 Evaluate a working professional’s portfolio the way a hiring manager would, naming what works and one change. (Course Competency 8)
  • 6.6 Produce a complete LinkedIn profile (or Google Sites profile page) with a headline, About section, experience, skills and portfolio links. (Course Competency 2)

Overview

Most creative jobs are filled through people as much as postings. This module builds your professional profile, puts it to work with real connections and a mentor search, checks your portfolio with peer critique, and finishes the interview practice series with the question every portfolio interview asks: walk me through a project. LinkedIn requires members to be 16 or older; students under 16 complete the same work on a Google Sites profile page.

About 14.5 hours in all.
