Module 6, Networking and LinkedIn

Updated LinkedIn Profile

This assignment measures Module Objective 6.6, which supports Course Competency 2.

Objectives

  • 6.6 Produce a complete LinkedIn profile (or Google Sites profile page) with a headline, About section, experience, skills and portfolio links. (Course Competency 2)

Overview

With your resume finished, your profile should carry the same information and more: media, recommendations and connections beyond a one-page resume.

5 points, Exercises. About 1.5 hours.

Instructions

  1. Transfer your final resume content to the profile. Leave off your phone number and address.
  2. Update your headline and About section to match the roles you are applying for.
  3. Add your skills and ask classmates and your instructor to endorse your top three.
  4. Attach portfolio images or video to the relevant experience or education entries.
  5. Update Open to Work preferences and your professional groups.
  6. Students under 16. LinkedIn’s User Agreement does not allow members under 16. If you are under 16, build the same content as a Professional Profile page on your Google Sites portfolio (headline, About section, experience, education, skills and portfolio links), and complete any networking step through the course Discord and the public pages of the organizations you follow. You are graded on the same criteria.

Example

LinkedIn surfaces more complete profiles in recruiter searches. Fill in every section.

Accessibility

Give every media item a title and a description of what it shows, and caption any video before you attach it.

Submission

Upload a screenshot of the updated profile and paste its link.

Rubric (5 points)

CriterionPoints

Media attached with titles and descriptions; endorsements requested (Objective 6.6)2
