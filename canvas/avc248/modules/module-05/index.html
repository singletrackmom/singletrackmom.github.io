Module 5: Freelance Business

Module 5: Freelance Business

Weeks 9 to 10

Objectives

Module 5 objectives. By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 5.1 Identify three financial, legal or professional risks of freelance work and one way to reduce each. (Course Competency 6)
  • 5.2 Identify the questions, clauses and terms (scope, schedule, payment, revisions, cancellation, ownership) a client agreement must cover. (Course Competency 6)
  • 5.3 Evaluate a three-month post-graduation plan against your Personal Goals Statement and revise the items that do not fit. (Course Competency 1)
  • 5.4 Explain, in a 60 to 90 second recorded answer, why you want to work for one organization, citing three facts from your research. (Course Competency 7)
  • 5.5 Locate one stretch posting in your field, and identify one requirement you meet, one you do not, and a plan to close the gap. (Course Competency 5)

Overview

Many graduates in creative fields freelance by choice, alongside a staff job, or while they look for one. This module covers the business side: the risks, the questions you ask a client, and the written agreement that protects both of you. It also finishes your resume and continues the interview practice series with recorded company research.

About 16.75 hours in all.
