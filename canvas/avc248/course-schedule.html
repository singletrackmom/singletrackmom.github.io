Course Schedule, Spring 2027

Everything is due at noon, Arizona time, on Thursday and Sunday. Projects and project steps (marked “Discord the day before”) go up on Discord for critique by noon the day before, and the Canvas submission includes three critique comments. Exercises, discussions, quizzes and reflections are due at noon with no separate Discord step. Late work is accepted for 24 hours at 10% off; nothing is accepted after noon the day after the due date. The interview practice series runs from Week 3 to Week 12 and builds to the mock interviews in Week 13. About 120 hours over 15 weeks.
