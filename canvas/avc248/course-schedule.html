Spring 2027. Everything is due at noon, Arizona time, on Thursday and Sunday. Projects and project steps (marked “Discord by”) go up on Discord for critique by noon the day before, and the Canvas submission includes three critique comments. Exercises, discussions, quizzes and reflections are due at noon with no Discord step. Late work is accepted for 24 hours at 10% off; nothing is accepted after noon the day after the due date. Interview practice runs all semester, from written responses to recorded OBS answers to a full mock interview in Week 13. About 120 hours over 15 weeks.
