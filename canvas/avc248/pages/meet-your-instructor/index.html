Meet Your Instructor

Meet Your Instructor

An audio introduction is posted in Canvas before the term begins. The transcript below is the full text.

Transcript

Welcome to this course in the Digital Media Arts Department at Glendale Community College. My name is Michelle Blomberg and I am your instructor. You can call me Michelle or Ms. Blomberg; my last name has one o, not Bloomberg. I have worked as a graphic designer, web designer, marketing manager and instructional technologist, and I am now full-time faculty here at GCC. I am originally from Michigan and have close ties to Colorado, where I spend much of my time. My husband is a professional photographer, and we have four children, all in college. I love camping, snowboarding and mountain biking. My bachelor’s degree is in visual communications, and I returned to school later for a master’s degree in education; as a single parent, I could do that because of online learning. I believe in online learning for adults, and I hope this course helps you build your network, make new connections and leave ready for the next step. Please contact me if anything in your life affects your studies. I am here to help you succeed in this course and in your program.

Your Instructor

Message your instructor through Canvas Inbox or ask in the course Discord channel (#avc248-design-self-promotion). Messages receive a reply within one business day. Questions about course content are best asked in Discord, where classmates benefit from the answer; questions about grades or personal matters go to Canvas Inbox.

Assignments are graded with written feedback within one week of the deadline, and resume drafts within five days. Optional Zoom check-ins are announced in Canvas Announcements and Discord; see Zoom. Read Meet Your Instructor for an introduction.
