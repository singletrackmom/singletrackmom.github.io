Career Counselor

Student Success Tools · Case study

OverviewLive toolPRD

Built first, and it stays for the whole class. It coaches the student toward the interview, then turns their gaps into a learning plan, a network, and a path to a mentor.

Goal

Most career advice assumes the student already knows what they want. Students who do not know their next move stall, and a list of forty tasks makes it worse. The goal is one move the student can finish this week.

Audience

Capstone students, whether they are applying for jobs, going freelance, or still deciding.

Process

It asks before it advises. After every step the student pastes in an update: where each job stands, what each panel asked for, and the gap list so far. It helps them choose which openings to go after, work through a panel’s changes without writing for them, and practice what the interview flagged. At the end it returns three plans: professional development, networking, and finding a mentor, who is a real person and not an AI. Every reply ends with one thing to do this week.

Status

Working prototype.
