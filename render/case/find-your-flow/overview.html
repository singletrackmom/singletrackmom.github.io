Student Success Tools · Case study

OverviewCase studyPRD

One realistic career a day, each paired with a live opening and verified pay.

Goal

Choosing a career as one giant list is overwhelming. The goal is one honest option at a time, small enough to look at.

Audience

One young person exploring careers.

Process

Each morning the agent picks a career, verifies growth and median pay against the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, finds a real local training path, confirms one live job on an employer’s own careers page, and composes a short letter in a locked, phone-first template.

Status

Retired, having succeeded. Twenty-seven letters went out, the reader chose a direction and enrolled, and the agent was switched off.
