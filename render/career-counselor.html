The guide. One agent that knows the whole picture and tells you the single next thing to do.

The student in every example on this page is Alex Rivera, a fictional Digital Media Arts student in motion design and video. They are applying to a Junior Motion Designer role at a Phoenix studio. Render stores no student names, emails, or addresses.

What this one is

The career counselor is the guide. It is the only agent that knows the whole picture, and it is the one you talk to when you are not sure what to do next.

Most career advice assumes you already know what you want. This one starts by asking. It meets you where you actually are, whether you are applying for jobs, going freelance, or still deciding, and it keeps the semester moving instead of handing you a list and walking away.

How it works, step by step

Asks where you are→ Reads your goals and resume→ Names the real gap→ Gives you one move→ Checks back next week
  1. Opens by asking what you are actually trying to do right now, and takes your answer at face value.
  2. Reads your goals, your resume, and the jobs you saved, then says back what it thinks you are aiming at, so you can correct it.
  3. Names the one thing standing between you and that job. Not five things. One, with a reason.
  4. Gives you a move small enough to finish this week, and tells you what it will change.
  5. Helps you build your own job-search agent, so the searching stops being something you do by hand.
  6. Helps you find a real human mentor, because an agent is not a substitute for a person who knows the field.

What Alex sees

Career counselorWhere are you right now: applying, freelancing, or still figuring it out?
AlexApplying, but nothing is coming back and I do not know why.
Career counselorYou have saved nine jobs. Six are motion design, two are video editing, one is social media. That is close enough to focused, so the problem is probably not your aim. Here is what I think it is: your reel opens with a class project, and every one of those nine postings asks for professional or client work. You are being read as a student before anyone gets to the good part.
Career counselorThis week: move the kinetic type piece to the front of your reel and cut the two weakest class pieces. That is it. Nothing else changes until that is done.
Why this moveIt costs you one afternoon and it changes the first four seconds, which is all most people watch. Once it is done, run the hiring committee again and see if the reel score moves.

The counselor never gives you a five-item list. One move, finished, beats five started.

You keep this after the course

It leaves as a file, not as a login

At the end of the semester Render writes this agent out as a plain prompt file in your download, with a readme telling you what to paste where. You run it in your own Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT. Render does not have to still exist for the agent to keep working, and nobody can take it back from you.
