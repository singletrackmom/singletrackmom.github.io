The four agents a student builds in Render, and the scheduled agents built on the same pattern.

Agents students build inside Render

AI MentorIn pilot. Built first, and it stays for the whole class. It coaches the student toward the interview, turns their gaps into a learning plan and a network, and keeps pushing them to find a human mentor. Job Search AgentIn pilot. A search agent the student builds from their own goals. It confirms every job is still open, and it notices what the postings keep asking for. Will I Get an Interview?In pilot. Every job gets its own panel of four. They read the resume the student aligned by hand, the cover letter they wrote, and their portfolio, then say what to change in each. Interview PanelIn pilot. The four people who read the application for that job now interview the student for it, coach every answer, and say what to fix in the portfolio first.

Job search agents on a schedule

Digital Media Jobs FeedBuilt and running. Posts verified entry-level openings to the program Discord every day. Find Your FlowRetired, having succeeded. One realistic career a day, each paired with a live opening and verified pay. FocusRuns daily. Finds fresh photo-gig leads and a marketing idea for a working photographer, published to a private board. SoarRuns weekly. Rebuilds an aerospace dashboard for a college-bound engineer: news, launches, clubs, and a live internship watch. Summer WorkRuns on a schedule. Finds high-paying, flexible summer gig work, verified live on employers’ own sites.

Professional development model

CultivateRefreshes on every visit. A personal hub for professional development and a curated news feed. The first AI build, the experiment that started all of this.
