San Rafael Swell → Great Basin National Park
Baker, NV — on US-50 "Loneliest Road in America"
~4.5 hrs · US-6 / US-50 westUpper Lehman Creek CG, NPS · Site 5

Head south to US-6 and pick up US-50 west through central Nevada — the "Loneliest Road in America." Wide empty skies, distant mountain ranges, almost no traffic. Great Basin National Park is a genuinely underrated gem right on the Nevada-Utah border. Upper Lehman Creek sits at 7,500 ft on Wheeler Peak Scenic Drive — three miles up from the visitor center, closer to the alpine action.

Eastern Sierra — pre-Yosemite staging
~4 hrs · CA-89 south → US-395 southFirst-come or ~$25Lundy Canyon CG or June Lake Loop

South on CA-89 toward Markleeville, then pick up US-395 south through the Eastern Sierra. This is one of the best mountain drives in the West — the Sierra escarpment on your right, high desert to your left. Stop at Mono Lake South Tufa on the way in for the alien limestone tower formations. Camp at Lundy Canyon (creek, aspens, trout) or the June Lake Loop (surrounded by small mountain lakes).

Yosemite — The Waterfall Hike
Mist Trail to Vernal Fall · classic Yosemite
No drivingShuttle to Happy Isles — Stop 16

The one everyone comes for. Take the shuttle from the valley to Happy Isles and hit the Mist Trail. The climb to the top of Vernal Fall involves steep granite steps cut directly into the cliff face and you'll get completely soaked from the spray — in the best possible way. Ponchos or rain gear are not optional in early June. The view from the top of the fall is staggering.

★ Today's Hike
Mist Trail to Top of Vernal Fall
2.4 miles RT · moderate · 1,000 ft gain · steep granite steps + heavy mist spray · bring ponchos · trailhead at Happy Isles (Shuttle Stop 16) · allow 3–4 hours

Afternoon: recover, swim in the Merced River below the valley, wander Yosemite Village. El Capitan Meadow at dusk is worth the drive back — the wall turns golden.

Yosemite Mountaineering School & Guide Service
Full day activityCrane Flat (last night)

All day in Yosemite Valley on the granite walls with a private guide — just the two of you. YMS is the only authorized guide service in the park, with certified guides who've climbed these walls for decades. Private guided climbs are personalized to your ability level; the guide selects routes and teaches technique throughout.

📞 Book This Now — Peak Season Books Weeks Out
Private Guided Climb for 2 — Yosemite Mountaineering School

Meets 8:30 AM at Curry Village (Half Dome Village). Full day, ~7 hours. Helmets provided free. Guide brings all gear. Good for any experience level — guide tailors the day to you.

Beginner areas in the valley include Swan Slab and Five Open Books — protected granite faces with real climbing, real exposure, and real views of the valley from height.

⚠️ Tioga Pass — Check Before Leaving

CA-120 through Tioga Pass must be open June 1 (eastbound approach) and June 4 (eastbound exit). Check nps.gov/yose road conditions. Normal years it opens late May. If still closed on June 1, re-route via CA-140 Big Oak Flat entrance — adds time but you still make Crane Flat.

🏔 Tahoe Camping — Short Notice Reality

California state parks (D.L. Bliss, Sugar Pine Point, Emerald Bay) open reservations 6 months out and June is packed. Best bets right now: Nevada Beach USFS on recreation.gov, and Fallen Leaf Lake USFS. Check ReserveCalifornia daily for D.L. Bliss cancellations — they do happen.

🎫 America the Beautiful Pass

At $80 the annual interagency pass covers entry to Great Basin, Yosemite, and any other NPS sites on the trip. Buy online at store.usgs.gov/pass or at any national park entrance. Pays for itself in 2 parks.
