What this is

A reference document I built for myself during the transition planning phase, mapping what an EdTech Product Manager actually does day to day. Compiled from interviews, job descriptions, blog posts from working PMs at Instructure and Udemy, and Scrum/PM literature, then translated into the specific vocabulary, tool stack, and workflows used in current EdTech roles.

It exists because PM transition guides tend to be written for software generalists. None of them addressed the specific question I needed answered: what is the lived reality of being a Product Manager at a learning platform company, and which of my existing skills already map to that work? So I built the version that did.

What's inside

  1. What an EdTech PM actually does (the three types)
  2. A real Monday, hour by hour
  3. The tool stack: Jira, Figma, Confluence, Slack, Loom, Amplitude, Miro
  4. UX vocabulary for the current job market (JTBD, Discovery vs. Delivery, etc.)
  5. Scrum ceremonies as they actually happen on a 2-week sprint
  6. Two case studies: how Udemy and Instructure PM teams think differently
  7. Interview question patterns and how to answer them with my background

Why it lives here

Cultivate is my career system, not just a PD tracker

The original purpose of Cultivate was a faculty-facing personal learning environment. As the EdTech transition path got more serious, the tool grew to hold the artifacts that go with it: the research doc, the resume vault, the day-in-the-life guide. The connectivist principle behind Cultivate is that learning lives in the connections between resources, not in any one document. This is one of those resources.

How to use it

If you're a colleague considering the same transition: the most useful section is probably section 2, the hour-by-hour Monday. Everything else is unfamiliar vocabulary until you can picture the rhythm of the work. Once the day shape is clear, the tools become much easier to learn in context.

If you're an EdTech recruiter: section 6 (Udemy vs. Instructure) is where I work through how my GCC LMS-migration leadership and ProQuest PM background map to your specific team's day-to-day. That mapping is also the basis for how I tailor cover letters.
