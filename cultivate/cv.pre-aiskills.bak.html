Curriculum Vitae

Research & Professional Interests
Work focused on closing the gap between learning and real-world practice: in curriculum, in tools, and in the systems that support faculty and students. Spans instructional design, UX, graphic design, career readiness, and AI integration. Those threads show up together whether in course design, tool development, or helping faculty think through how technology and AI fit into their teaching. Graduate research in connectivism and personal learning environments, a framework since applied to building AI-powered tools that let faculty and students construct and tend their own learning infrastructure. Particularly interested in how AI changes what is possible in learning design, not just as a content tool but as a thinking partner, and in prompt engineering as a competency that should mirror industry demand. All current courses taught are online, continuously refined over fifteen-plus years, and all tools are built for learners who are navigating education at a distance. A current research thread asks what still counts as evidence of learning once an AI model can produce the artifact, and which authentic-assessment formats, built around process evidence, the record of making, the response to critique, the decision a student can defend, still hold up.

Render, AI Student Career Launch Framework

Glendale Community College · AVC 248 Integration · singletrackmom.github.io/render · 2025 to Present

A connectivist personal learning environment for students developed in conjunction with Career Services. Gives final-semester students one place to use compartmentalized AI prompting to create their own projects and learning goals, track job search or freelance work, build their portfolio pipeline, and maintain career infrastructure after graduation. Seven modules map to a formal career-launch competency taxonomy. Owned end to end from discovery and user research through learning-experience and journey design, requirements, build, and usability testing. Students choose one real job on day one and build the environment against it across the term; at the capstone Render runs a skills-gap analysis between what a student actually built and what that job requires, then exports a portable career agent and a personal learning plan the student keeps and runs in any AI tool after graduation. It is a single self-contained HTML file with vanilla JavaScript, fifteen AI functions on the Claude API, Google Sheets and Apps Script for an anonymous Career Services data flow, and no student PII, first name only, stored on the student’s own device. Usability tested with students in March 2026 and refined from what it surfaced. Prototyping in HTML/CSS/Vanilla JS using Claude AI; student pilot planned Fall 2026. Overview →

CopaMigo, Bilingual Student Support Routing Prototype

Glendale Community College · singletrackmom.github.io/copamigo · 2025 to Present

Early-prototype AI triage tool designed to close the gap between resources that exist and students who don’t know what to call what they need. Students describe their situation in plain English or Spanish; the tool routes them to the right human service with a handoff card showing contact info, hours, and what to ask for. Built around 14 service modules and 100+ hand-verified service URLs, with email human-handoff, low-level tutoring delivered inline before escalation, and a planned live chat staffed by social work interns. Designed for district-wide deployment with a campus picker for all 10 Maricopa colleges. In testing.

District Student Support & Success AI Strategy

Maricopa Community Colleges District · AI Resource Center, Domain 5

Co-chair of Domain 5 (Student Support & Success) on the district committee chartered to identify where AI can responsibly improve the non-classroom services that drive student retention and well-being, across all 10 colleges and 140,000+ students. The work runs as three coordinated initiatives, grounded in the evidence on why students leave: a service inventory and cross-college crosswalk that maps 50+ student-facing functions across all 10 colleges (the same service is named differently at each); a student-journey usability study with three research personas, a full task roadmap, and a live capture instrument; and a prioritized, human-in-the-loop AI roadmap that answers simple questions itself and routes the rest to a person. Each barrier is scored on a Nielsen severity rating and confirmed by a second rater; the instrument set includes a 46-task taxonomy across all ten colleges, and a method pilot of twelve walkthroughs surfaced the first catastrophic finding, with fieldwork this fall. Framed as organizational change work that surfaces duplicated effort and unmet need and frees staff time for direct student support.

AVC100 Introduction to Digital Media, Course Redesign

Glendale Community College · Launching May 2026 · 2025 to 2026

Contracted redesign of the department’s highest-enrollment course, a 1-credit Digital Media Arts entry point with a documented low student success rate. Redesign grounds every module in retention research; each module directly introduces Student Services including Advising, GCC Cares, Tutoring, and Disability Services, embedding wellness into the learning experience. Built as IMSCC for Canvas import. Success is defined as learning, confidence, belonging, and connection to services rather than completion alone. In an end-of-course survey of the students who completed it, apprehension toward opening the software fell from a first-week average of 3.3 to 2.0, and “I know where to go for help at GCC” rose from 3.4 to 4.8; end-of-term averages included 4.7 for making something real in each program, 4.6 for being able to do it again unaided, 4.6 for wanting another course that uses the software, and 4.9 for believing someone here would help them. These are one strand of evidence, read alongside student reflection and the persistence research the design rests on.

Dial Your Course, Canvas Course-Quality Auditor

Glendale Community College · singletrackmom.github.io/course-dialer · 2026 to Present

Prompted by a regional accreditor’s seat-time finding for online courses, where the course package is the only evidence the promised student hours were delivered, it is a drop-in tool that reads a Canvas .imscc course export and runs nineteen checks against it: seat time and workload, measurable objectives and their Bloom’s Taxonomy level, outcome and objective alignment, alignment to institutional learning outcomes, accessibility, and how AI-resistant the assessments are. It can also enforce a course style guide so pages and formatting stay consistent. It reports only what it can compute, names the standard and the location for every finding, and writes approved fixes back into a package ready to reimport. Nothing is transmitted and no account or server is used, since a Canvas export carries no student data. Automates the countable part of the Quality Matters and SUNY OSCQR reviews Michelle performs by hand as a lead reviewer. In testing across Digital Media Arts; product requirements written for a model-assisted second version. Overview →

Syllabus Checker, Automated Compliance Review

Glendale Community College · singletrackmom.github.io/syllabus-checker · 2026

AI Pilot Governance Framework, Prototype to Production

Maricopa Community Colleges District · AI Resource Center · 2026

Developed the framework the district AI Resource Center can use to take a faculty-built AI prototype off a personal server and run it as a safe, district-hosted pilot with real faculty, staff, and students, then move a successful pilot into production.
