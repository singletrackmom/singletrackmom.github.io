Student Evaluation Summary

Faculty Evaluation Plan · Michelle Blomberg · Spring 2026

What this summary shows. The FEP requires student or service-recipient evaluation from at least one class. I have gone beyond that, drawing from two sources. The first is the official department course evaluation, sent to students by email. The second is my own in-course evaluation, which I run because response rates on the emailed survey are low. To capture honest, representative feedback, I offer extra credit in every class for completing an evaluation, students earn it by completing either my evaluation or the department’s emailed one, their choice. This page summarizes the results, highlights representative student comments, and the full original evaluation reports are attached separately.
A note on the classes shown
A little critical feedback is normal and expected. The classes summarized here are representative of my teaching across the cycle. Full reports for every section are available; this summary highlights the courses with complete, representative results.

Category 2, My Own Course Evaluations

These are my own anonymous in-course evaluations, run through a Google Form, which I use because few students complete the emailed department survey. I offer extra credit in every class for completing an evaluation, either mine or the department’s. Spring 2026. Older responses from a prior cycle were excluded, so only the last three years count.
