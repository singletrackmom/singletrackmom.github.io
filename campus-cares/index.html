The brief the team works from: the client, the goal, and the roadmap of deliverables for the GCC Cares brand program.

The Client

GCC Cares is the college’s basic needs department: a social worker director and a team of social work interns who help students with emergency funds, housing, food, and benefits enrollment. The department is moving its services into the Basic Needs Support Hub, a former bookstore building that opens in phases.

Goal

A brand system GCC Cares can run without a designer: identity, templates, and a brand manual that staff apply on their own. The goal and the success measures are on the Overview.

Constraints

  • No designer on staff. Everything has to be usable by social work staff and student workers after the design team moves on.
  • A phased building. Signage and graphics have to work in a space that is still being built out, and change as each zone opens.
  • Fire code. Nothing hangs from the ceiling, so large graphics must stand on their own.
  • Client sign-off. The team writes the content, and GCC Cares approves every statistic and message before it is designed.

Design Voice

Warm, hand-crafted, and community-centered: a neighborhood market, not a charity office. Hand-drawn icons, a grounded and calming palette, and welcoming first-person language (“Come on in,” “Help yourself”) so students feel like regulars rather than recipients.
