Open House

Experiential Learning · Case study

OverviewDesign briefOpen House

The first public test of the GCC Cares identity: a brochure, wayfinding signs and four display towers for the opening of the Basic Needs Hub.

What the Team Is Making

PieceWhat it does
BrochureA four-page piece built to outlast the event, with a separate open house insert. It makes the case in order: the need, the impact, thanks to donors and partners, and how to help.
Wayfinding signsDirectional signs that bring visitors across campus to the Hub.
Four display towersSix feet tall, one for each area of the Hub: food, the GCC Cares team, community partners and the Kids Corner. Each names the space, shows what will happen there, and carries eight infographic panels on the need and on GCC Cares’ impact. Two more towers follow as the Hub grows.

Research

The team gathered everything GCC Cares had: the department’s web pages, interviews with staff, and its case for support, remodel plans and flyers. Every statistic was traced back to its source, checked against GCC’s own documents and national research from The Hope Center and the U.S. Government Accountability Office, and corrected where the math or the source did not hold. The result is one source of truth that feeds both the brochure and the towers, so every number a donor reads is one the department can defend.
