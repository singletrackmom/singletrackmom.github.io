The role, in one breath

  • You set program-experience strategy, scout emerging tech (especially AI), turn it into pilots that scale analyze data, and align academic, product, and technology teams.
  • Hybrid Salt Lake City. Pay band ~$97K to $146K. Team: Program Experience Futures.
  • WGU is competency-based: students progress by proving competency, not seat time; flat-rate term tuition; centralized curriculum built once and delivered at scale.
  • Mission to anchor every answer on: expand access for working adults and help them graduate into better futures.

WGU now (drop one or two naturally)

  • WGU Labs is the research arm, focused on responsible, efficacy-tested AI. They run real studies, not hype, and have an AI Playground of tools.
  • WGU launched the nation’s first at-scale, competency-based B.S. in AI Engineering (2026).
  • WGU partnered with Aera to bring AI to student support services which is exactly your Student Support and Success domain and CopaMigo lane.
  • Mentors vs Evaluators: Program Mentors stay with a student to graduation; Evaluators score assessments against rubrics. Knowing this signals you get the model.

Your 6 stories (one for each thing they screen for)

Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep results honest.

1. Scalable, learning-science-grounded design: the AVC 100 redesign, grounded in retention research, built as a reusable template adjuncts can run.
2. Product design thinking, student-centered: Render, built from a real classroom observation, no-PII, students keep it after graduation.
3. Translating AI into practical innovation: the AI-literacy unit and judgment teaching in the capstone, plus the Render and CopaMigo prototypes.
4. Collaboration across a matrixed org: the 10-college ARC domain, plus ProQuest product management working from requirements engineers built.
5. Strategy to pilots to scale: the 10-college UX study turning into a prioritized, human-in-the-loop AI roadmap, with Fall 2026 pilots.
6. Raising the bar across centralized curriculum: adopting district-shared courses, building stackable microcredentials, founding the Faculty AI Community of Practice.

Brush up (beyond Scrum)

CBE model

Competency over seat time, Program Mentors vs Evaluators, subscription tuition, centralized curriculum. You are eager to design within CBE not claiming CBE experience.

Learning-science and design vocabulary

Backward design, evidence-centered design, formative vs summative, Universal Design for Learning, accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA, ADA Title II, you just did this work), design thinking, rapid prototyping, A/B testing, learning analytics, efficacy research.

AI in education

Responsible and ethical AI, bias, hallucination, human in the loop data privacy and FERPA, your no-PII / data-sovereignty theme, and how you would measure whether an AI tool actually improves outcomes.

Have ready

Qualtrics and Tableau (the UX study, outcome metrics); your two-decade arc (11 yrs program director, 7 yrs director of instructional technology, ProQuest PM); a 60-second “why WGU.”

Practice questions

Opening and behavioral

Walk me through your background and why this role.

Tell me about a time you turned a new technology into something people actually used.

Describe a project that stalled or failed. What did you learn?

Tell me about aligning people who did not report to you.

Role and strategy

A promising AI capability lands tomorrow. How do you decide whether and how to bring it into the student experience at scale?

How would you take an idea from concept to pilot to scaled solution here?

How do you prioritize when everything feels urgent?

How do you measure whether a program-experience change worked?

WGU and CBE fit

What attracts you to competency-based education, and where might your experiential teaching translate?

How is designing for self-paced working adults different from a traditional term?

How would you partner with Program Mentors and Evaluators?

AI ethics

Where should there always be a human in the loop?

How do you protect student data and trust while still innovating?

How do you tell real impact from AI hype?

Scrum and project management

How do you run a backlog and decide what ships next?

What does a Product Owner actually own?

Tell me about managing a complex project from initiation to completion.

Curveballs

Pitch me an AI idea for your first 90 days.

A faculty group resists an AI rollout. What do you do?

You said the Scrum cert is in progress, what have you taken from it so far?

Questions for you to ask them (pick 3 or 4)

  • What does success look like for this role in the first 6 to 12 months?
  • How does Program Experience Futures work with WGU Labs and the academic programs day to day?
  • How are AI pilots scoped, governed, and tested for efficacy today?
  • How do you balance moving fast on AI with responsible-AI guardrails at this scale?
  • What is the first program-experience problem you would want this person to take on?
  • How does this team intersect with Program Mentors and Evaluators?

Stay honest (your guardrails)

  • PSPO I is in progress finishing this summer. Never say “certified.”
  • You co-chair the Student Support and Success domain only not the whole committee.
  • The Faculty AI Community of Practice is launching (Fall 2026), not already running.
  • You are eager to design within CBE you are not claiming CBE experience.
  • No invented numbers. Say “improved completion,” not a percentage you cannot back up.
  • Render is built and heading to its first pilot not a finished production tool.
