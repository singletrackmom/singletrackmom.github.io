What this simulation is

The capstone is a single, defended, end-to-end consulting engagement for a recurring client, RiverCity, taking a genuine decision from a one-page brief through scoping, a leakage-safe build, an unannounced distribution-shift event that breaks earlier models, a detection-and-adaptation cycle, and a live stakeholder defense. It integrates every outcome in the course.

This tool is the preparation layer: a readiness checklist that confirms the engagement is complete, then a recorded defense that speaks randomized questions on camera so the student can rehearse and self-assess. The live meeting with the sponsor is still the graded defense. It is built on the reusable Defense practice engine.

What it helps you do

  • Confirm the engagement deliverables are complete before the defense.
  • Rehearse answering unscripted, randomized stakeholder questions on camera.
  • Watch yourself back and self-assess against the defense rubric.
  • Practice conceding real weaknesses and answering “what would change your mind?”
  • Build fluency so the live defense is rehearsal, not improvisation.
Why it resists AI substitution

The graded object is fifteen weeks of decisions on a private, messy, shifting engagement, defended live before people who push back. A model can draft text, but it cannot own the specific choices this student made or answer the sponsor’s follow-ups in real time.
